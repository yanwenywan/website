# 886. Revenge

"Cough, cough!"

Xu Qi’an covered his mouth and coughed hard twice. Only after the women in the hall had all looked over did he slowly step across the threshold.

He looked exactly like a doddery old man.

"What's wrong with you?"

As the principal wife, Lin'an was startled and quickly rose from her chair, hurrying over in small steps.

The other women also cast him anxious and concerned looks, all except the nine-tailed fox.

Xu Qi’an waved a hand and said in a hoarse voice:

"I was injured in the battle with the Buddha. My qi and blood are exhausted, my lifespan has been badly damaged, and I'll need a long time to recover.

"Sigh, who knows whether it'll leave me with lasting problems."

The nine-tailed fox suddenly cut in:

"If his qi and blood have withered, perhaps he won't be able to perform as a man in future."

Lin'an and Mu Nanzhi's expressions changed at once. Yeji half believed it and half didn't.

Auntie grew anxious as soon as she heard it. "That serious? Did you go to the Sitianjian for medicine?"

Dalang was the only male descendant of the eldest branch. He didn't even have children yet. If he could no longer do that, wouldn't the eldest branch lose its lineage?

...Xu Qi’an glanced at the nine-tailed fox and ignored her. "I'll be recuperating at home for a while. It's been ages since I had Auntie's cooking."

Auntie immediately stood up. "I'll go and check the kitchen and make a few dishes you like."

Back then, the Xu household had not been well off. Although they had a cook now, Auntie had often cooked herself in the past. She was not some pampered noble matron by birth.

Xu Qi’an turned to Mu Nanzhi and said:

"Aunt Mu, I remember you've got medicinal herbs growing in the back courtyard. Boil me a bowl of medicinal soup to replenish qi and invigorate the blood."

Mu Nanzhi, who knew she was the reincarnation of the Undying Tree, gave an "mm" and rose expressionlessly, looking like she meant to settle accounts with him later.

Xu Qi’an continued:

"Little sister, the robe you made for your big brother is worn through."

Xu Lingyue smiled gently and said in a soft voice:

"I'll make a few more robes for Big Brother."

All through this, Xu Qi’an kept coughing without pause, making it clear to the women, "I'm feeling terribly unwell, so don't start any trouble."

After that series of manoeuvres, only Lin'an, Yeji, and the nine-tailed fox remained in the hall. Xu Qi’an did not even have a very good excuse, and said:

"Lin'an, go back to your room first. The queen and I need to discuss something."

Lin'an puffed out her cheeks. "What is there that I can't know?"

She was no meek and virtuous wife. She had plenty of fighting spirit.

Xu Qi’an did not force her to leave. Looking at the nine-tailed fox, he said sternly:

"Your Majesty, you need to sail overseas once more and subdue the beyond-mortal descendants of gods and demons. The more the better."

The nine-tailed fox pondered for a moment and said:

"So that once Desolation awakens, it won't be able to subdue the overseas descendants of gods and demons and use them to strike back at Jiuzhou?"

Talking to clever people really was convenient... Xu Qi’an said:

"If they refuse to submit, kill them all. Leave not a single one alive."

The nine-tailed fox thought for a moment and said:

"Even if they submit on the surface, they'll betray us when the time comes. Without common interests or sufficiently deep feelings as support, the descendants of gods and demons simply won't be loyal to me, or to the Great Feng.

"When that time comes, perhaps the moment Desolation arrives, they'll take the initiative to surrender and betray us."

Xu Xinnian shook his head.

"No need to make it so troublesome. Subdue them, then carry out a large-scale migration, and that'll be enough.

"The sea is vast and boundless. Desolation can't possibly spend large amounts of time searching for them and subduing them, because it simply wouldn't be worth it. If the descendants of gods and demons join the war, they'll be a fatal threat to us.

"But to Desolation, Its opponents are the other Beyond-ranks. The descendants of gods and demons can play only a negligible role."

Xu Qi’an added:

"You can tell them that once Desolation awakens, it will devour all descendants of gods and demons at the beyond-mortal level. That's true enough, and it will make those overseas descendants remember once more the fear and humiliation of being dominated by Desolation."

What followed was a discussion of details, including but not limited to taking Sun Xuanji along and setting up teleportation formations along the route, so that the nine-tailed fox could quickly return to Jiuzhou and not lose her way in the boundless sea.

As well as executing any descendants of gods and demons who refused to cooperate on the spot, with absolutely no soft-heartedness.

They would promise that in future the descendants of gods and demons could return to live in Jiuzhou.

They would establish a nation for the descendants of gods and demons, support a powerful beyond-mortal descendant of gods and demons as its leader, and so on.

Lin'an sat with her slim waist straight and her face stern, listening with utmost concentration, though in truth she understood nothing at all. Only after the nine-tailed fox left did she confirm that her husband really had been discussing serious business.

...

"Your Majesty!"

Yeji hurried after the nine-tailed fox, bowed, and said in a low voice:

"Yueji has fallen, while you were out at sea."

The nine-tailed fox gave an "mm". "I advanced to first rank overseas and awakened my spiritual essence. When I encountered Desolation, I had no choice but to sever a tail to survive."

In front of Yeji she was imposing and forceful, entirely lacking the alluring charm she showed in Xu Qi’an's presence. She said coolly:

"Not only her. Among you eight sisters, any one of you may face the risk of falling.

"When the great calamity descends, I won't show mercy to any of you. Do you understand?"

A first-rank Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox had nine lives. Once all nine lives were spent, she too would fall.

Until then, she would not die, and this could not be changed by the nine-tailed fox's own will.

In other words, severing a tail to survive was a passive ability. Every time she died once, she would lose one tail.

"Yeji understands. To die for Your Majesty is our fate." She glanced at her, then cautiously probed, "Your Majesty's feelings towards Xu Lang..."

The silver-haired demoness frowned and snorted.

"This ruler certainly wouldn't like a lecher. What infuriates me is that he keeps pestering me in every possible way, and because he's a half-step Martial God, he dares put his hands on me.

"Mm, this time I came to the Xu residence to stir things up precisely to remind him.

"Otherwise he'll keep setting his sights on me."

Yeji pressed her lips together.

"What if he insists on setting his sights on you, Your Majesty?"

The nine-tailed fox said helplessly:

"Then we'll just have to take it one step at a time. Who told him to be a half-step Martial God?"

*Clearly you're the one setting your sights on him. Aren't you bullying an honest man...* Yeji muttered inwardly. She would have to say a few bad things about Her Majesty in front of Xu Lang later.

Otherwise she might bring all seven sisters, no, six sisters, to compete with her for a man.

Back in the inner hall, Xu Qi’an raised his brows at his little brother and transmitted his voice:

"When the enemy is fierce and united, you must learn to divide them and defeat them one by one. The self-injury ruse is a wonderful thing. A man's self-injury ruse is just like a woman's tricks of crying, causing a scene, and threatening suicide.

"It's effective everywhere."

Xu Xinnian gave a cold laugh.

"You can hide for a while, but not for a lifetime. Every one of the sisters-in-law are suspicious."

"Which is why you need to divide the enemy." Xu Qi’an got up without another word and headed for the study.

Xu Xinnian happened to be on leave that day and had nothing to do, so he followed him over.

Xu Qi’an spread out some paper and instructed:

"Erlang, grind ink for your big brother."

Xu Xinnian snorted, but obediently ground the ink.

Xu Qi’an dipped his brush in the ink and wrote:

"I have drifted overseas for half a month already, and I miss my wife Lin'an deeply. We had only just married when I had to put to sea, leaving her to guard our lonely chamber by herself. The guilt in my heart is unbearable. Day and night, all I see is her voice, her face, her smile..."

Shameless! Xu Xinnian denounced him inwardly, then pointed out expressionlessly:

"Big Brother, you've used that wrongly. 'Her voice, her face, her smile' is used for the deceased. You should write 'her voice and face still vivid before me'."

As soon as he said that, Xu Qi’an smacked him on the scalp.

"Get lost!"

*Does he really think i'm just a crude martial artist?*

"But I know Lin'an is broad-minded and sensible, and that at home she can live harmoniously with Mother and Auntie, so I feel much more at ease. If I do not advance to half-step Martial God on this voyage overseas, the Great Feng is in peril..."

Very quickly, one family letter was finished. He deliberately mentioned at the end that "the mission is arduous", expressing how hard his life at sea had been.

Then came the second, the third, the fourth...

After writing them all, Xu Qi’an used his qi to dry the ink, then picked incense ash from the censer and rubbed it over the writing.

"This can cover up the smell of fresh ink. Otherwise one sniff and it'll be obvious. Learn from this," he instructed his little brother.

*You won't have this many younger sisters-in-law...* Xu Erlang thought to himself, I am utterly devoted to Simu.

He had only just finished grumbling inwardly when he saw his elder brother writing the second family letter:

"Nanzhi, after half a month apart, I miss you deeply..."

Xu Xinnian blurted out:

"So you really are having an affair with Aunt Mu."

"Call me uncle-in-law from now on!" Xu Qi’an immediately took advantage and pushed his luck.

...

When it was time for dinner, Second Uncle Xu returned from duty and pulled his frost-haired nephew and son into a round of toasting and drinking.

Slightly tipsy, he swept a glance over his daughter Xu Lingyue, his wife's sworn elder sister Mu Nanzhi, his niece-in-law Lin'an, and his nephew's concubine Yeji from the Southern Marches, and asked in puzzlement:

"Why do you all look so unhappy?"

Auntie said worriedly:

"Ningyan has suffered a serious injury. In future he may, may... not have children."

*No, no, Mother, they aren't unhappy because of that. They suspect Big Brother's been enjoying himself romantically overseas.* Xu Erlang felt despair at his mother's slowness.

The sisters-in-law might be worried and therefore muddled, but they weren't stupid. By now they had already worked it out.

A first-rank martial artist was already something heaven could not bury and earth could not destroy, never mind that Big Brother was now half-step Martial God.

"Don't talk nonsense. Ningyan is a half-step Martial God. He can't even die, so how could he be injured..." Second Uncle Xu suddenly stopped speaking.

"Exactly, Ningyan is a half-step Martial God now. There won't be anything wrong with his body." Ji Baiqing warmly put food into her legitimate eldest son's bowl and fussed over him.

She did not care in the least how many romantic entanglements her son had outside. She wished she could seize every beauty under heaven and make them all her legitimate eldest son's wives.

Xu Yuanshuang looked at her eldest brother with a face full of admiration and said:

"Big Brother, you must teach Yuanhuai well. Yuanhuai is already fourth rank."

As the second fourth-rank martial artist in the Xu family, Xu Yuanhuai ought to have been full of pride, but now he did not feel the least bit smug.

He simply kept his head down and ate.

After dinner ended, Mu Nanzhi returned to her room with a cold expression.

That night, after washing up, Second Uncle Xu sat cross-legged on the small couch in white inner garments, breathing and cultivating, but no matter what he did he could not settle into the right state.

So he said to Auntie, who was reclining by the bed and reading an illustrated storybook:

"What happened today reminded me of something. Ningyan may very well never have children."

Auntie put down the storybook, straightened up in shock, and cried:

"Why?"

Second Uncle Xu pondered for a moment, then said:

"Ningyan is now half-step Martial God. In essence, he's already different from us. Don't ask how he's different, because I can't explain it. You just need to know he's no longer mortal.

"Don't you find it strange? He's a dual cultivation partner with the National Teacher, and it's nearly been a year, yet the National Teacher still hasn't conceived.

"And it's been a month and a half since he married Princess Lin'an, and she hasn't conceived either."

Auntie looked stricken, her brows tightly furrowed.

"Then what do we do?"

Second Uncle Xu comforted her:

"It's only my guess. I can't be sure... Besides, with Ningyan's current cultivation, he can't even die. Whether he has children or not isn't all that important."

"Rubbish!" Auntie threw the storybook at him.

"If he doesn't have children, wouldn't that mean I raised this brat for nothing?"

...

In the spacious and luxurious bedchamber, Xu Qi’an held Lin'an's soft, delicate body in his arms, his palm caressing her supple snake-like waist. Her body was slick with sweat, her hair stuck to her face, her eyes were dreamy, and her breathing came in tender gasps.

Scattered together with her skirt, dudou, and other garments were letter after letter from home.

Easily deceived Lin'an had been moved on the spot when she saw that the Running Dog had written her so many family letters.

Then, after Xu Qi’an's gentle teasing and caresses, she surrendered completely and flung the nine-tailed fox's words beyond the clouds.

"Ningyan!"

Lin'an wound her lotus-like arms around his neck and said coquettishly:

"I want to go back to the palace tomorrow to see Mother Consort."

Xu Qi’an looked back at her.

"If you want to go, then go. Why ask me?"

Lin'an said quietly:

"Huaiqing won't let me enter the rear palace to see Mother Consort. Apparently Mother Consort has been stirring up the court officials lately and getting them to force Huaiqing to appoint a crown prince. Mother Consort wants Brother Emperor's eldest son to become crown prince."

Although Noble Consort Chen had suffered a crushing defeat, she had not given up, because her daughter had married Xu Qi’an.

Her status as Silver Gong Xu's mother-in-law alone was enough to keep anyone from looking down on her.

Those in court with quick minds, hoping to curry favour where none had yet been offered, had set their sights on Consort Dowager Chen.

*Given your Mother Consort's level, she'd better stop meddling. Huaiqing simply can't be bothered with her. If she found the time, one finger would be enough to crush her...* Xu Qi’an thought this inwardly, but he could not say it aloud.

"Huaiqing is worried that Consort Dowager Chen will stir you up again and send you to make trouble for her, isn't she?"

Lin'an twisted her waist unhappily.

"I wouldn't so easily let Mother Consort use me as a weapon."

*Oh, please...* Xu Qi’an said:

"Lin'an, do you still want revenge on Huaiqing? Do you want to suppress her thoroughly and swagger before her?"

Lin'an's eyes lit up. "You've got a way?"

*Of course. For example, the younger sister turning the tables and becoming the elder sister, making Huaiqing call you elder sister...* Xu Qi’an held it back and changed the subject.

"You don't miss me at all."

"I do," Lin'an said hurriedly.

Xu Qi’an immediately seized her left and right hands and said in a deep voice:

"You haven't even trimmed your nails, and you say you miss me."

Lin'an: "?"

...

"Auntie!"

Baiji knocked on the window, her tiny figure reflected on it.

"That rotten man told me to bring something to you."

Baiji's childish voice came from outside.

Mu Nanzhi, dressed in thin inner garments, opened the window and saw the tiny, exquisite Baiji carrying a little sheepskin bag on her back, bulging full.

Mu Nanzhi snorted, scooped Baiji into her arms, undid the clasp of the sheepskin bag, took out a stack of papers that was neither especially thick nor especially thin, and sat by the table to read.

"Nanzhi, after half a month apart, I miss you deeply..."

At first she curled her lip in disdain, but gradually she became absorbed, the corners of her mouth lifting from time to time. Before she knew it, the candle had nearly burnt away.

Reluctantly, Mu Nanzhi put down the letters, opened the window, and tossed Baiji back out again.

"Go sleep with your Sister Yeji. Don't come looking for me before noon tomorrow."

Baiji gave a soft little cry and scampered off to find Yeji.

After finally managing to knock open Yeji's window, she was thrown back out again.

"Go sleep with Xu Lingyin. Don't come looking for me before noon tomorrow."

"Hmph!"

Baiji hmphed at the window and ran off crossly.

Late at night, Mount Jing City.

The full moon shed a frosty white radiance, dimming the stars in the sky.

Below the altar where the Warlock God's statue stood, robed warlocks gathered in the darkness like an ant colony.

One after another, warlocks in long robes and hoods sat cross-legged below the altar, as though some grand ritual were about to be held.

Li Lingsu's two lovers, the Dongfang sisters, were among them.

Dongfang Wanqing looked around at the silent warlocks nearby and said in a low voice:

"Sister, what's happened?"

Not long ago, Great Warlock Sarun Agu had summoned all warlocks within the borders of the three kingdoms, ordering them to gather at Mount Jing City within two days.

At present, several thousand warlocks had gathered in Mount Jing City, though many low-ranking warlocks still had not managed to arrive.

Dongfang Wanrong's expression was grave.

"Teacher said a great disaster is coming upon the three kingdoms."

Only by gathering in Mount Jing City did all warlocks have a sliver of hope of survival.

Dongfang Wanqing did not understand. "The Warlock God has already partly broken free of the seal. Can't He protect you?"

She used "you" because Dongfang Wanqing was not a warlock, but a martial artist.

At that moment, a warlock beside them said:

"I heard Elder Irbu say yesterday that that man has now come fully into his own. Forget the Great Warlock, even the current Warlock God probably can't suppress him.

"I imagine this so-called great disaster is connected to that man."

The seductive Dongfang Wanrong frowned.

"Who is the 'that man' Elder Irbu was referring to?"
