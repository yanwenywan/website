# 589X. 第七十一章 苗有方

In the bedroom, by the bedside, several candles cast a fiery glow.

Half of Luo Yuheng's face was bathed in gentle orange, while the other half was shrouded in shadow, just like her current image, a mixture of a passionate woman and a fairy.

In Xu Qi’an’s eyes, it possessed an irresistible allure.

Luo Yuheng was both shocked and angry, her emotions laced with panic.

She knew that, at this moment, Xu Qi’an’s presence would be a huge temptation for her.

At the same time, struggling to suppress the karmic fire, she was in no state to throw this brat onto her flying sword and send him thousands of miles away. Not that she couldn’t, but if she did, she would inevitably fail to suppress the fire.

Then, with no one at hand for dual cultivation, she would certainly die.

Luo Yuheng gritted her teeth, saying, “Xu Qi’an, are you trying to force yourself on me?”

What an outrageous thing to say—coming in here and buttering me up, I’ll get myself beaten to death… Xu Qi’an closed the door and walked over to the bed, stopping when he caught Luo Yuheng's tense and wary gaze.

“National Teacher, what I mean is, if we don’t dual cultivate tonight, you’ll certainly have to do so with me tomorrow anyway, or else you won’t be able to resist the karmic fire...”

Luo Yuheng looked at him coldly, not responding.

“Tomorrow, which of the seven emotions will it be?” Xu Qi’an asked.

“There is no pattern to which of the seven emotions will appear.”

Luo Yuheng glanced at him, her eyes involuntarily drifting down from Xu Qi’an’s handsome face, skimming over his chest and abdomen… She quickly forced herself to look away.

Xu Qi’an nodded, sitting down by the bed, adopting a tone of earnest discussion:

“In that case, how do you know the next personality will be willing to dual cultivate with me? What if she refuses, standing firm to the end, what then?”

At this, Luo Yuheng’s long, straight brows knitted gently. She thought for a moment, then replied coldly, “Faced with life and death, I will make the right choice.”

Xu Qi’an suddenly placed his hand on Luo Yuheng’s thigh. “Since that’s the case, why won’t you dual cultivate with me now?”

Luo Yuheng’s delicate body trembled. The two were so close that Xu Qi’an could clearly see goosebumps rising on her neck.

“I’d rather die than dual cultivate with you.”

Her willow brows arched sharply.

“You see! You see!” Xu Qi’an accused.

“How do you know the other personalities won't be just as determined as you to refuse dual cultivation with me?”

“...Get out.” Luo Yuheng, left speechless, could only lose her temper.

Xu Qi’an believed that, under normal circumstances, Luo Yuheng would be willing to dual cultivate with him—partly due to the affection between man and woman, partly because dual cultivation was essential.

But during the eruption of karmic fire, her character changed dramatically, almost as if she had a separate personality. Her behaviour stood in great contrast to usual.

For example, this “anger” personality—fiery, irritable, easily provoked—magnified Luo Yuheng’s slight resistance to the extreme.

No matter what, she absolutely refused to dual cultivate with him.

When Xu Qi’an was in the outer room, he suddenly realised, Luo Yuheng had told him yesterday that in the “seven emotions” state, she would lose control, acting in ways unlike her usual self.

Wasn’t this Luo Yuheng subtly warning him, not to be swayed by the personalities of the seven emotions, but to stick to the plan—seven days, seven dual cultivations, not one day less?

With the National Teacher’s character, she certainly wouldn’t say directly, “No matter what happens, we must stick to dual cultivation.”

“National Teacher, the night is long, time for dual cultivation.”

Xu Qi’an pretended not to hear her rebuke, and started undressing.

He shrugged off his outer robe and tossed it aside; soon his underclothes came off as well, exposing his strong, masculine upper body before Luo Yuheng’s eyes.

Her breathing suddenly quickened a little as she got up in a rage. “If you won’t go, I’ll leave.”

With those words, she got straight out of bed without even putting on her shoes, and stumbled out.

Xu Qi’an grabbed her arm. In the struggle, they both fell onto the bed.

Amid Luo Yuheng’s delicate cries, Xu Qi’an felt something soft and upright on her chest being deeply pressed against him.

“Pa!”

Luo Yuheng slapped him across the face, crisp and ringing.

In the darkness, they remained where they had fallen—him on top, her below—their eyes meeting.

The atmosphere between them turned ever more intimate. Luo Yuheng inhaled the scent of a man, felt his burning breath, her face flushing fiercely, her gaze turning hazy.

She could not resist her own body. She needed dual cultivation to dispel the karmic fire.

To fight her body’s desire, Luo Yuheng bit her lip lightly for a moment of clarity, then tried to slap him again.

But this time she was unsuccessful—her wrist was caught and pressed above her head by Xu Qi’an, then her other hand was also subdued.

Xu Qi’an lowered his head, gently kissing Luo Yuheng’s cheek—her skin soft and fine, her scent enticing.

After nibbling her face several times, he buried his lips in the National Teacher’s neck, licking, sucking, kissing.

Luo Yuheng’s delicate body stiffened, goosebumps rising all over her.

She stared blankly up at the canopy above, her eyes full of confusion, shame, resistance—and a faint trace of fascination.

Even last night, she had not experienced such tender intimacy.

This novel feeling was both shameful and intoxicating. Slowly, she began to follow her heart's desire and ceased to resist.

At this time, she heard Xu Qi’an beside her: “National Teacher, relax. The first time’s always the hardest—tomorrow, I’ll just lie here and let you take the lead.”

Luo Yuheng was furious, reaching out to tear at his mouth.

They wrestled fiercely, the bed shaking with their struggle, nearly breaking into a real fight.

Fortunately, at this time, Luo Yuheng was suffering the pain of karmic fire and unable to use her cultivation. Otherwise Xu Qi’an would have been sent eight hundred miles away with a single Meteor Sword strike.

Xu Qi’an grabbed a neatly folded quilt and pulled it over them. They wrestled on under the covers.

Gradually, Luo Yuheng’s resistance weakened. At the end of the bed, a pair of white, delicate, shapely feet stuck out, then a pair of large feet pressed down.

With her petite feet suddenly tensing, her arches like bows, all of Luo Yuheng’s struggle vanished.

She grasped the bedsheet with both hands, her lips slightly parted, eyes vacant as she stared up at the canopy, lying limp and unresisting.

No one knew how much time passed before that shameless brat, still not satisfied, said brazenly,

“National Teacher, come on, roll over.”

“Xu Qi’an, don’t push your luck...” Luo Yuheng gritted her teeth.

They began wrestling again.

“National Teacher, do you want to know if your knees can touch your shoulders?”

“Xu Qi’an, are you trying to die?”

“Let’s give it a try.”

“……”

………..

The next day, early morning.

By the bedside, skirts, white undergarments, a plain lotus-embroidered dudou, and a belt were strewn messily on the floor...

Xu Qi’an felt something moist and soft brushing repeatedly across his face, making it impossible for him to continue sleeping peacefully.

Half-awake, he opened his eyes to see Luo Yuheng’s breathtakingly beautiful face right before him. Her eyes were full of affection as she gently kissed his cheek, neck, and lips.

A huge question mark flashed across Xu Qi’an’s mind as he spoke uncertainly, “National Teacher?”

Was this the National Teacher he knew?

That cold and lofty National Teacher, as ethereal as an immortal?

Looking back at the image he had of Luo Yuheng, Xu Qi’an simply could not equate the woman before him, trapped in love and desire, with the National Teacher of the Great Feng.

Luo Yuheng pursed her lips and gave a soft laugh. “Did you not enjoy kissing last night? Hmm, it did feel rather wonderful.”

“……”

Xu Qi’an lay there rigidly, not daring to move an inch.

Luo Yuheng’s snow-white arms reached out from under the covers, hooking around his neck as she spoke coquettishly,

“You were passable last night, but it’s not enough—I want more.”

Desire persona? Xu Qi’an was struck by a thought, vaguely guessing at the reason.

Or perhaps something else—among the seven emotions, there was also a ‘Joy’ persona, which was a rather positive emotion… he muttered inwardly.

Faced with such a stunning beauty’s advances, Xu Qi’an of course would not refuse. With a flip, he pinned her beneath him, and soon, the quilt began to rise and fall rhythmically.

“I want more.”

“All right.”

“I want more.”

It really was the “Desire” persona.

“All right.”

“I want more.”

“National Teacher, let’s have some lunch first.”

“With our cultivation, we haven’t needed to eat for a long time.”

“No, I still need to eat. I’m a martial artist.”

“Are you saying you’ve run out of energy?” Luo Yuheng retorted angrily.

“Ha, you clearly don’t know how formidable a martial artist can be.”

………

“I want more.”

“Nat—National Teacher, it’s dusk now…”

“I don’t care, I don’t care, are you done or not?”

“Hmph, you underestimate a martial artist’s stamina.”

………

“National Teacher, it’s already night, let me at least have a bite to eat.”

“One last time.”

“…All right.”

“National Teacher, aren’t you tired?”

“Enough nonsense—you’re not to get out of bed today.”

………

“National Teacher, it’s dawn…”

At a certain moment, he poked his head out from under the covers and saw daylight shining through the window.

In that instant, Xu Qi’an was so overjoyed he nearly wept.

Once it was daylight, the persona would shift—the ‘Desire’ persona would leave, and he could finally crawl out of the wolf’s den.

Since the start of last night’s midnight hour, over two nights and a day, he had truly not left the bed.

Finally, it’s over—today, no one can stop me, even if Jesus comes, it’s useless, I mean it… Xu Qi’an vowed fiercely in his heart.

The lust in Luo Yuheng’s eyes gradually faded, signalling the shift of persona.

She sat up hugging the quilt, glancing at the messy bed with flushed cheeks and a shy gaze.

“National Teacher, I have other matters to attend to. If you’re tired, why not rest a little longer?”

Suppressing the ache in his waist, Xu Qi’an lifted the quilt and got out of bed, preparing to bend down to collect the scattered clothes on the floor.

“Wait.”

Luo Yuheng suddenly grabbed his hand.

Xu Qi’an turned back, his expression freezing, and saw the beautiful National Teacher’s eyes full of fear as she said in a trembling voice,

“I am plagued with karmic fire now. I could burn to death at any moment. Double cultivate with me once more, or I’m scared~”

Xu Qi’an’s heart sank, forcing a stiff smile. “But we’ve already double cultivated for a whole day and two nights. You’ll be fine.”

Luo Yuheng shook her head slightly, biting her lip, looking heartbreakingly pitiful. “But there is still a chance the karmic fire could run out of control. Unless I’m absolutely certain, I feel uneasy.”

My National Teacher really is too cautious… Xu Qi’an’s face twisted slightly.

The curtains gently swayed, never ceasing.

………

By midday, Xu Qi’an arrived in an empty room, summoned the Stupa Tower, and ascended straight to the third floor.

Mu Nanzhi thought this scoundrel had come to coax her. She quickly put on a cold face, pressed her hands together, and took on a posture of becoming a nun.

Unexpectedly, Xu Qi’an didn’t even glance at her but went straight to sit cross-legged before the Tower Spirit, the old monk, and solemnly said,

“Master, I’ve reached enlightenment.”

The Tower Spirit was stunned, then smiled with delight. “What have you realised?”

Xu Qi’an’s face was devoid of joy or sorrow. “Form is emptiness.”

The Tower Spirit looked increasingly amazed, nodding with a smile. “Well spoken!”

Mu Nanzhi stared, eyes wide in disbelief.

Yongzhou city, Liubo Gambling House.

Miao Youfang strolled leisurely into the gambling house with a string of candied haws in his mouth. He had unremarkable looks, dark skin, and piercing eyes that gave him a sharp, shrewd air.

Yet he didn’t have the slick mannerisms of a street rascal. There was an edge to his bearing, and his composure was steady and proper.

After surveying the hall, Miao Youfang made his way to the table where dice were being shaken.

He was here at the gambling house for two reasons: first, to meet the owner, Liu Lang; second, because his silver was running low and he needed to earn some travel funds.

The dice hand called, “Place your bets!”

Gamblers crowded around the table, placing their bets, heated eyes following the dice cup, shouting “big” or “small” in excitement.

Miao Youfang’s ears twitched slightly. He could tell from the sound that someone had tampered with the dice.

All gambling houses were like this. Opening for business, how could they rely on luck alone? There was always some trickery involved, more or less.

But it didn’t matter to him. However much the house cheated, he would never lose.

This was the lesson he had learned from countless experiences.

Roughly a month ago, Miao Youfang had noticed that his luck had suddenly taken a turn for the better.

Wherever he went, good fortune followed. At first, even the daughters of wealthy families in his hometown inexplicably fell for him.

But Miao Youfang was an aspiring young man. Resolutely, he rejected the rich ladies’ advances and carried on with his journey as a roaming swordsman.

While travelling, he occasionally befriended wandering heroes and gained guidance from senior martial artists, attracting the admiration of various fairy-like maidens.

Once, during a drinking party with young heroes, he got a little too carried away. A flower courtesan stole his purity, leaving Miao Youfang ashamed and furious—he had wanted to save his chastity for his future wife.

He swore never to drink again.

Then, the very next day, he ended up in bed with the flower courtesan once more…

But his luck soon ran out. While travelling in Qingzhou, he encountered a group of formidable experts. Unlike before, when he could always befriend such people, this time with just a few words the group broke out into a fight.

Luckily, some of his friends happened to be passing by and lent a hand. Combined with his own skills and resourcefulness, he barely managed to escape.

After that, through a series of coincidences and lucky breaks, he successfully evaded their pursuit and arrived in Yongzhou.

Within just two incense sticks’ time at the gambling house, he had won four hundred taels of silver, the pile before him growing ever larger.

By the time he reached six hundred taels, a burly staff member of the gambling house came over and said gruffly, “Brother, our boss wants to see you.”

Here we go… Miao Youfang glanced at him, nodded without expression, gathered his loose silver and ingots, picked up his bulging purse, and said,

“Lead the way!”
