# 764. Forgiveness

The wheels rumbled on. In the luxurious and spacious carriage, Wang Simu sat rather awkwardly on a wool-padded soft chair, occasionally glancing at Auntie, who sat upright and motionless, her expression unreadable.

*She has such a strong presence, it makes me feel some pressure...* Wang Simu thought to herself. Daunted by the pressure from her future mother-in-law, she dared not move a muscle.

*Why isn’t Simu moving at all? Why does she look so tense and serious? Is the idea of meeting the Empress Dowager really that frightening? Say something, for heaven’s sake. My backside is already hurting from sitting so still, I want to shift…* Auntie maintained a cool and dignified manner, but inside she was growing increasingly anxious.

But she had never been to the palace to see the Empress Dowager before, and thought this kind of ceremony was necessary.

If Wang Simu did not move, neither would she.

Xu Manor was not far from the Imperial City. Half an hour later, the opulent carriage entered the city, and after another quarter of an hour, it finally arrived at the palace gates.

After answering the Yulin Guards’ questions, the carriage entered the palace with ease and stopped beside the carriage shed.

Supported by her maid, Wang Simu stepped down onto a small wooden stool, and then turned about to help Auntie, just as the maid had helped her, descend from the carriage.

The future mother-in-law and daughter-in-law led the maids towards Fengqi Palace. Auntie kept her eyes forward, maintaining the bearing she had practised many times at home and, with deliberately mild tones, said,

“Simu, this is my first time in the palace. I’m not too familiar with the rules here. Can you tell me about them?”

In fact, Auntie did know a few things. After all, the Empress Dowager was an exceedingly thorough person. Knowing that Madam Xu had never entered the palace before, she had long ago sent a palace matron to the Xu Residence to teach the appropriate etiquette.

It was just that Auntie had not paid much attention, frequently yawning and growing drowsy during lessons. Yet, after a few days of following along, miraculously, she made not a single mistake.

It was not that Auntie was exceptionally gifted, it was simply that she was Silver Gong Xu’s auntie. How could she possibly get it wrong?

Wang Simu answered every question, softly explaining the palace’s rules. Auntie, upon hearing this, thought, *Well, that’s not quite the way I learned. That wretched old matron, how dare she trick me?*

Had they been at home, Auntie would already have set her hands on her hips and raised her eyebrows.

As they conversed, a eunuch led the group into Fengqi Palace.

The environment and furnishings of Fengqi Palace gave Auntie pause, it was hard to imagine this was where the Empress Dowager lived. It was all far too cold and austere.

Crossing the threshold, in a spacious and bright reception hall scented with sandalwood, Auntie met the Empress Dowager, the birth mother of the current female Emperor, a woman of distinguished bearing and breathtaking beauty.

Auntie had encountered her fair share of beauties, partly because her nephew was a notorious lecher and there were often beautiful women staying at the house.

Including herself and her eldest daughter, Xu Lingyue, both striking beauties in their own right.

But upon seeing the Empress Dowager up close, Auntie suddenly realised, were she twenty years younger, she might have been the number one beauty in the capital. Oh, but that National Teacher was the actual number one beauty in the capital.

As for some so-called Flower Goddess, Auntie did not know her, and so paid her no heed or thought.

The Empress Dowager was a cold and reserved sort, and had not been the least self-effacing or courteous towards Auntie just because of Xu Qi’an.

The Empress Dowager was now without desire and therefore unyielding. Some time ago, having become Emperor, her daughter Huaiqing came to see her and attempted to break off the engagement between Lin’an and Xu Qi’an, but the Empress Dowager rebuffed her, saying the marriage was already set and could not be changed.

Huaiqing tried to use her own presence to force her mother into submission, only to find that the latter, having no more worldly desires, did not fear her at all, and Huaiqing suffered a humiliating defeat.

After that, Huaiqing, out of pique, refused to visit her mother ever again.

The Empress Dowager herself was quite unconcerned.

“Silver Gong Xu is a young hero, the husband dreamed of by countless unmarried young ladies. I have also heard a little of his past.”

The Empress Dowager sipped her tea and spoke unhurriedly, her words graceful and detached:

“Lin’an is a princess of the Great Feng. Naturally, it is impossible for her to share a husband with anyone else. I have heard from Simu that you are a strong-minded mistress, able to keep him firmly in hand from an early age.

“I need you to give me a definite answer on this matter.”

*When did I ever keep him firmly in hand? That little brat is always vexing me, just like Lingyin, always going against me day after day...* Auntie kept her expression unreadable, but silently felt wronged on her own behalf.

She had no idea how to accept such a question, so she turned to glance at Wang Simu.

*Why is she looking at me? Is she unhappy about me telling tales to the Empress Dowager, wanting me to deal with the trouble I caused?* Wang Simu felt a sudden chill, but smiled serenely and replied:

“Please rest assured, Your Majesty. Silver Gong Xu and Her Highness Lin’an are deeply in love, and he will certainly not disappoint Her Highness.”

*Oh, it looks like Lingyue and Simu had already agreed on their story. Well, that’s a relief…* Auntie’s eyes brightened, and as the Empress Dowager glanced over, she nodded.

The Empress Dowager nodded as well.

“That is excellent.”

Thereafter, both parties discussed the details of the wedding, occasionally drifting onto other topics.

Whenever Auntie felt the Empress Dowager’s conversation became too lofty, she would glance at Wang Simu.

Wang Simu only thought that her mother-in-law was giving her an opportunity and was treating her as a future daughter-in-law to be groomed, so she acted all the more diligently.

After lunch, Wang Simu returned to the carriage, feeling as if a weight had been lifted, like she had fought a battle, exhausted both mentally and physically.

*Neither the Empress Dowager nor my future mother-in-law are easy to deal with. I really pity myself, stuck between them. Erlang, when will you return to the capital?* Wang Simu suddenly began to miss her fiancé a little.

At the same time, she felt endless admiration for her future mother-in-law. Even though it was her first time entering the palace and meeting the Empress Dowager, she could still maintain such composure and poise, making one feel as though she herself were the Empress Dowager.

Ordinary women, even if their families suddenly became wealthy and their status changed overnight, could never cultivate such a manner or bearing so quickly.

*My future mother-in-law truly is hidden talent…*

*I’m exhausted. My face is almost frozen from holding the same expression. That blasted Xu Ningyan, he even got me involved in his wedding business…* Auntie wished she could rub her face to loosen it up.

The Imperial Study.

Seated behind her grand desk, having finished reading the memorials, Huaiqing spread out a piece of rice paper and began writing:

“Daoist Venerable, Way of Spirit and Incense, Earth Book, arcanists, the Jianzheng, Gatekeeper...”

In her mind, she began to string together these clues.

Back then, the Daoist Venerable had wiped out the Way of Spirit and Incense and collected the Mountain and River God seals. His intention was unclear, but it had already been proven to be linked to the Gatekeeper.

This point had been deduced by tracing back through the arcanist system founded by the first generation Jianzheng.

The arcanist system was clearly an extension or offshoot of the Way of Spirit and Incense, and the present generation of arcanists were suspected to be Gatekeepers, what did this imply?

It suggested that the Way of Spirit and Incense of that year was most likely already connected with the Gatekeeper, and that the Gatekeeper was intended to be born from the Way of Spirit and Incense.

Thus, the Daoist Venerable’s actions conformed to this logic.

The three questions that Xu Qi’an raised within the Earth Book were the cause-and-effect of this truth.

*The earth sect spirit of the Daoist Venerable became an artifact spirit, so the first Jianzheng had nothing to do with the Daoist Venerable. The first generation must have, by happenstance, received the legacy of the Way of Spirit and Incense. Now it seems, the Daoist Venerable's original method of forging the Earth Book was mistaken.*

*On the other hand, did the first Jianzheng, entirely by accident, step onto the correct path of the Gatekeeper? Still, something feels off.*

Huaiqing pondered in silence, racking her brains.

However, as even the Heaven and Earth Society members had no idea what a “Gatekeeper” was or what it symbolised, it was extremely difficult to draw any effective inferences.

A thought struck Huaiqing, and she drew in her wandering thoughts to refocus on the fundamental issue--the Daoist Venerable!

*According to existing clues, it is not hard to deduce that the Daoist Venerable has always been trying something. The Earth Sect avatar tried the Way of Spirit and Incense. So what are the Heaven Sect and Human Sect avatars attempting?*

*That’s right: back then, the Daoist Venerable who drove all the descendants of gods and demons from Jiuzhou, was that the original, or was it one of the Heaven or Human Sect avatars?*

*Besides, with the Earth Sect avatar as reference, the strange disappearance of the Heaven Sect Daoist Leader, the truth behind it has already surfaced.*

This was also an experiment by the Daoist Venerable, but all seemed to have gone wrong.

After a moment, Huaiqing pinched the bridge of her nose and decided to inform Xu Qi’an about this matter, *let him rack his brains, we're tired...*

Just then, a eunuch hurried inside and quietly said,

“Your Majesty, the mistress of the Xu family has just left for the Empress Dowager’s chambers.”

Huaiqing said indifferently,

“Alright.”

She paused, then said,

“Make a trip to the Sitianjian and send the woman Xu Qi’an left there to the Xu residence. Then deliver a message to Lingbao Temple, informing them that Silver Gong Xu and Lin’an will be married in a month.”

Huaiqing still had no interest in targeting a novice like Lin’an, she was merely giving her dear Silver Gong Xu a little extra pressure.

Let him focus on fighting in Yongzhou and not get distracted by romance.

Xunzhou, Prefect’s Office, meeting hall.

Yang Gong had summoned all senior officers here for a council of war, including Xu Qi’an, their pillar of support.

The defence of Xunzhou had been won, but it was only a local victory, the overall situation remained dire.

The real crisis lay ahead for the Great Feng defenders.

Sun Xuanji entered the meeting hall with Protector Yuan. Yang Gong and the other commanders were startled, looking at Protector Yuan and wondering what was going on.

Protector Yuan was in prison garb, shackled at the wrists and ankles, with a wooden cangue about his neck, looking every bit like a condemned criminal on the way to his execution.

Protector Yuan swept his gaze over the group, easily reading their thoughts and understanding their confusion. He sorrowfully explained,

“Silver Gong Xu made me wear this, to teach me a lesson, to remember the trouble that a loose tongue brings.”

Everyone was mightily cheered, yet couldn’t help but ask,

“What if you forget your lesson again?”

Protector Yuan said mournfully,

“In that case, the next step would be beheading me.”

Yang Gong waved a hand,

“No need for that, no need for that.”

Xu Erlang also waved a hand,

“Big Brother was a bit much this time.”

Miao Youfang sighed,

“No matter what, Protector Yuan is still an ally. Silver Gong Xu really was a bit much.”

Protector Yuan glanced at them and felt even more miserable.

Yang Gong’s true thoughts were:

*So even this monkey had his day, heaven’s justice is ever fair, retribution unfailing. Silver Gong Xu is ridding the people of a menace!*

Xu Erlang’s thoughts were:

*Big Brother must’ve faced something extraordinarily awkward; I’ll find out the details later, haha!*

Miao Youfang’s thoughts were:

*If he gets beheaded, will he share some monkey brain with me?*

Yet, having learned from Silver Gong Xu’s example, Protector Yuan forcibly suppressed his instincts, resisting the urge to voice or interpret people’s inner thoughts.

Li Mubai said sourly,

“Monkey, how did you manage to upset Xu Ningyan?”

Protector Yuan was just about to answer when Xu Qi’an himself appeared, slowly walking in from outside.

Everyone looked at him, dumbfounded.

Silver Gong Xu had a glaring iron sword stuck in the top of his skull, the blade thrust straight into his crown, only the hilt showing.

*This, this is too brutal…* Yang Gong and the rest were all dumbstruck, their gaze snapping to Protector Yuan. *What kind of disaster had you brought down?*

“B-big Brother, what happened to you?”

Xu Erlang was so distressed his mouth nearly split to his ears.

“I inadvertently offended the National Teacher, she made me reflect with a sword stuck in my head. Whenever the sword forgives me, she will forgive me.”

Xu Qi’an glanced at Protector Yuan,

“Whenever the sword forgives me, I’ll forgive you!”

Protector Yuan asked urgently,

“When will the sword forgive you?”

Xu Qi’an replied, giving the “monkey” a look of open-hearted resignation,

“It’s a divine sword without an artifact spirit.”

“…” Protector Yuan stood there dumb as a wooden monkey.

Sun Xuanji patted Protector Yuan on the shoulder.

*Strive to be a mute in your next life.*

Xu Xinnian coughed and said,

“Sir Yang, everyone is here. Let’s begin the council.”

He was afraid he couldn’t hold back from teasing his big brother.

Back in the day, big brother was constantly embarrassing and picking on him.

Now, the tables had well and truly turned.
