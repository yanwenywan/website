# 869. The Secret of the Great Calamity

Once he was caught in the pull of the black hole, with Xu Qi’an's strength, although he wouldn't be sucked dry that easily, he had no way of breaking free. Given enough time, he'd still end up dead.

Desolation was just this sort of extreme god-demon. It had only one innate divine ability, devour all things.

Even in ancient times, It had been one of the finest among gods and demons.

When It cast Its devouring divine ability with all Its might, regardless of the cost, below Beyond-rank, It was nearly invincible.

Before Xu Qi’an had advanced to half-step Martial God, he couldn't contend with Desolation in this state.

At that moment, the Taiping sabre raised a circular screen of light, covering Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox and blocking that terrifying suction outside.

But very quickly, that circular screen trembled violently like a bubble in the wind, as though it would shatter at any moment.

"I'm going to sleep..."

The Taiping sabre's faint thought entered Xu Qi’an's mind.

*It still hasnt't digested that sabre?* As the thought flashed through Xu Qi’an's mind, he raised a hand and gave the distant void a light grasp.

The two of them and the sabre vanished instantly and appeared far away. By then, Desolation's devouring divine ability had all but drained this stretch of space of its spiritual essence. Without the slow restraint, Xu Qi’an flickered several times in succession and completely shook off Desolation.

After the black hole pursued in the direction Xu Qi’an had vanished for some distance, it slowly stopped and no longer moved.

Desolation fell into slumber.

The black hole still continued swallowing everything around it.

At the edge of Gods and Demons Island, Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox appeared out of thin air. After confirming they were safe, the former took out the Earth Book fragment and tipped out some robes while checking on the Taiping sabre's condition.

The reason he took out robes was that the Nine-tailed Fox was stark naked at the moment, without a stitch on, her fair and lovely body relying entirely on fox tails to cover her spring light.

"How is it?"

After getting dressed, the silver-haired Yao enchantress was the first to look at the Taiping sabre.

"The spirit of the weapon has fallen asleep. I can't sense its presence." Xu Qi’an shook his head.

The two of them examined the Taiping sabre together. The dark-gold blade was covered in twisted, tree-like patterns. The Nine-tailed Fox only needed one glance to feel tightness in her chest, nausea, dizziness, and swimming vision.

Apart from that, there was no change whatsoever on the sabre's surface.

*We got the sabre, but what exactly was that thing inside the pillar of light, and why would it trigger a great tribulation? I still don't know it at all. Feels like I came for nothing...* Xu Qi’an muttered unhappily.

"Let's leave this place first."

He put forward the suggestion steadily.

"Right!"

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox nodded hard.

...

Outside Gods and Demons Island, blue waves rippled and held the ship aloft.

The Mermaid Queen and Island Master Nulang, who had been waiting outside for nearly two months, sat cross-legged at the bow and continued waiting patiently.

The rest of the descendants of gods and demons were scattered about, chatting or eating. No one left.

For the descendants of gods and demons, with their long lifespans, time didn't mean very much.

They hadn't even invented stopwatches.

Two months wasn't short, but neither was it long. Given the importance of this island and the yearning these descendants of gods and demons had for the spiritual essence inside it, they would keep watching indefinitely.

"Island Master Nulang, Mermaid Queen, do you think the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox and that human expert died on the island?"

A handsome young-looking man drifted over standing on a clam shell and asked with a smile.

Island Master Nulang shook his head.

"No."

The handsome man raised his brows.

"That was the monster that swallowed up all the powerful descendants of gods and demons."

He didn't believe the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox and that human expert could stand against such a being.

Where had these two got such confidence from?

Island Master Nulang looked towards Gods and Demons Island shrouded in thick fog and said:

"That monster once went to the mainland, but It was beaten back and forced to flee overseas again. The one who drove It back was that supreme human expert."

The handsome man froze.

"How do you know?"

Zhenzhu took over in her soft, pearly voice:

"He said so."

The handsome man sneered.

"And you believed him just because he said so? Anyone can boast."

The descendants of gods and demons in the distance burst into laughter.

Island Master Nulang gave him a look and said flatly:

"So you think they knew how terrifying that monster was, then went onto the island just to die?"

The handsome man's face stiffened at once.

After a long while, he muttered unwillingly:

"That may be true, but it's not as though you don't know how terrifying that monster is."

At this moment, a great wind rose over the sea, a strange great wind.

The gale blew towards the depths of Gods and Demons Island.

The descendants of gods and demons outside the island discovered in shock that the thick fog in the sky was collapsing, gathering towards some place within the island. The whole island began trembling without warning, and ripples appeared across the sea with it.

A terrifying aura came from within the island, as though the most powerful god-demon of ancient times had awakened.

The descendants of gods and demons present felt instinctive fear.

"What's happening?"

One of the descendants of gods and demons cried out.

No one could answer his question.

But one thing was certain. Some dreadful upheaval was taking place inside the island.

Island Master Nulang and the Mermaid Queen exchanged a glance. Fear flashed in the former's eyes as he considered whether to slip away.

The latter felt more worry than fear.

Zhenzhu had some friendship with the Queen of the Wanyao Kingdom and Xu Qi’an.

*So they really started fighting? No matter who wins or loses, withdrawing first can't be wrong...* Just as Island Master Nulang was about to "dive into the sea" and flee, a figure flashed before his eyes. It was Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox, who had entered the island months ago.

A smile bloomed across Zhenzhu's soft and lovely face.

"You've finally come out."

In her delight, she forgot to transmit with divine sense.

So Xu Qi’an ignored her.

"Did you run into that monster?"

Island Master Nulang asked.

The handsome man standing on the clam shell, along with the descendants of gods and demons nearby, all looked over.

The Nine-tailed Fox said flatly:

"We had a fight with It. That fellow has gone mad inside the island."

*Gone mad inside the island...* The handsome man's expression immediately turned complicated. Did the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox mean that the two of them had forced that monster into a dead end?

Even if not, it was close enough.

And it seemed that monster didn't even dare chase them out, and could only rage madly inside the island?

He carefully glanced at the human male, and reverence rose in his heart.

The surrounding descendants of gods and demons fell silent as cicadas in winter. In their eyes, Xu Qi’an was already a terrifying expert no weaker than that monster.

The Queen of the Wanyao Kingdom said concisely:

"Turn back. Let's leave this place first, then talk."

Now wasn't a good time to touch Desolation's bad luck. Better to leave first, observe afterwards, and only then decide whether to go back and finish It off.

"Right!"

Island Master Nulang was extremely eager and willingly acted as a sailor, steering the waves so that layer upon layer they surged forward, pushing the ship to speed away in the opposite direction from Gods and Demons Island.

The descendants of gods and demons looked at each other, hesitant and uncertain whether they ought to leave with the ship.

Just at that moment, with a thunderous boom from Gods and Demons Island, huge fissures tore apart the coastline. The island was breaking up.

That settled it. None of the descendants of gods and demons hesitated any longer, and they too left with the ship.

Standing at the bow, Xu Qi’an watched the distant coastline collapse, chunk after chunk sinking to the seabed.

Presumably the spiritual essence of the gods and demons within the island was now also surging into the black hole, becoming nourishment for Desolation to complete Itself.

*Didn't run into any spiritual essence similar to the martial artist system on the island, what a pity. Still, the Taiping sabre snatching away the so-called source of the great tribulation was an unexpected delight. Once it wakes up, I'll ask what that thing actually is...* Xu Qi’an then cast his gaze over the surrounding descendants of gods and demons.

He was considering whether to go on a killing spree and plunder their essence blood.

*Forget it, just a bunch of chickens and dogs...* He let out a breath. Though this overseas journey hadn't brought the gains he wanted, it had still brought an unexpected delight.

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox lay weakly on a soft couch, tugged at the neckline over her chest, and said in surprise:

"The clothes fit rather well."

*That just shows you and Mu Nanzhi are equally broad-minded in the chest,* Xu Qi’an replied inwardly, then said:

"Is it getting to autumn?"

The Nine-tailed Fox tilted her head, thought for a moment, then shook it.

"I don't know. We don't pay much attention to the changing of the seasons. The turning of the four seasons and the shifts in the solar terms are things you humans need to care about."

Because the human race needed to farm.

Xu Qi’an was just about to speak when he suddenly felt something in his heart and lowered his head to look at his wrist.

The glass bead silently lit up, even though he hadn't done anything.

A vague and unreal figure floated out from within the glass bead.

White robes, white hair, white beard, with eyes like bottomless abysses.

"Jianzheng? Did you tamper with it when you refined the artefact?"

Xu Qi’an could tell this was only a thought, the kind that would dissipate very soon.

The Jianzheng slowly nodded.

"This thought was infused by me when I refined the artefact. It can only last for a quarter of an hour.

"Time is short, so I won't waste words with you. You've obtained the 'Gate', and that means you now possess the qualifications to become the gatekeeper. There are some things I can now explain to you clearly."

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox on the soft couch behind immediately sat up straight and paid close attention to the movement here.

...

Western Regions, Beichang City.

Zhulai, wearing a clean long robe and a layered tall hat, led a camel and slowly headed for the city gate with his caravan.

The caravan behind him consisted of one hundred and twenty people and eight flatbed carts drawn by poor-quality horses.

Zhulai's current identity was that of a merchant. He owned a caravan of his own and was now preparing to do business in neighbouring Banyue City.

Of course, all of this was a disguise. This caravan was made up of Mahayana Buddhists. If a migration on such a scale was to avoid detection and remain relatively secret, then it had to be disguised.

The mobility of a caravan solved that problem perfectly.

Naturally, not everyone could leave the city in the name of a caravan either. How could a tiny place like Beichang City produce that many caravans?

Luckily, the Buddhist Dharma Assembly convened by Ananda had become the perfect excuse for Mahayana Buddhists to leave the city with their families and whatever gold, silver, and valuables they could carry.

To return to the matter at hand, the reason Zhulai had been able to organise this migration as the caravan's owner was that he was now a minor leader in the Beichang branch of Mahayana Buddhism.

And the reason he had become a minor leader was that he had introduced all his beggar friends into Mahayana Buddhism as believers.

His performance had been outstanding.

Although he was about to leave his homeland, Zhulai felt not the slightest reluctance. The only impressions his homeland had left him were hunger, cold, and barrenness.

Compared to that, the Central Plains, with its pleasant climate and fertile land, attracted him far more.

Most importantly, Mahayana Buddhism was about to found its own sect and establish its own territory. This was something that made every believer incomparably excited and thrilled.

As they neared the city gate, Zhulai calmly called for his fellow disciples behind him to pick up the pace.

"Hold it!"

Suddenly, two gate guards stopped him.
