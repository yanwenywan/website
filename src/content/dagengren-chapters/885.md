# 885. Ruse of Self-injury

While Huaiqing beckoned the pearl over, she glanced at the fox-tailed, stunning Yao beauty with a smile on her face, then looked at the sincere-faced Xu Qi’an.

Then she reached out and took the mermaid pearl.

The instant the pearl touched her hand, it bloomed with a clear, bright radiance, like the light bulb from Xu Qi’an's previous life. Even in the nearing-noon daylight, it was dazzling enough, bright enough.

"It actually glows."

Huaiqing gave a soft gasp of surprise, her expression and tone carrying a hint of delight.

With this pearl, there'd be no need to light candles in her bedchamber, and its glow was clear and brilliant, far more splendid than candlelight.

A rare treasure indeed.

Then she noticed Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox looking at her with odd expressions.

But the two expressions weren't the same.

Xu Qi’an's gaze and expression were rather complicated, joy, teasing, relief, tenderness, pride, helplessness, and more. It'd been a long time since Huaiqing had seen such a complex mix of emotions on his face.

The Nine-tailed Fox, on the other hand, looked teasing, on the verge of laughter, with even a trace of hostility.

Huaiqing was exceptionally clever, and immediately sensed that something was amiss.

Just then, she saw the Nine-tailed Fox clutch her stomach and burst out laughing. With a face full of mischief, she said smilingly:

"Legend says that as long as one holds the mermaid pearl, when one sees the person one loves, it lights up.

"I thought the ruler of a nation, the dignified female Emperor, would be somehow different. Turns out you're just like any ordinary woman, deeply in love with a philanderer and lecherous man.

"Tsk tsk, you hid it well. I've seen countless women in my life, and I truly didn't realise you liked Silver Gong Xu that much."

Huaiqing looked at the mermaid pearl in her hand. Her face first went pale, then flushed with a captivating red.

She abruptly looked at Xu Qi’an. Shame, anger, embarrassment and awkwardness flickered in her beautiful eyes, just like back at Xu Ningyan and Lin'an's wedding, when Protector Yuan had bluntly exposed her true feelings.

She hadn't expected Xu Qi’an to "set her up" in this way.

"Er, Your Majesty..."

Xu Qi’an coughed. He had just been about to smooth things over and ease the Emperor's embarrassment when he saw the blush on her cheeks suddenly turn pale.

Then she looked at him with a gaze full of incomparable disappointment, with sorrow hidden beneath it.

Huaiqing said coldly:

"Are you very pleased with yourself?"

*Hm? What's with that attitude? Is she lashing out from embarrassment...* Xu Qi’an was taken aback.

Huaiqing coldly flicked her sleeve and threw the mermaid pearl back.

Xu Qi’an reached out and caught it in his palm, instinctively propping up his qi so that it didn't truly come into contact with his hand.

He suddenly understood why Huaiqing was angry.

If the pearl glowed when its holder faced the person they loved, then when he'd held it just now, it hadn't reacted at all.

What did that mean?

It meant that Xu Qi’an loved no one.

No wonder Huaiqing was disappointed. No wonder she was angry.

*This woman's mind turns far too quickly...* When Xu Qi’an had held the pearl earlier, there'd actually been a layer of qi between his palm and the pearl.

That way, there'd be no reaction, so Huaiqing wouldn't notice anything wrong. And on top of that, he had another concern, if Huaiqing learnt of the pearl's special property, she might turn to ask him:

"Who does the pearl glow for?"

And the Nine-tailed Fox would gleefully stir trouble by chiming in, "Yes, who?"

That would've been extremely awkward.

Letting out a sigh, he withdrew his qi and grasped the mermaid pearl.

So, in the eyes of the Nine-tailed Fox and Huaiqing, the pearl bloomed with a clear and bright radiance.

The frost on Huaiqing's face quickly melted away. The disappointment and hurt between her brows vanished, and she stared at the pearl in a daze.

"Oh my, so Silver Gong Xu has secretly been in love with me all along."

The Nine-tailed Fox gave a startled cry, blinked her eyes, her lashes fluttering, then said shyly:

"Th-this, our races are different, we can't be in love."

*Get lost, get lost...* Xu Qi’an dearly wanted to spit in her face.

To avoid another repeat of what had just happened, he put away the pearl and cupped his fists.

"Your servant has been out at sea for several months. I should return to my residence first."

Huaiqing did not stop him, only gave a slight nod.

"I want to visit the Xu residence too!"

the Nine-tailed Fox said coquettishly.

Xu Qi’an ignored her. The large eyeball on his wrist lit up, and he teleported away.

Swaying her slim waist and twisting her hips, the Nine-tailed Fox ran out of the imperial study and turned into a streak of white light, fleeing after him.

Once they had gone, the vast imperial study fell silent. The eunuchs and palace maids had long since withdrawn. Sitting alone in the empty study, Huaiqing could hear her own heart pounding in her chest.

She cupped her face in both hands and gently let out a breath.

Fine then. At least her feelings had been conveyed indirectly. The hot potato was now in Xu Ningyan's hands, and she wouldn't concern herself with it any more.

...

Northern frontier.

A note in the Geographical Records of Jiuzhou:

> Snake Mountain, no vegetation, abundant metal and stone. In the mountain lived a great serpent named Zhujiu.

On the summit of Snake Mountain, Jing's iron cavalry had built a sacrificial altar over ten metres high. To the east, south, west and north of the altar were corpse mounds made from the bodies of the Yao and barbarian tribes.

"Rain Master Nalan, everything is ready."

The ruler of Jing, Xiahou Yushu, stepped onto the altar and bowed with utmost respect.

On the altar, Nalan Tianlu stood with his hands behind his back and gave a slight nod.

"Begin!"

Xiahou Yushu seized a torch and threw it into the brazier. The lamp oil ignited at once, and flames surged up from the basin, belching black smoke.

The rolling black smoke spread beneath the azure sky, plainly visible.

The iron cavalry of Jing on the mountain and at its foot all put down their weapons and dropped to their knees. With thumbs interlocked, left palm wrapping the right, they closed their eyes and prayed to the Warlock God.

The faith of tens of thousands converged together. Though soundless, to Nalan Tianlu's ears it became one grand summons after another.

In distant Mount Jing City, the statue of the Warlock God gave a rumbling shudder, and black qi diffused from it, drifting gracefully towards the northern frontier.

The black qi crossed thousands of mountains and rivers, and in only a dozen breaths reached Snake Mountain tens of thousands of li away. It spread out above the summit and formed into a blurry face.

Everyone on Snake Mountain felt heaven and earth dim, as if night had fallen.

Xiahou Yushu did not dare open his eyes, but he sensed an overwhelming power enveloping the whole of Snake Mountain.

*The Warlock God has come. The altar has summoned the Warlock God...* His heart shook, and he quickly cast out all stray thoughts, becoming even more devout and reverent.

Nalan Tianlu bowed towards the enormous face in the sky. Then he took out a celadon bowl from his sleeve. The bowl was filled with clear water, and within the water swam a crimson snake as thick as a chopstick.

Zhujiu!

Nalan Tianlu had sealed It inside the bowl.

Nalan Tianlu placed the bowl on the table covered in yellow silk, then stepped back several paces.

The blurry face in the sky opened a mouth vast enough to swallow mountains, rivers, sun and moon, and inhaled sharply.

The river dragon in the bowl inevitably flew up, leaving the celadon bowl and being sucked into the Warlock God's mouth.

At the same time, the corpses strewn in the four directions around the altar gave off threads of blood qi, which were likewise drawn into the Warlock God's mouth.

*Although Yan's national fortune has been handed over to the Buddha, the fortune of the northern frontier can be counted as making up for the Warlock God's losses...* Nalan Tianlu thought.

Although they had sounded out the Jianzheng's trump card and understood that apart from supporting Xu Qi’an's ascent to Martial God, he had no other means left;

The Buddha had not allowed the Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts to suffer casualties. The devouring of Leizhou had ended up all thunder and little rain. Overall, the Church of the Warlock God's move had suffered enormous losses.

Nalan Tianlu even felt that the Buddha had withdrawn so cleanly mostly because he had intended to take all the advantages for himself and deny the Church of the Warlock God any chance of profiting as the fisherman.

Not long after, the Warlock God's open mouth slowly closed, and a voice entered Nalan Tianlu's ears:

"Well done."

The voice was impossible to distinguish as male or female, vast and majestic.

Nalan Tianlu maintained his bowing posture and did not move.

"Return to Mount Jing City at once."

The majestic voice came again, then dissipated along with the black clouds.

...

Xu Manor.

In the study, Xu Qi’an looked at Xu Xinnian across the table and said:

"So that's what happened."

The extraordinarily handsome Second Master Xu rubbed his brow and sighed.

"This is completely beyond the pressure my rank should have to bear. Other than despair, what can a mere ordinary man like me possibly do?"

Xu Qi’an patted his little brother on the shoulder.

"You can be responsible for strategy. A tactician doesn't need to go into battle himself."

After saying that, he rubbed Little Pea's head and asked:

"Have you still been dreaming about the big bug lately?"

Xu Lingyin was holding a stack of osmanthus cakes in her arms. It was the season for fragrant autumn osmanthus, so the household made osmanthus cakes every day.

"Yep!" Little Pea replied indistinctly.

"It says every day I'm gonna turn into bones, but if I turn into bones, what if Master and Baiji gnaw on me?"

She had mistaken "Gu", the insectoid lineage, for the homophonic word for "bone", because in daily life, her mother often scolded her by saying:

Are your bones itching for a beating?

Or:

Lingyin, I've stewed pork bone soup for you today.

Xu Xinnian sighed.

"So that's what it means, if she doesn't transform into gu, she won't escape the great catastrophe."

If any Beyond-rank of the major systems replaced the Heavenly Dao, then all cultivators in that system would rise with them.

By urging Xu Lingyin to cultivate transforming into gu as soon as possible, the Gu God was clearly grooming her as a trusted subordinate.

Xu Qi’an said in a deep voice:

"If Lingyin transforms into gu, she'll become a dim-witted gu beast that only acts on instinct and won't retain her humanity.

"Of course, in the Gu God's eyes, humanity is utterly meaningless anyway."

If transforming into gu hadn't had such severe side effects, the shaman clans would long since have defected to the Gu God. They wouldn't have passed down generation after generation the belief that the Gu God must be sealed.

On hearing this, Xu Lingyin's faint brows stood upright.

"As dumb as Baiji?"

She looked terrified.

*You and Baiji are two halfs of the same coin, where do you get the confidence to look down on her...* the two brothers thought at the same time.

Still, though her intelligence left much to be desired, her emotions absolutely couldn't be lost.

If Xu Lingyin lost her emotions, she'd become a gu beast that only knew how to eat.

At that point, whenever Gu Beast Lingyin appeared, all life for ten thousand li would be wiped out and not a blade of grass would grow.

*Four Beyond-ranks... just thinking about it is despair-inducing.* Xu Xinnian gave a grunt and said irritably:

"A strategist is a strategist. Where did this dog-headed part come from?

"The great catastrophe is a matter for the future, and despair is for the future too. But before it arrives, big brother still has a lot you can do.

"Of the four Beyond-ranks, the Buddha's position is already established. Even if big brother becomes a half-step Martial God, you can't rashly enter the Western Regions. There's no need to bother with the Buddhist sect.

"The Gu God has no subordinate force. big brother can simply move the shaman clans into the Central Plains in advance, then wait for It to break free of the seal. There is no better solution.

"But Desolation and the Church of the Warlock God require special attention.

"Once the former returns to its peak, it may gather together the descendants of the overseas gods and demons and bring them under its command. That would be an extremely formidable force. big brother should send people early to win over the descendants of the gods and demons and turn them into our own people.

"As for the latter, the Warlock God has not yet broken free of the seal, and you are now a half-step Martial God, so you could wipe out the Church of the Warlock God. But I think the Warlock system excels at divination. They won't have left such a glaring weakness."

*My brother Xinnian really does have the makings of a Prime Minister...* Xu Qi’an nodded in satisfaction.

"No matter what tricks the Church of the Warlock God has left behind, monks may flee but the temple won't run. I'll make them pay. As for gathering the descendants of the gods and demons, who should I send?"

Xu Xinnian looked towards the doorway and revealed a strange smile.

"That new sister-in-law of mine, of course. The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox, right?"

Hearing that, Xu Qi’an also copied Xu Xinnian and rubbed his brow.

"If not for the fact that she accompanied me out to sea, I'd definitely have hung her up and beaten her by now."

After months away, with the eldest son back home, everyone had originally been quite happy. Then, all of a sudden, a seductive fox spirit had popped out from behind him and said smilingly:

"Hello, little sisters. I'm Xu Ningyan's Yao companion. From now on, I'll be your big sister."

Xu Qi’an had hurriedly said no, no, she's joking, the two of us are completely innocent, as clear as the sun and moon.

But no one believed him.

Who would believe a man who spent every day listening to songs in brothels?

*That's just what fox spirits are like, fearing the world won't be chaotic enough and stirring up trouble everywhere...* Xu Qi’an snatched away Xu Lingyin's cakes, then pressed down on her head and held her in place.

Watching his younger sister squeal in urgency, he suddenly felt much more balanced.

Xu Xinnian had no intention whatsoever of upholding justice for his little sister. Instead, he took two cakes and stuffed them into his mouth.

"If there's nothing else, I'll head out first."

"Where are you going?"

"To watch the show."

...

Inner hall.

The Nine-tailed Fox sipped tea, pinched a cake delicately between her fingers, and swept her gaze over Lin'an with her stiff expression, Mu Nanzhi full of cold sneers, Xu Lingyue expressionless, Yeji looking aggrieved, and Auntie, who was afraid of demons and didn't know where to put her hands.

"You little sisters really can't take a joke," the Nine-tailed Fox said with a smile.

"Silver Gong Xu and I are completely innocent."

She said they were innocent, but kept calling them little sisters one after another.

Mu Nanzhi gave an "oh".

"So innocent, yet you followed him out to sea through life and death together?"

Going through life and death together was what the Nine-tailed Fox herself had just said.

"We each took what we needed," the Nine-tailed Fox said pitifully.

"If I really had something with him, would I have just stood by and watched him flirt with the mermaid queen and even accept a token of love from her?"

The smell of gunpowder in the inner hall instantly intensified.

At this point, even Auntie felt Dalang had gone too far.

At the doorway, Xu Xinnian turned back in surprise to look at his big brother. *Overseas too?*

And with that single glance back, Xu Xinnian was stunned.

The big brother before his eyes had hair white as frost, an exhausted expression, and eyes filled with the vicissitudes carved by time.

In an instant, he seemed to have aged several decades.

*Using the ruse of self-injury...* Xu Xinnian understood at once.
