# 581X. 第六十三章 洛玉衡一剑定风波（求月票）

The National Teacher is here?!

Xu Qi’an almost burst into tears of joy and wanted to shout, “Auntie, I don’t want to work hard anymore.”

Composing himself, he sent a voice transmission in reply, “Wasn’t it supposed to be three days?”

“Within three days,” Luo Yuheng replied succinctly.

Perhaps because of the upcoming dual cultivation, her tone sounded especially cold and reserved.

“National Teacher, I’ve run into some trouble, I’m entangled by a Buddhist Vajra, please come rescue me, quickly. Let’s meet in the mountains thirty li south of Yongzhou city.” Xu Qi’an sent the message impatiently.

“A Buddhist Vajra… Why have you come into conflict with the Buddhist sect, is it about the Dragon Qi?” Luo Yuheng asked.

“He wants to take me to the Western Regions and have me join the Buddhist order, leaving all desires behind,” Xu Qi’an replied equally concisely.

“I’m coming right now,” Luo Yuheng said without wasting any more words.

Xu Qi’an no longer delayed, turned and walked to the pagoda spirit elder monk, and said, “Master, let’s head to the deep mountains fifty li south of Yongzhou city.”

The pagoda spirit elder monk nodded.

…………

To the south of Yongzhou city, deep in the mountains where there were no signs of people.

A dark-golden pagoda sixty metres tall descended from the sky with a deafening boom, crashing into the mountain and shaking the surrounding peaks violently, rocks tumbling all around.

Dunan Vajra leapt down from the pagoda, his muscles writhing as he eased the piercing pain.

The Stupa tower had been resisting him all along, and the magic treasure’s power kept eroding his flesh.

Dunan Vajra understood the depths of the Stupa tower; among Buddhist arts, sealing magic was the foremost.

The Stupa tower was second to none in this regard.

In sealing and support, it ranked among the very best of the Buddhist sect’s magic treasures, which was why it was used to suppress the severed arm of Shenshu.

But there was no perfect magic treasure—the greatest flaw of the Stupa tower was its lack of powerful offensive capabilities.

So long as he held off the Stupa tower and waited for Duqing and Dufan to arrive, the ambush could still be a complete success… Dunan Vajra breathed a long sigh of relief, circulating his qi to soothe the pain in his flesh while keeping a close eye on the Stupa tower.

After last time’s negotiations with the Sitianjian’s fourth-rank spy, Dunan Vajra had laid a trap tailored for Xu Qi’an.

He used three Dragon Qi hosts who had “joined the Buddhist order” as bait, letting them wander the east, south, and west of the city, relying on the Buddha Son’s sharp sense for Dragon Qi to successfully lure him out.

To leave nothing to chance, Dunan Vajra gave the teleportation magic treasure gifted by the Tianjigong to each Dragon Qi host.

If pursued or ambushed, the host could instantly shatter the teleportation device, and Dunan Vajra would be able to rush over without delay.

However, he had underestimated how troublesome the Buddha Son would be.

He had almost suffered a reversal, only for his target to escape.

“If I capture the Buddha Son in one stroke, I can break the stalemate at Arlanda. With the Church of the Warlock God, Great Feng, and the Yao and barbarians all losing out, the perfect chance for the Buddha’s radiance to spread across the nine provinces is coming.

“Capturing the Buddha Son will secure victory.”

Dunan Vajra took a deep breath, gathered his strength, and slammed his dark-golden fist against the Stupa tower, creating a thunderous crash.

The Stupa tower trembled slightly but made no further attempt to escape, as if it had given up.

He’s waiting for Sun Xuanji… Dunan Vajra’s eyes flickered as he focused on sensing the surroundings.

It was an easy guess—Sun Xuanji and the Buddha Son had once cooperated to seize the dragon ley in Leizhou. Now, the Buddha Son was cornered and unable to flee, lingering here only to wait for reinforcements.

Dunan Vajra remained unperturbed. A third-rank arcanist was indeed hard to deal with, and while it might be nearly impossible for him to capture or kill a Master of Heaven’s Secrets, his foe would also not be able to seize the Stupa tower under his nose.

He only needed to remain here and await Duqing and Dufan, and victory would tilt in Buddhism’s favour.

While this thought flashed through his mind, Dunan Vajra suddenly saw a brilliant golden light streaking from the horizon like a golden meteor.

At first it was still far off, but in the blink of an eye, it was upon them.

Waves of golden light swirled and surged, surrounding a radiant figure as she descended atop the Stupa tower.

This was a woman for whom no praise would be excessive. Her features were flawless, her skin paler than snow, a dab of cinnabar on her brow, glowing scarlet and striking to behold.

She wore an intricate and luxurious Daoist robe, a lotus crown binding her lustrous azure-black hair. In her left hand she held a horsetail whisk; in her right, a sharp green-bladed sword.

Her clear eyes sparkled like stars, coldly gazing down at Dunan Vajra beneath the tower.

“Luo Yuheng…”

Dunan Vajra was deeply shaken. He had not expected the one to arrive would be Luo Yuheng, the Daoist Leader of the Human Sect.

The Human Sect was famed for its swordsmanship—the best in all three Daoist sects for attack and killing techniques.

“Luo Yuheng, is your Human Sect set on interfering with Buddhist matters as well?” Dunan Vajra said coldly.

Luo Yuheng’s red lips parted. “Get lost, or die.”

Dunan Vajra snorted. “I’d like to see just how strong your Human Sect’s swordsmanship is—let’s see how many strikes it takes to break my Vajra Body.”

Just hold out for fifteen minutes—within a quarter of an hour, Duqing and Dufan were sure to arrive… Since ascending to third rank, Dunan Vajra’s Vajra Body had never been broken, and he was full of confidence.

He was certainly no match for Luo Yuheng, but it was hardly easy for her to break a Vajra Protector’s body.

As soon as this thought arose, he saw Luo Yuheng draw her three foot green blade. The moment the sword left its sheath, the world filled with sword energy. Countless arcs of sword qi, both real and illusory, filled every inch of the sky.

Even the surrounding grass and trees seemed like weapons, ready at a moment’s notice.

Luo Yuheng’s hand turned gently, making the sword trace a circle; the sword phantoms in the sky followed and wove a ring as well.

When the circle was complete and her sword returned to its place, the thousands upon thousands of sword shadows merged into one.

“Go!”

The female National Teacher tossed the iron sword, which turned into a streak of lightning, streaking straight for Dunan Vajra.

In that instant, Dunan Vajra only felt a tidal wave of sword qi come crashing towards him with irresistible force. For the first time, he felt his own strength was insignificant.

He let out a deep, heavy roar. Under his dark-gold skin, his muscles and veins bulged, and his nine-foot frame swelled even further.

As he bellowed, Dunan Vajra clasped both hands together, catching the iron sword.

His feet ploughed deep grooves into the ground as he was pushed backward by the force of the stroke until, with a crash, he slammed into the mountain.

The sword’s force was unbroken, with continual booms echoing out, and the low slopes suffered violent collapse and fractures, with rocks, earth, and trees coming down in great swathes.

So strong... Xu Qi’an stood by the window, watching this scene, feeling his heart flutter.

Even though he was a third-rank now, he still could not suppress his own shock at Luo Yuheng’s strike.

With a casual slash, she had forced a third-rank Vajra into such a sorry state, able only to defend, unable to fight back.

“The National Teacher’s cultivation is just a tribulation away from the first rank…”

He marvelled inwardly. Suddenly, a shadow fell over the window. Luo Yuheng was standing in the air at the window, blocking the light, her gaze cold as she looked him over.

“You’re still not leaving?”

Xu Qi’an snapped back to his senses—if he delayed any longer, the other two Lohan Vajra would arrive.

He did not hesitate further, turning around and calling to the pagoda spirit, “Master, let’s retreat quickly.”

The Stupa tower soared into the sky, turning into a stream of light and disappearing swiftly.

Luo Yuheng stood atop the pagoda, her robes flowing, radiating immortal grace.

After flying non-stop for half an hour, the Stupa tower landed in a wilderness. The door to the first level opened, and Luo Yuheng stepped lightly down from the pagoda spirit and entered within.

“National Teacher!”

Xu Qi’an was already waiting on the first floor.

Luo Yuheng nodded slightly and said, “Is this the Stupa tower from Leizhou? How did it become your magic treasure?”

“It’s a long story, but simply put, I received the Dharmapara Bodhisattva’s token, was acknowledged by the pagoda, and gained its temporary allegiance.” Xu Qi’an said.

Unfortunately, I don’t practise Buddhist teachings, so I can’t use its true power to the fullest… he thought ruefully.

“Dharmapara?” Luo Yuheng frowned.

“I’ve heard Dharmapara Bodhisattva has been missing for over three hundred years, and the monks at Arlanda can’t find him.” Xu Qi’an explained offhandedly, and then added, via voice transmission:

“Actually, I got the token from Zhenbei King’s deputy, Chu Xianglong, but I hid this from the pagoda spirit.”

As they spoke, they ascended to the third level, where Luo Yuheng nodded in silent greeting to the pagoda spirit elder monk.

“So, a little lass from the Human Sect…” the severed arm of Shenshu clicked its tongue, “impressive cultivation, first-rate among second ranks, but… you’re not far from dying.”

Throughout history, almost no Human Sect Daoist Leader reached first rank—at second-rank peak, they suppressed karmic flames, but when suppression was no longer possible, they perished to the heavenly tribulation.

The severed arm of Shenshu tempted, “Release my seal, and I’ll tell you how to survive the tribulation.”

Xu Qi’an said bluntly, “Find someone with fortune for dual cultivation, right?”

Shenshu’s words caught in his throat, and after a while, he gave a dry laugh to cover his embarrassment, “Kid, you know quite a lot.”

“Master, times have changed…” Xu Qi’an sneered, “You’ve just been sealed for five hundred years, and your news is outdated.”

Shenshu’s aura changed, and he snapped harshly, “Boy, are you looking for death?”

……….

A quarter of an hour after the Stupa tower departed, a ray of rosy light swept over from the horizon. It was a nine-petalled lotus platform, upon which stood a Vajra with dark gold skin and a flaming halo burning behind his head.

This Vajra’s appearance was hideously ugly, with a vicious look in his eyes. His very image was enough to make ordinary people’s legs go weak with fear.

It made one suspect that perhaps his embryo had suffered some trauma, resulting in features so unkind to the world.

But for those from the Western Regions, they could recognise at a glance that he belonged to the Asura clan, famous for their ugliness and belligerence.

Beside the Asura Vajra stood a withered old man, knees crossed, head bowed, holding a flower in his hands. His white eyebrows drooped to his cheeks, and there was a fleshy mole between them.

His eyes were closed, as if meditating on the Dao.

The lotus platform hovered over the chaotic stones below. The Asura Vajra, Dufan, looked down for a moment and said in a deep voice,

"Dunan, junior brother!"

Several seconds later, a noise came from among the mangled rocks. Debris tumbled down, and Vajra Dunan climbed out.

He was in a pathetic state; his red and yellow kasaya was in tatters, his dark golden skin dull and lacklustre, and there was a trace of golden blood at the corner of his mouth.

"You’re injured. In the Great Feng today, who could beat you into such a sorry state?" The Asura Vajra Dufan frowned.

"Daoist Leader Luo Yuheng of the Human Sect," Dunan Vajra replied.

The elderly man sitting cross-legged on the lotus platform, the Luohan of Passion, opened his eyes slowly and said, "Dunan, you’ve startled the snake by hitting the grass. Why not wait for Dufan and me to arrive before making your move?"

Vajra Dunan pressed his palms together and said, "That second-rank arcanist was also plotting against the Buddha Son. I merely wished to act swiftly and capture the Buddha Son before he could. I underestimated the strength of the Buddha Son."

Tianjigong had requested a collaboration, to which Dunan had agreed, but it was only a feint.

He had originally intended to capture the Buddha Son before that arcanist could make a move, which was why he had not waited for his fellow disciples, Dufan and Duching.

"But at least we’ve now tested the Buddha Son’s trump cards," Vajra Dunan added.

"He has Luo Yuheng’s assistance, and Sun Xuanji of the Sitianjian is helping him too. We must consider how to deal with them. As for startling the snake, the Dragon Qi host is a matter of open conspiracy; as long as he wishes to collect Dragon Qi, he’ll have to face us sooner or later.

"This is not our only chance. There will be many."

The Luohan of Passion made a gesture of holding a flower between his fingers, his voice resonant and calm: "Only an arcanist can deal with an arcanist. There’s no harm in working with Tianjigong."

Vajra Dunan raised his non-existent eyebrows (for he had none), and said, "Has our agreement with that arcanist been concluded?"

The Luohan of Passion nodded.

…………

Inside the inn.

Li Lingsu forcefully pushed open Mu Nanzhi’s door and said anxiously,

"I’ve just made enquiries. As expected, the senior Xu encountered Vajra Dunan."

Mu Nanzhi’s face drained of colour. Instinctively, she clutched the white fox in her arms, "A third-rank Vajra?"

"A third-rank Vajra?" the little white fox echoed, her voice crisp.

Li Lingsu nodded.

Only after returning to Yongzhou city had he learned of the fierce battle that had broken out not long before. Several commoners had died in the shockwave, and more than a dozen had been injured.

According to martial artists who had witnessed the fight from afar, one of the combatants was a monk in a kasaya, noted for being tall, having dark gold skin, and no eyebrows, beard, or hair.

The other had an ordinary face, but was able to command animals.

Comparing this with the information Mu Nanzhi had given him before his enquiries, the conclusion that Xu Qian had run into a third-rank Vajra was easily reached.

"Do you know what’s happening now?" Mu Nanzhi asked anxiously.

Li Lingsu shook his head regretfully.

Mu Nanzhi paced back and forth in the room, frowning deeply.

Li Lingsu was seeing her this anxious for the first time. The Madam Xu of the past had always been leisurely and carefree; everything she said and did carried a lazy tone, as if she were a celestial maiden untouched by worldly concerns — aside from a faint liking for him, perhaps — earthly affairs had never troubled her heart.

Phew, it’s good. Madam Xu still cares a lot for Xu Qian after all. That’s best; if she always thought about me, sooner or later Xu Qian would kill me. Ah, this damned charm of mine...

While the two humans and one fox remained anxious, the window suddenly rattled with a flapping noise.

A black wild bird alighted on the window frame and spoke in human tongue, "Don’t worry, I’m quite well."

Li Lingsu and Mu Nanzhi turned around in astonishment, their faces alight with joy.

The little white fox was delighted too.

"How are you now? Are you hurt? Have you escaped your pursuers? Is that bald puppet still with you?" Mu Nanzhi fired off a string of questions.

The wild bird pecked its head, "I’m fine. Stay put in the inn and there will be no trouble. Just wait for my return."

Then, it swiveled its head to "glare" at Li Lingsu, "Come with me outside the city for a bit."

………

Northern suburbs of Yongzhou city, Cyan Apricot Garden.

This was Gongsun Xiangyang’s usual place to invite friends for group activities during his leisure time, and was famous among certain circles in Yongzhou.

Whenever a banquet was held, carriages of high officials and nobles streamed in unceasingly; the most renowned oiran from all the major brothels of Yongzhou city came happily at his invitation, and left with faces frosted white in satisfaction.

Normally, Cyan Apricot Garden was especially peaceful and tranquil. Save for the servants and handmaids, family members of the Gongsuns rarely stayed here.

The garden was elegant, planted with plum, orchid, bamboo, and chrysanthemum. Winding paths led to secluded nooks, and there was even a hot spring in the rear courtyard — the true reason Cyan Apricot Garden was so favoured by Gongsun Xiangyang and other nobles.

In a tea room adorned with renowned calligraphy and paintings, Xu Qi’an and the National Teacher sat opposite each other, drinking tea and discussing all that had happened since leaving the capital.

Luo Yuheng held her teacup, her face bare and calm as she listened quietly.

Elegant and cool, with the vermilion between her brows setting off her noble and aloof beauty; if one considered her status as National Teacher of the Great Feng and Daoist Leader of the second rank, that ethereal beauty gained an added air of intimidating majesty.

It was hard to imagine such a woman would engage in dual cultivation with me... The experienced Xu Qi’an felt a twinge of unease.

Among all the women he had met, Luo Yuheng’s looks and bearing ranked second; after all, the reincarnated Flower Goddess was basically cheating.

As for her figure, constrained by the fashions of the time, Xu Qi’an did not get to see Princess Huaiqing in tight denim, nor the curvy Consort Wang in body-hugging trousers, nor the peerless Luo Yuheng beneath her Daoist robes.

All he could go by was the majestic swell of her chest, and estimate she was truly well-endowed.

"By the way, I’ve already asked Li Lingsu to come, National Teacher. May I trouble you to lift his seal?" said Xu Qi’an.

"So that in the next seven days, he can protect Mu Nanzhi?" Luo Yuheng replied coolly.

Damn, it really is going to take seven days. Auntie, can we talk this over... Xu Qi’an’s heart sank.

Luo Yuheng seemed to realise her slip of the tongue, and fell silent.

As an awkward atmosphere settled, the sound of footsteps came from outside.

Led by a handmaid from the garden, Li Lingsu entered:

"Senior, today was perilous indeed, you actually ran into Vajra Dunn..."

His voice stopped abruptly as he stood frozen in the doorway, staring blankly at Luo Yuheng.
