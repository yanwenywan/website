# 875. The Ancient Battlefield Under the Sea

Those eyes were enormous beyond compare, lashless, threaded all over with blood vessels, staring at the crowd without a trace of emotion.

Then cracks split open across the ground, and every crack was an eye. Some were the size of ordinary human eyes, some as large as cartwheels, water vats, or ponds, with no fixed scale.

But one thing was the same, their gaze was even more terrifying than the stare of an ancient fiend, even more enough to make one's heart tremble with dread.

The eye on that distant mountain range suddenly rolled rapidly and fixed itself on Arhat Du'e. At once, all the eyes turned to stare at Arhat Du'e.

Right after that, countless pupils began to tremble violently.

His fortune warned him again. A chill rose in Arhat Du'e's heart, a chill that seeped into his bones. What he felt first was not fear, but smallness.

The smallness of his own self.

The other side seemed to be the incarnation of the will of heaven and earth. Just by gazing at him, it made Arhat Du'e unable to resist the urge to kneel and submit to that will.

He had never felt this even when facing a Bodhisattva directly.

It was not just him. In the distance, Chu Yuanzhen, Li Miaozhen, Daoist Jinlian, and Sun Xuanji felt the same.

*Magnificent, vast, majestic...* none of these were enough to describe those eyes, that existence.

If one had to find a fitting word, it would be "Heaven"!

A sense of smallness rose unbidden in every heart.

The smallness of being born human.

Since stepping into the beyond-mortal realm, they had never experienced such a thing. Even when Daoist Jinlian had once faced Luo Yuheng's heavenly tribulation, he had never felt this way.

"Go!"

The moment Arhat Du'e shouted, he turned his head and found that only Hengyuan remained beside him. Al-Asura had already fled without a trace.

...

Arhat Du'e didn't hesitate. Buddhist light rippled from the nine-petalled lotus throne, propelling him away like a streak of golden lightning.

Master Hengyuan followed close behind.

"Mahayana Buddhism, Mahayana Buddhism..."

A horrifying howl came from behind them.

Li Miaozhen and the others, who were watching warily from even farther away, saw that stretch of plain come alive. The ground surged like waves, turning into earthen walls thousands of feet high, blotting out the sky as they crashed towards Arhat Du'e and Hengyuan.

When that great wave had pursued them for several miles, clods of earth and wild sand began to cascade away with rustling sounds, revealing its true form. It was dark red flesh, flesh like a tsunami, blanketing heaven and earth.

The feeling of smallness vanished.

Though the other side was still terrifying, so powerful it made them tremble and fear, that innate smallness of being human had disappeared from everyone's hearts.

The dark red flesh condensed into a giant hand that covered the sky. The instant this enormous palm appeared, it crossed the distance of space and loomed over Arhat Du'e, al-Asura, and Hengyuan.

*The Walker Dharma Aspect?* Arhat Du'e was greatly alarmed.

The three of them continued riding the wind without pause. Above each of their heads appeared the second-rank Executioner Phala, dazzling seven-coloured light shining together as they tried to withstand the grasp by force.

Li Miaozhen and Daoist Jinlian both stretched out their hands, frantically adding good luck to the three of them.

At the critical moment, that giant hand collapsed.

The flesh composing it seemed to lose its power and suddenly caved in, smashing into the ground. In an instant, it was like a mountain toppling, making the earth quake violently and sending dust flying.

Al-Asura, Arhat Du'e, and Hengyuan had escaped death, yet still didn't dare stop. Only after returning to Li Miaozhen's side and the others did they dare turn back to look.

That dark red flesh was now slowly sinking into the ground until it disappeared.

"That scared the life out of me."

Al-Asura rubbed his bald head.

Arhat Du'e and Hengyuan said nothing, but from their expressions and their eyes, they probably felt much the same as al-Asura.

"When we approached last time, He didn't attack me..."

After pondering for a moment, Arhat Du'e said,

"Just now, my fortune warned me. He wanted to devour me and reclaim the fortune."

Everyone's minds were in turmoil, all kinds of questions flashing through them. Daoist Jinlian said:

"We can discuss this later. First, let's leave the Western Regions and return to Leizhou. We'll wait for Master Shenshu to come over."

After returning to Leizhou territory, the group landed on an uninhabited hill and sat cross-legged beneath an ancient pine.

Chu Yuanzhen, the top graduate with a lock of white hair hanging before his forehead, was the first to open the discussion.

"That, was that the Buddha?"

The orange cat Daoist, Arhat Du'e, and the others nodded.

Master Hengyuan pressed his palms together. His brows were tightly furrowed, and his expression was grave.

"Why has the Buddha become like this?"

No one answered.

A body transformed into mountains and rivers was something unheard of, beyond the limits of their understanding.

Daoist Jinlian looked at Arhat Du'e and said:

"Master, you said He wanted to devour you and reclaim fortune?"

Arhat Du'e nodded.

Daoist Jinlian slowly inclined his head and voiced his view.

"Though we didn't dare go deeper into the Western Regions past Him, what Arhat Du'e said is probably true."

He meant the matter of the Buddha devouring the living beings of the Western Regions and turning into mountains, rivers, and cities.

Li Miaozhen frowned.

"But when we came, we really did see plenty of living people. They hadn't been devoured by the Buddha. There's no reason for Him to have swallowed only half..."

Before she could finish, al-Asura snatched up the thread of the discussion. His voice was low and magnetically masculine.

"Did any of you notice that when the Buddha made His move, there was a clear change halfway through? The earth and stone crumbled away, exposing the dark red flesh. The two states before and after gave me different feelings.

"He was still terrifying, but He seemed to have lost a certain ability to cow the mind."

*An ability to cow the mind? He'd dressed up the fact that he abandoned Arhat Du'e and Hengyuan and fled in quite fresh and elegant terms...* Li Miaozhen muttered inwardly.

Still, she understood what al-Asura meant by "cowing the mind".

Because she had felt that same smallness too.

Arhat Du'e nodded and said:

"And the area where He lost that ability was precisely the area He had not assimilated."

At that moment, he saw Sun Xuanji flick his sleeve and produce a low desk, together with brush, ink, paper, and inkstone, then begin writing furiously.

As though realising something, Chu Yuanzhen summed up:

"Could it be that the establishment of Mahayana Buddhism caused the Buddha to lose part of His fortune, and because of that, the Buddha can no longer expand further? So His attitude towards Arhat Du'e changed clearly before and after."

This guess of his was based on the premise that Beyond-ranks required fortune, combined with the changes in the Western Regions and Arhat Du'e's warning from fortune.

The orange cat Daoist stroked his beard.

"This poor Daoist thinks the same. That move with Mahayana Buddhism has had outstanding results.

"Without the support of fortune, the Buddha seems unable to keep expanding. So long as we don't enter the territory of the Western Regions, there should be no danger."

Everyone voiced their agreement.

Then they noticed Sun Xuanji still holding his brush, frozen in place.

When they went over for a look, they saw he had written on the paper:

"The Buddha lost fortune and can't continue devour..."

He hadn't been able to write the rest, because Chu Yuanzhen had already said it all.

"Ah, well..." Lady Flying Swallow offered a comforting remark. "Bring Protector Yuan next time."

*Protector Yuan is only a fourth-rank little Yao. He really shouldn't have to bear adventures so far beyond his cultivation...* the kind and simple Master Hengyuan thought.

*Good idea, scare that dead monkey to death...* that was what everyone except Arhat Du'e was thinking.

Chu Yuanzhen immediately sent the conclusions of their discussion to the Earth Book chat group.

【ONE: The inference is correct. That's probably how it is. I asked Duke Wei and Scholar Zhao. They don't know much about the Buddha's abnormality, but Scholar Zhao said it's probably related to the so-called great calamity.】

*The Buddha in that state does indeed deserve to be called a great calamity. If He is allowed to expand at will, the consequences would be unimaginable...* the members of the Heaven and Earth Society felt this deeply.

【ONE: Xu Ningyan, Xu Ningyan, have you seen the message?】

He asked several times in a row, but no one answered.

【NINE: Xu Ningyan is still overseas and hasn't returned.】

With that crude first-rank martial artist absent, everyone always felt a bit less secure.

【SEVEN: That bastard Xu Ningyan, could he have run off overseas and decided not to come back? If he can't advance to half-step Martial God, he probably will run. After all, he's so lustful and lecherous.】

*Shouldn't it be greed for life and fear of death? What's that got to do with being lustful and lecherous? Li Lingsu's taking the chance to smear Xu Qi'an again...* the members of the Heaven and Earth Society grumbled inwardly.

【TWO: Senior Brother, have you reached the Southern Marches?】

【SEVEN: I have. I'm waiting for Master Shenshu to come out of seclusion. Speaking of which, the beauties of the fox tribe really are extraordinary. Especially a few of Yeji's sisters, each one a nation-toppling beauty. Every single one of them is more beautiful than you, Junior Sister.】

【TWO: Senior Brother, when we get back to the capital, I'll tell your wives that. Unless, of course, you tell me how many Ji there are in the Southern Marches.】

【SEVEN: Senior Brother was wrong. Senior Brother still has matters to discuss with the three beauties Youji, Lingji, and Qingji. Take care, everyone.】

...

In the deep sea.

A gigantic ocean trench stretched down beyond sight, so black as though it could swallow light itself.

Xu Qi'an scattered a handful of fiery red shells. As they fell towards the trench, he detonated them with his qi.

The fire element contained in the shells burst open violently, expanding into clusters of fireballs in the lightless black depths of the sea.

The undercurrents instantly became turbulent.

In the flickering firelight, Xu Qi'an and the Nine-Tailed Fox saw a thick tentacle shoot out from the trench. It was enormous, like a giant python that could swallow the sky, and every sucker was as big as a water vat.

Broken patterns were branded across the surface of the tentacle.

*Could this Beyond-rank god-demon be a giant squid...* Xu Qi'an felt a little disillusioned. Out of sheer curiosity, he had been looking forward to seeing what the other's true form would be like.

The tentacle twisted as it lashed over, sweeping viciously at the Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox.

The undercurrents on the seabed boiled at once, and all Xu Qi'an could hear by his ears was the rumbling crash of colliding currents.

*More sensitive to the descendants of gods and demons...* Xu Qi'an raised his brows and stood by with folded hands, making no move to help the vixen against the enemy.

The Nine-Tailed Heavenly Fox also didn't use her strongest weapon, her tails.

Her eight fluffy fox tails slapped like the tentacles of a jellyfish, propelling her towards the tentacle. Clenching her delicate fist, she threw a punch that left dense bubbles in its wake.

Bang!

The muffled sound rang through the sea bed, like a torpedo exploding.

In Xu Qi'an's sight, the space ahead was instantly covered in dense bubbles. The surging undercurrent swept in all directions like a shockwave and slammed into his chest.

The Nine-Tailed Fox's body flew backwards, dragging out a vacuum trail across the seabed.

Seeing this, Xu Qi'an gained a fairly clear estimate of the tentacle's strength.

Though the queen's raw strength was inferior to a martial artist's, as a descendant of gods and demons, her physical might absolutely surpassed that of first rank experts from other systems.

But in that pure contest of punching force just now, she had clearly been no match for the tentacle.

*The stronger it is, the greater my gains after I devour it. Maybe I really can step into the half-step Martial God realm in one stroke...* Xu Qi'an transmitted his voice:

"Your Majesty, help me tie it down. I'll go below and look for its true body."

The Nine-Tailed Fox gave an "Mm" in her usual tone. Though she had lost the clash of strength just now, she wasn't injured.
