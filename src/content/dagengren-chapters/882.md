# 882. A Battle Strength Beyond Shenshu

*Xu Qi'an?*

*When had he arrived?*

The appearance of this first rank martial artist caught the beyond-mortals nearby and afar completely off guard.

It was as if he had appeared out of nowhere, without any warning, slipping past the perception of all these extraordinary experts.

"Silver Gong Xu's here~"

In the distance, Luanyu gave a delighted cry, her eyes sparkling, her smile blooming like a flower.

Longtu let out a sigh of relief. "He made it back just in time. Now we've finally got a fight on our hands."

"Barely," Chunyan, the Heart Gu Master, murmured.

With Xu Qi'an, this first rank martial artist, joining the battle, the Great Feng could finally claw back some of its disadvantage. At the very least, when facing the three Bodhisattvas, they were no longer limited to passive defence, but could take the offensive.

It was even possible that a first rank martial artist could assist a half-step Martial God, and perhaps hold the Buddha in a stalemate.

*He's finally back...* al-Asura, Daoist Jinlian and the others all felt a weight lift from their hearts.

The Great Feng badly needed combat power at the level of a first rank martial artist.

Only then could they free their hands to aid Shenshu and increase his chances against a Beyond-rank. Shenshu alone simply couldn't defeat the Buddha.

Shenshu's current miserable state was the best proof of that.

Although a half-step Martial God would not be easily defeated, if the Buddha kept grinding him down like this, defeat would only be a matter of time.

The court only hoped that ending would come a little later, so that more common folk could be evacuated, and more innocent lives could be saved.

As countless thoughts surged through everyone's minds, they saw Xu Qi'an emerge step by step, bringing Arhat Du'e with him.

Wherever he passed, the dark red flesh-like matter, like churned mud, was knocked aside by his qi. Though it lunged at him like mad, it simply could not get close to him.

This scene was exactly like how Shenshu had forced them back step by step not long ago.

*This...* the hearts of the Great Feng's extraordinary experts pounded wildly. A bold guess began to take shape in their minds, and because of it their heartbeats quickened, their blood surged, and irrepressible delight rose within them.

Following behind Xu Qi'an, Du'e stepped out into the normal zone bit by bit. The gaunt old monk stared at him and slowly said,

"You've advanced to half-step Martial God?"

The moment that question was spoken, everyone's gaze focused on Xu Qi'an.

Sarun Agu darted several hundred metres away, staring at him without even blinking.

Yang Gong, Sun Xuanji, Daoist Jinlian, and farther back Chu Yuanzhen and Hengyuan, all of them couldn't help holding their breath.

Xu Qi'an gave a slight nod, his expression calm.

"Mm."

A calm expression, a flat tone.

But to those who heard it, it was like a giant boulder crashing into a lake. No, like a meteor smashing into the ocean, stirring emotions like a tsunami.

Another half-step Martial God had been born.

In Jiuzhou, a new half-step Martial God had appeared.

Since the end of the age of gods and demons, through endless ages, there had only ever been one clearly recorded half-step Martial God, Shenshu.

Now, and from now on, a new name would be entered into the annals of history--Xu Qi'an!

In a certain sense, the number of half-step Martial Gods was even rarer than Beyond-ranks.

Vaidurya Bodhisattva, Agaruka Bodhisattva, and Samantabhadra Bodhisattva had only been somewhat surprised to see Xu Qi'an rush over. Now, the expressions of all three gradually stiffened.

Luo Yuheng, cold as frost, gazed at Xu Qi'an, her eyes and brows gentle.

What she was looking at was not a half-step Martial God, but her own man.

*Most likely he saw the Jianzheng, or got something from the Sitianjian before going out to sea...* Daoist Jinlian stroked his beard and smiled. In an instant, he connected many things.

As someone equally skilled in scheming and laying plans, Daoist Orange Cat understood the unfathomable depth of the Jianzheng. He had long held a measure of foresight and expectation regarding Xu Qi'an's advancement.

*When we first met in the Southern Marches, he was only a mere third rank martial artist. In just a year, he's already a half-step Martial God...* al-Asura felt both stirred and excited. Recalling the past, it all felt dreamlike.

*He really made it this far, becoming the second half-step Martial God in the world of Jiuzhou. That's my disciple. I'm the teacher of a half-step Martial God. The calamity in Leizhou can be resolved today, and the innocent common folk won't suffer...* Yang Gong's hands, hidden in his sleeves, trembled slightly as his emotions surged.

Sun Xuanji: "..."

All Senior Brother Sun's countless words remained in his heart.

From within the night came Chu Yuanzhen's wild, unrestrained laughter, sweeping away the gloom.

Beside him, Master Hengyuan pressed his palms together, his face full of relief.

"Shenshu alone is already this terrifying. Now Xu Qi'an has also become a half-step Martial God. That should be enough to contend with a Beyond-rank, right?" Irbu muttered softly.

Fear could clearly be seen and heard in both his tone and his expression.

*So he really was your trump card, Jianzheng...* Sarun Agu's eyes darkened. No emotion could be read from his old, coarse face.

A certain thought in his heart had finally been confirmed.

After a brief moment of blank shock, only one thought remained in Luanyu's mind:

*I slept with a half-step Martial God. I'm the most accomplished leader in the history of the Desire Gu tribe!*

Her face flushed red with excitement, and her whole body trembled.

The leaders of the Shaman Clans were equally ecstatic. Though they had not slept with a half-step Martial God, Xu Qi'an becoming the second half-step Martial God in the history of Jiuzhou also meant that the Southern Marches now had a half-step Martial God as an ally.

What had happened in the Western Regions today might well become the model for the Southern Marches tomorrow.

They couldn't help but fear it.

But now, with such a powerful ally, the leaders all felt much more at ease.

"Amitabha, may Mahayana Buddhism endure for a thousand autumns and ten thousand generations!"

A sincere smile appeared on Du'e's gaunt face.

Xu Qi'an swept his gaze across the Great Feng's beyond-mortals, the Shaman clan leaders, the Warlocks of the Church of the Warlock God, and the Buddhist Bodhisattvas. Seeing the fear, excitement, and fury on their faces, he withdrew his gaze and looked towards Shenshu and the Buddha.

Then he returned to Shenshu's side step by step, parting the muddy flesh-like matter along the way.

During this process, every extraordinary expert looked over, their eyes following his footsteps.

How strong was this newly advanced half-step Martial God?

"Well done."

Xu Qi'an nodded towards Shenshu.

At this moment, fresh flesh had already grown back over Shenshu, but he had not fully recovered. He looked like a body flayed of its skin.

The power of the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect still clung to his bones, eroding his body and resisting the indestructible trait of a half-step Martial God. It made Shenshu's regeneration relatively slow.

"You've done well too," Shenshu said flatly.

As the two spoke, the towering Buddha statue formed from flesh-like matter looked down on Xu Qi'an from above and, for the first time, gave voice in a grand and majestic tone:

"Gatekeeper!"

*So the Beyond-ranks really do know everything, know the Martial God is the predetermined Gatekeeper. Only Desolation overseas doesn't know...* Xu Qi'an gave a low chuckle.

"You and the Warlock God hunted first rank martial artists and refused to allow them to grow, all to prevent the emergence of a Gatekeeper.

"Now that I've already become a half-step Martial God, undying and indestructible, can you still kill me?"

*What is he talking about...* al-Asura, Yang Gong, Daoist Jinlian and the others were completely baffled. The Shaman clan leaders were equally unable to make sense of it.

*Isn't the Gatekeeper the Jianzheng? What did that have to do with martial artists?*

Vaguely, they felt the information hidden in Xu Qi'an's words was extremely important, but this was not the time to dig to the bottom of it. They could only force down their itch-like curiosity and remain still.

The Buddha answered Xu Qi'an with silence.

And in that silence, one miniature sun after another slowly took shape.

"Don't be careless," Shenshu warned.

"I know."

Xu Qi'an raised his wrist, making the big eyeball light up. He tried to cut space apart and shift the miniature suns away.

But he quickly failed.

Every single miniature sun contained the Buddha's will within it.

They were one whole.

Unless they were all transferred away in one go, but this magical artefact couldn't slice away a space several kilometres across. That was beyond the range it could cover.

At that moment, the Buddha vanished strangely and reappeared behind Xu Qi’an. The Vajra Dharma Aspect surged with terrifying force as twelve fists crashed down.

That blow was enough to hammer a first rank martial artist flat on the ground.

The Buddhist script representing "man" in the Great Samsara Dharma Aspect lit up and cast a beam of golden light onto Xu Qi’an.

The light of the Great Samsara Dharma Aspect missed. Xu Qi’an appeared behind the Buddha, just as ghostlike, silent and without a trace.

But he failed to strike and tear apart the Buddha image condensed by the Buddha... The Great Mercy and Great Compassion Dharma Aspect was chanting sutras, waves of Sanskrit echoing out and dispelling the enemy's will to fight.

The Great Wisdom Dharma Aspect reversed its wheel of light, lowering the intelligence of the enemies around it.

Relying on His own strange and unfathomable methods, the Buddha recreated the very scene He had used earlier against Shenshu.

Seeing this, the first to react was Sun Xuanji. He opened his storage treasure and took out another bronze bell... Arcanists were always this rich.

Yang Gong flicked his Confucian cap, and clear light surged forth in encouragement.

Daoist Jinlian raised a hand towards Xu Qi’an, intending to add fortune to him.

"Boom!"

Thunder roared across the sky as bolts as thick as water vats descended. In an instant, heaven and earth were dyed blazing white, and torrential rain poured down.

This interrupted Sun Xuanji and the others, forcing them into passive defence.

Summoning wind and rain.

The ability of a second rank Master of Rain.

*The Church of the Warlock God has entered the fray as well...* The beyond-mortal experts on the side of the Great Feng all felt their hearts sink.

Luo Yuheng did not hesitate. She brought two fingers together like a sword seal and directed her flying sword at Xu Qi’an. The blade erupted with intense seven emotions and six desires, enough to drag one into indulgence and corruption.

Chu Yuanzhen had used this move before.

As expected, the Buddha raised the colourless glazed domain, draining all colour from everything around.

Even the flying sword of an Earthly Immortal could not avoid falling in the face of a spell cast by a Beyond-rank being.

At this moment, the Buddha's Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect had already taken shape.

Luo Yuheng and the others, having seen Shenshu's miserable state a moment ago, watched with mounting anxiety.

Even a half-step Martial God would have to pay a grievous price to withstand the Buddha's fully charged strike.

And to take the Great Sun bombardment head-on without any defence...

The Great Sun slowly crashed towards Xu Qi’an.

Suddenly, a delicate laugh rang out in the night sky.

The laughter was sweet and enchanting. It was not loud, yet it drilled clearly into everyone's ears, into their souls, bringing a bewitchment like a hundred claws scratching at the heart. Irbu, Chu Yuanzhen, and the other cultivators of lower rank began seeing waves of illusions before their eyes.

They were dizzy and dazed.

Sarun Agu and the other first and second rank experts followed the sound and looked over. Beneath the heavy night curtain, a peerless enchantress came walking through the air with snowy bare feet. She was so beautiful she seemed no mortal creature, and her eight fox tails spread behind her like a peacock's fan.

Enchanting and gorgeous.

The nine-tailed fox had arrived, and with her came layer upon layer of pressure surging like ocean tides, blanketing every expert present without distinction.

*F--first rank?!*

The expressions of the three Bodhisattvas of the Buddhist sect changed slightly.

They knew very well what a first rank nine-tailed fox meant. Before they had time to feel either shock or anger, the attention of all the beyond-mortal beings, including them, was drawn at once to Xu Qi’an and the Buddha.

Under the assault of the demonic sound, Xu Qi’an broke free from the effects of the various Dharma Aspects.

And at that very moment, the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect came crashing down.

Taking a deep breath, Xu Qi’an removed the bracelet and swallowed it whole. Then blood mist burst from every pore of his body, his vital blood burned, and power surged through every limb and organ.

Blood sacrifice!

He shook both arms and said in a deep voice:

"The power of all living beings!"

Strand after strand of invisible, intangible currents came rushing in from all directions, pouring into his body like rivers flowing into the sea.

His aura soared explosively.

And that wasn't all. Twisted patterns appeared on the surface of his body, like tattoos, densely covering every inch of skin.

Anyone who saw those markings felt terror explode in their heart. It was as if he himself was the embodiment of "power", a god who ruled over all strength in the world.

Finally, he collapsed all his qi and settled all his emotions.

One Blade from Heaven and Earth!

By this day, he no longer needed to rely on sabre intent to use this spell.

Under the guidance of the secret art of One Blade from Heaven and Earth, all these powers collapsed into the black hole formed in his dantian.

Sarun Agu's face changed slightly. Without a word, he swept his sleeve, mounted a streak of black light, and fled into the distance.

The Buddhist Bodhisattvas, the beyond-mortal experts of the Great Feng, and the leaders of the shaman clans all abandoned their fighting and retreated one after another.

During this retreat, they looked back and saw the newly advanced half-step Martial God throw a punch at the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect.

In the next moment, they lost their sight.

The intense light scorched their eyeballs. Every person who dared turn back shed two streams of bloody tears from their eyes.

Boom!

Vast qi and golden Buddhist light formed an enormous mushroom cloud, billowing up more than a thousand metres into the sky.

The "mud" formed by the Buddha's body was scraped away layer by layer, annihilated chunk by chunk. No living being remained within several miles, only Shenshu and Xu Qi’an, the two half-step Martial Gods.

The Buddha image standing with eight great Dharma Aspects behind it had used the Walker Dharma Aspect to evade to a distant place the instant the Great Sun Tathagata exploded.

After an unknown span of time, everything finally calmed. All that remained was the ruined earth and two crimson skeletons.

"It really hurts..."

Xu Qi’an transmitted with divine sense.

...

"Stronger than Shenshu..."

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva said in a low voice.

The power Xu Qi’an had just unleashed had already surpassed Shenshu.

Vaidurya Bodhisattva and Agaruka Bodhisattva said nothing. Silent and grim, they now felt just as heavy-hearted as Daoist Jinlian and the others had felt moments before.

At this point, the Buddha's plan to seize the initiative by devouring the Central Plains ahead of time had completely failed.

"Ah, this is troublesome."

Sarun Agu sighed. He glanced at the two Masters of Wisdom beside him. Their faces were pale and their eyes full of fear.

By contrast, the beyond-mortal experts of the Great Feng led by Luo Yuheng, as well as the leaders of the shaman clans, were both shocked and overjoyed.

Clearly, the battle strength Xu Qi’an had unleashed had completely exceeded their expectations and filled them with powerful confidence.

Xu Qi’an spat out the Earth Book fragment. His skeletal hand gripped the Sovereign Sword as he said:

"Master, help me withstand the effects of those Dharma Aspects."

The half-skeletal Shenshu said nothing and charged at the Buddha first. Taking advantage of his effective restraint of the Buddha, Xu Qi’an collapsed his qi and emotions, fused them with the power of all living beings, and unleashed Broken Jade.

Across the land stretching a thousand miles, a chasm over a thousand feet long split open. The fleshly matter along the edges was charred black and dried hard, completely stripped of life.

But to the Buddha, such injuries meant nothing at all.

A sword wound simultaneously split open across Xu Qi’an's chest, then promptly healed.

*You can't kill a Beyond-rank. Ordinary methods can't threaten a Beyond-rank at all...* Xu Qi’an was not surprised. Rather, he thought this was only natural.

Why had the Confucian Sage only sealed the Buddha and the Warlock God, but not killed them?

It wasn't that he hadn't wanted to, but that he had had no way!

"If you want to kill a Beyond-rank, you need special means."

At that moment, Shenshu spoke.

"Special means?" Xu Qi’an asked humbly.

"I don't know," Shenshu said flatly. "But I know how to force Him back."

He said no more and demonstrated in person.

A vast and terrifying power surged out of Shenshu's body like floodwaters bursting through a broken dam.

In an instant, the elemental forces of heaven and earth fell into chaos. Black clouds pressed down overhead, lightning flashed and thunder rolled, yet what descended was not rain, but flames.

The earth took on a metallic sheen as earth-spirit and metal-spirit fused in disorder.

The whole land shook. Strange phenomena filled the skies, and for a moment it was impossible to tell whether heaven and earth were rejecting the martial artist, or the martial artist was rejecting heaven and earth.

This was the manifestation of a martial artist becoming a world unto himself.

When Xu Qi’an had first entered first rank, he too had triggered heavenly phenomena and drawn a lightning tribulation.

But it had been nowhere near as exaggerated as Shenshu's.

*Expand the domain of a half-step Martial God, forming a region of disorder to resist the Buddha's devouring of this world...* Watching the layers of fleshly matter recede, Xu Qi’an's heart stirred and he understood what Shenshu meant.

He immediately followed Shenshu's example, expanding his own domain and summoning heavenly phenomena.

At once, the fleshly matter surged like an enraged ocean, raising layer upon layer of waves that crashed, pounded, and stormed into the vast domain upheld by the two half-step Martial Gods.

Then it was scorched by that terrifying power into dry, hardened "soil", all life within cut off.

The two sides remained deadlocked for a moment before the waves of dark red fleshly matter retreated.

The Buddha gave up on devouring Leizhou and refining the Mountain and River Seal.

If there had only been one half-step Martial God, He could have worn the opponent down through slow, constant "grinding", like water dripping through stone, until the other side was exhausted.

But against two half-step Martial Gods, the one who would be worn down in the end would only be Him.

"Let's catch up and have a look," Xu Qi’an said, his jaws opening and closing.

Shenshu nodded his skull.

The two half-step Martial Gods vanished instantly.

...

"Go!"

Vaidurya Bodhisattva placed a hand on each of the two Bodhisattvas' shoulders and vanished with them on the spot.

Sarun Agu drew out the God-beating Whip, wrapped up Irbu and Uda Batu with it, and turned into a streak of black light fleeing into the distance.

Al-Asura and the nine-tailed fox bore a deep blood feud with the Buddhist sect and were unwilling to leave it at that, so they gave chase westward.

"It's over!"

Yang Gong let out a sigh of relief, feeling utterly exhausted in both body and mind.

Yet there was an excitement in his expression that he could not suppress. Successfully driving back the Buddha meant that, in the face of the great calamity, the Great Feng had now gained the initial ability to protect itself.

Sun Xuanji exhaled deeply.

Daoist Jinlian shook his head.

"Let's follow and have a look. Xu Qi’an and Shenshu seem to want to make a trip to the Western Regions."

The Western Regions were the Buddha's territory. His display in Leizhou certainly couldn't compare to what He could do in the Western Regions.

If a real fight broke out, and they hurried over now, they might still be able to offer some help.

Arhat Duqing pressed his palms together.

"The Western Regions are dangerous. It's best to observe from afar and not step into the territory of the Western Regions."

Luo Yuheng had originally wanted to pursue the Church of the Warlock God, but on hearing this, she had no choice but to give up the thought and head for the Western Regions together with Daoist Jinlian and the others.

...

At this moment, Li Miaozhen was still waiting for Xu Qi’an between sea and sky.

"That son of a bitch, why's he so slow?!"

Lady Flying Swallow thought anxiously.
