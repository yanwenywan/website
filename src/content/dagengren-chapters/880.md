# The Birth of a Half-step Martial God

The twelve pairs of arms of the pitch-black Dharma Aspect held aloft the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect, like a giant from myth lifting up the sun.

It was a scene of overwhelming visual impact.

Yet even stronger than the spectacle was the force unleashed by their clash. The twelve pairs of arms surged with terrifying monstrous strength, crushing the golden sun, trying to squeeze it apart and snuff it out.

Meanwhile, the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect continuously radiated a power that purified all things, seeking to evaporate and cleanse the half-step Martial God from flesh to soul.

The martial artist's qi and the Buddha light of the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect intertwined and tangled, turning into a storm that destroyed everything as it ravaged in all directions.

The dark red flesh-like matter covering the ground like mud was scraped away layer by layer. Within a hundred-metre radius beneath Shenshu's feet, not even half an inch of flesh matter remained.

At this moment, even the Buddha's "flesh body" could no longer approach Shenshu.

"Clang, clang, clang..."

Daoist Jinlian struck the bronze bell with all his strength. His Daoist robe flapped fiercely, his hairpin had fallen away, and his white hair and beard streamed in the wind.

Yang Gong, wearing the Confucian cap of the Lesser Sage, used magic to amplify the power of the bell's toll.

The bell was only providing support. The one truly resisting the control of the Buddha Dharma Aspect was still Shenshu's own formidable primordial spirit.

While closely observing the battlefield, Chu Yuanzhen took out his Earth Book fragment and sent a message:

【FOUR: Ziyang Jushi came at just the right time and solved our urgent crisis. It's just that Master Shenshu may not necessarily be a match for the Buddha.】

He put it rather tactfully.

Anyone could see that even Shenshu, already at his peak condition, was still somewhat inferior to the Buddha.

Moreover, if this battle had taken place on the vast lands of the Western Regions, Shenshu might already have been defeated.

In the imperial palace, Huaiqing looked at this message and pressed her brow with a headache. She did not reply to Chu Yuanzhen, because she did not know how to answer.

"Duke Wei, Lord Zhao, Lord Wang, what good strategies do the three of you have for today's upheaval?" Huaiqing asked in a heavy tone.

This time, even the resourceful Wei Yuan had no solution.

He pondered for a moment, then replied:

"Do all we can, and leave the rest to fate."

Wang Zhenwen added details:

"Send orders at once. Have the Leizhou Provincial Administration Commissioner call upon the officials of every prefecture and county to move the people of Leizhou eastward. Move as many as can be moved.

"If Shenshu is defeated, then we'll watch and wait, see how the Buddha intends to nibble away at the Central Plains, and at what pace. We can discuss countermeasures then.

"Also, send someone overseas to bring back Silver Gong Xu. The Great Feng urgently needs his fighting strength now."

Zhao Shou sighed:

"Without becoming a half-step Martial God, I'm afraid it will be hard to stop the Buddha. But given the present situation, we can only take things one step at a time. There's nothing else to be done."

Huaiqing gave a quiet "Mm", then swiftly sent a message:

【ONE: We will immediately order the Leizhou Provincial Administration Commission to evacuate the people of Leizhou. We ask all of you to do your utmost to hold the Buddha back and buy time. Daoist Lanlian, you're fast, please go out to sea at once and look for Xu Qi’an.】

Xu Qi’an was overseas, too far away to be contacted with the Earth Book fragment.

What Li Miaozhen had to do was not truly find Xu Qi’an in the vast ocean, but to bring his Earth Book fragment back within transmission range and inform him of the changes in Jiuzhou.

【TWO: Understood.】

Li Miaozhen knew why Huaiqing had chosen her to go overseas. Her path overlapped with Daoist Jinlian's, so whether she stayed or went made little difference.

As for Arhat Du'e, Master Hengyuan, and even al-Asura, though their paths also overlapped with others, Arhat Du'e was currently a prized target. It was too dangerous for him to act alone, and he did not have an Earth Book fragment.

Master Hengyuan was, in essence, only fourth rank. He could only briefly erupt with the power of the Executioner Phala. Sending a fourth rank overseas would be too much to ask of him.

Al-Asura was the peak among peak second ranks, an important combat force who could not be spared.

Thus Li Miaozhen, adept at sword flight and swift as the wind, became the best choice.

Lady Flying Swallow had a strong sense of the bigger picture. She immediately accepted the task, and without even saying goodbye to her companions, she mounted her sword and shot southwards as a streak of light.

Bang!

Amid a tremendous explosion, the bronze bell before Daoist Jinlian shattered into powder.

Li Miaozhen abruptly turned back and saw the surrounding scenery drain of colour and turn black and white. She saw Vaidurya Bodhisattva, who had smashed the bronze bell, appear before Daoist Jinlian and swing the small jade knife in her hand, severing the Daoist's head.

She saw Agaruka Bodhisattva appear within the colourless barrier and effortlessly seize Arhat Du'e, who could barely move.

She saw Samantabhadra Bodhisattva, in the form of a young monk, standing in the distance and watching this with a smile.

The three Bodhisattvas had made their move.

The Walker Dharma Aspect came and went without a trace, and by attacking by surprise, they caught everyone off guard.

Moreover, they had quite rationally avoided attacking al-Asura, Chu Yuanzhen, and Hengyuan.

Because all three possessed a martial artist's danger sense.

How could two second ranks without danger sense evade an attack from a first rank Bodhisattva?

*No...* Li Miaozhen's pupils contracted violently, and the streak of light formed by her flying sword suddenly halted.

...

Overseas.

A gigantic monster floated on the sea surface, rising and falling with the waves.

It looked not unlike an octopus, except that its whole body was covered in blue-black scales, and the back of its head bore layer upon layer of keratin armour like a tortoise shell, clearly a material of tremendous defensive power.

Endless years had passed, yet this flesh body had still not rotted and remained full of vigorous life.

After reaching a certain level, the flesh body and primordial spirit were in fact separate matters. The primordial spirit might perish, but the vitality of the flesh body would not vanish with it.

Take a third rank martial artist, for instance. Even if his soul were scattered, his body would still retain vigorous life force. Only after several decades would it gradually weaken, and it would take a full century for that vitality to be completely lost.

As for a half-step Martial God, it was basically undying and imperishable. Even Beyond-rank would find it hard to kill.

Even if one day its life force really were ground away, the flesh body and primordial spirit would rot together. It would not leave behind a body brimming with vitality, because the essence, qi, and spirit of a peak martial artist had long since become one.

Fortunately, although this ancient god-demon controlled "strength", it was not a martial artist.

Otherwise, Xu Qi’an would never have got off so cheaply today.

After bringing the great octopus body to the surface, Xu Qi’an wasted no time. His Adam's apple bobbed, and he spat out the little jade mirror.

Ding!

His fingertip lightly tapped the back of the Earth Book, and one octagonal bronze disc after another, shrouded in hazy clear light, flew out in succession and hovered in the air.

There were exactly one hundred and eight bronze discs. Each octagonal disc was as large as a round table, and each was engraved with chaotic formation patterns. Some resembled tadpoles, some were crisscrossing lines, some looked like simple sketches of flames and waves...

Yet these completely unrelated formation patterns were arranged in a marvellous rhythm, containing some law of heaven and earth.

*So this is the formation disc capable of refining the essence of a first rank expert...* The nine-tailed fox in the sky above widened her beautiful eyes, trying to memorise the formation patterns on the octagonal bronze discs.

"If you want them, once I'm done, I'll give you the discs," Xu Qi’an said with a smile, raising his eyes while sitting cross-legged on the sea.

"We're all on the same side, no need to be polite."

"All right, all right..." The nine-tailed fox was certainly not some reserved and restrained young lady.

Xu Qi’an said no more. Remaining seated cross-legged, he slowly rose into the air.

The one hundred and eight bronze discs spread apart and fell into different positions, hovering above the great octopus and below Xu Qi’an.

The nine-tailed fox could sense that the positions of the discs had been placed with great care, secretly matching the mysteries of yin-yang and the five elements. The one hundred and eight discs formed an enormous Bagua covering a vast area.

Buzz...

The one hundred and eight bronze discs rotated clockwise, sending out circle after circle of rippling clear light.

At first there was no sign of anything unusual, but a quarter of an hour later, the nine-tailed fox saw strands of scarlet broken light slowly drift out from the great octopus's body. On closer inspection, the broken light was made up of twisted patterns.

They were extremely similar to the patterns branded upon the octopus's tentacles.

This was the spiritual essence of the ancient god-demon.

The spiritual essence was stripped out and gathered towards the bronze discs. After being filtered through the discs, these tiny strands of broken light continued rising and poured into Xu Qi’an's body.

One twisted pattern after another appeared on Xu Qi’an's skin. They were the spiritual essence of the great octopus.

The power of gods and demons came from spiritual essence. The remnant spiritual essence within this ancient god-demon's body was now being transferred little by little into Xu Qi’an.

Plundered by the Great Feng's first rank martial artist.

The one hundred and eight bronze discs continuously stripped away the great octopus's spiritual essence.

Xu Qi’an's aura slowly rose, while the remnant body of the ancient god-demon withered little by little.

...

A mouth split open on Shenshu's back and gently breathed out a strand of qi.

*Crack...* Vaidurya Bodhisattva's colourless domain shattered at once.

At the critical moment, Shenshu had no choice but to intervene, though all he could do was exhale qi and break the fixing domain.

"So you really have come."

Al-Asura drew a deep breath. The gaze beneath his brow bone sharpened as he swept his eyes over the three Bodhisattvas.

"Perhaps we should've let you ants show off a little longer?"

"Go, now!"

He did not forget to transmit a warning to Li Miaozhen.

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva smiled and asked in return.

Yang Gong and the others wore grave expressions and slowly moved closer together.

From past experience, three second ranks of different paths could join forces to hold back a first rank Bodhisattva of Buddhism.

Now that all three Bodhisattvas had gathered, they had no chance of victory at all.

On the other side, Sarun Agu suddenly drew out his shepherd's whip and said with a genial smile:

"Just watch the show. Why interfere in the matter between the Great Feng and Buddhism?

"The three Bodhisattvas joining forces isn't something you can contend with. Chiefs, you ought to treasure your lives."

He was warning the leaders of the shaman clans who wanted to step in and help.

Several of the chiefs ground their teeth in rage.

Heaven Gu Grandma glanced at Sarun Agu and said blandly:

"The Great Warlock speaks truly. Since the Great Warlock wants us to watch the show, then we'll watch."

The peerlessly beautiful Vaidurya Bodhisattva suddenly frowned and looked at the head in her hand. It dispersed into a streak of golden light.

Then soft Buddha light lit up Daoist Jinlian's body, and bathed in that Buddha light, a brand new head grew out from his neck.

"The Non-living Phala!"

Vaidurya Bodhisattva raised her delicate brows, and a rare chill entered her voice:

"You killed Arhat Duqing. The Jianzheng promised only to imprison him for three years, not to take his life."

Daoist Jinlian smiled and said:

"What has the Jianzheng's promise got to do with this poor Daoist?

"When the nation is broken and homes are destroyed, what does personal credibility amount to?"

Among the beyond-mortal main fighting forces of the Great Feng, al-Asura and Kou Yangzhou were not easily killed because of their paths.

Zhao Shou had his carving knife and Confucian cap to preserve his life.

Only he, the grand Daoist Leader of the Earth Sect, had no life-saving means at all.

He relied solely on the Heavenly Dao backlash of dragging the enemy down with him to deter foes. Killing one with deep fortune would bring heavenly tribulation.

Arhat Duqing's Non-living Phala had been something Daoist Jinlian kept to save his own life, just in case.

As it turned out, he really did need it.

On the other side, the Arhat Du'e held in Agaruka Bodhisattva's hand swiftly turned insubstantial and vanished in an instant.

It was only a false body, a false body summoned by the Worthy One Phala.

Arhat Du'e appeared at al-Asura's side. Right now, only al-Asura, that rough Asura Warrior Monk, could give Arhat Du'e even the slightest sense of safety.

"Du'e!"

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva shook his head in disappointment and sighed:

"Buddhism treated you well."

Arhat Du'e put his palms together and said indifferently:

"To us, you are merely heretics."

Vaidurya Bodhisattva's voice turned icy, already overflowing with killing intent:

"Why waste words? Crushing a few ants takes no effort at all."

She lifted a foot, about to bring it down.

Yang Gong cried out urgently:

"Withdraw three three thousand feet."

Vaidurya Bodhisattva's raised foot paused for a moment, but she still stepped down.

The surrounding scenery instantly lost its colours and turned black and white.

Yang Gong's spell had not been useless, but to the Walker Dharma Aspect, a distance of three three thousand feet could be crossed back and forth in an instant.

Outsiders would not even be able to tell that she had ever left.

Shenshu, locked in bitter battle, split open a mouth on his back once more and blew out another strand of qi.

This time, the Buddha did not let him have his way. The Immovable Lord Dharma Aspect formed seals with both hands and sealed off that battlefield, making the qi crash into a spatial barrier.

The colourless domain once more enveloped al-Asura and the others.

Just then, a streak of light rose in the east. When it first appeared, it was still like a star hanging in the night sky. In the next instant, it descended like a meteor and directly tore through the colourless glazed domain.

Sword qi filled heaven and earth!

"An Earthly Immortal has come. Now things are getting lively," Sarun Agu said with a smile.

...

Overseas.

The remnant body of the ancient god-demon had already shrunk to less than half its original size.

By contrast, Xu Qi’an now appeared as a thousand-foot-tall giant, his muscles swollen all over his body.

Twisted patterns were imprinted across his surface like tattoos.

The nine-tailed fox closed her eyes, not daring to keep looking at those spiritual essence patterns that made her dizzy and dazed.

In her eyes, Xu Qi’an at this moment was the very symbol of strength, the embodiment of "strength" itself.

Any characteristic or concept you could imagine about "strength" could be found in him.

Xu Qi’an kept his eyes shut, carefully sensing the changes in his body.

After seizing the spiritual essence of the ancient god-demon, his cultivation, already on the verge of entering the middle stage, smoothly broke through that final barrier and entered first rank middle stage, then pressed on through middle stage and entered the late stage. And even that was not the end.

His power continued to soar, continuing to approach great completion.

But it stopped at the final step before becoming a half-step Martial God.

Because the essence, qi, and spirit within him had fallen out of balance.

The word "balance" was the secret of a first rank martial artist, the very foundation.

When he devoured the spiritual essence of the ancient god-demon and his qi and blood surged by leaps and bounds, his primordial spirit had not been tempered along with them.

The consequence of imbalance was qi deviation.

He would remain at his current state, but lose the possibility of advancing to half-step Martial God.

At present there were two methods. The first was to seal away part of his qi and blood and wait until his primordial spirit improved in future, then fuse them.

The second was to dilute the primordial spirit and merge it into the excessively powerful essence and qi.

The latter was a forced breakthrough, the equivalent of walking a tightrope, and carried the risk of failure and qi deviation.

"I suddenly understand the true meaning of what Shenshu said back in the Refining Spirit stage."

Back when he had died in Yunzhou and spoken with Shenshu deep within his sea of consciousness, Shenshu had said:

For ordinary martial artists, refining the spirit only means they have initially found the limit. That is the lower grade. To keep breaking through the limit in desperate situations, that is the higher grade.

What Xu Qi’an had achieved in the Refining Spirit stage was the higher grade.

Impressive, wasn't it? But it was bought with his own life.

Among the nine ranks of the martial path, only the Refining Spirit stage primarily cultivated the primordial spirit.

At this stage, Xu Qi’an had built an incomparably solid foundation, firmer and more secure than other martial artists.

In other words, the toughness of his primordial spirit was stronger than that of other martial artists.

So he chose the second path, diluting his primordial spirit into his essence and qi and forcefully making the breakthrough.

His primordial spirit tore into countless fragments and merged with the vast essence and qi. If his former primordial spirit had been a complete piece of cloth, now it was a fishing net.

Its size had grown, but it was full of gaps.

The moment one of the threads snapped, his primordial spirit would collapse, and he would become a madman with a shattered mind.

Unfortunately, the "threads" did indeed begin to snap. Advancing to half-step Martial God was no easy matter.

Xu Qi’an first clearly felt the pain of his soul being torn apart, then his consciousness began to grow chaotic. As more and more threads broke, that chaos intensified.

He began to forget who he was, to forget his former identities, to forget the people around him.

Before long, Xu Qi’an would completely lose himself and become a lunatic bereft of reason.

At that moment, he heard cries coming from afar:

"Silver Gong Xu, Silver Gong Xu..."

Countless people were calling out, and countless voices merged into the three words "Silver Gong Xu".

At the same time, another voice also echoed in his mind:

"Supreme Buddha of the three thousand worlds, Supreme Buddha of the three thousand worlds..."

The two cries helped Xu Qi’an initially recover his sense of self. He quickly gathered his scattered spirit and repaired the snapped threads one by one.

...

High in the sky, the nine-tailed fox shivered all over. Her eight furry tails wrapped tightly around her body.

Curled up in mid-air, she looked like a frail and pitiful fox.

Great waves surged over the sea, the whole earth shook, and thunder intertwined across the sky, sending down bolt after bolt of heavenly tribulation.

Amid this apocalyptic scene, Xu Qi’an opened his eyes. His gaze was bright as pillars of light, instantly piercing through the dark clouds and shooting into the vault of heaven.

A half-step Martial God had been born.
