# 870. The Secret of the Great Calamity (II)

"Official Sir!"

Zhulai bent and bowed with humble deference, moving closer and putting on an obedient manner as he explained:

"We're going to Banyue City on business. May we ask what the matter is?"

One of the guards, holding a long spear, asked coldly:

"Where's your travel permit? Take it out!"

*Travel permit...* Zhulai froze for a moment. Banyue City was only forty li from here, and people travelling back and forth didn't need a travel permit. Only journeys of over a hundred li required travellers and merchant caravans to show one.

And travel permits were issued by the City Lord's residence.

Zhulai's caravan was disguised. There was no way they could get a travel permit issued by the City Lord's residence.

Seeing Zhulai remain silent, the guard blocking the road sharpened his gaze, while the other three guards beside him looked over coldly.

Zhulai's heart tightened. He turned back to glance at his fellow disciples, whose faces were showing tension, then smiled humbly and said:

"I--I've never heard of such a thing... Why does going to Banyue City also require a travel permit?"

He had been born a beggar. Bowing and scraping came naturally to him, and currying favour in a lowly manner was practically an occupational skill.

The guard with the spear was very satisfied with Zhulai's attitude and explained:

"Those are the orders from above. Don't ask why."

Because I don't know either.

That answer instead made Zhulai breathe a sigh of relief, because what he had just been thinking was, *could it be that there's a traitor among us?*

Someone had reported the Mahayana Buddhist migration to the City Lord's residence.

After all, there were many Mahayana Buddhists. There would always be some unwilling to move east. They themselves didn't want to leave the Western Regions, yet also didn't want to see others leave. With a twisted mindset, it wasn't impossible for them to report everyone.

Although the higher-ups of Mahayana Buddhism had promised that believers who remained in the Western Regions would not be abandoned, and had encouraged them to continue preaching there.

That had soothed the believers to some extent.

But Zhulai had grown up as a beggar and had seen too much of the ugliness of human nature. His first suspicion had been that his sect had suffered betrayal.

Fortunately, it seemed to be something else?

"Officer, could you do us a favour?" Zhulai took a silver ingot from his robe and quietly slipped it into the guard's hand.

"You bastard!"

The guard flew into a rage and rebuked him:

"We're warriors loyal to the City Lord. The orders of the City Lord's residence are our faith."

Zhulai thought for a moment, painfully took out three more silver ingots, and said with utmost respect:

"Officer, please see..."

The guard considered it, then said in a deep voice:

"The Buddha once said that Buddha wouldn't let those who believe in Him fall into hardship. I suppose the City Lord is the same."

He looked at the three guards behind him and asked:

"Wouldn't you agree?"

The three guards nodded with solemn expressions.

After smoothing things over, Zhulai let out a sigh of relief and turned to order:

"Move out!"

The caravan was finally able to leave the city smoothly, advancing slowly along the potholed official road outside the walls.

The sunlight shone on them, and every face seemed to glow with radiance, as though they were heading towards a new life.

The Western Regions forbade Mahayana Buddhism, so they would go to the Central Plains, to the fertile lands of the Central Plains, and spread their faith there.

At the same time, across the major city-states and kingdoms of the Western Regions, similar groups were leaving the homes where they had lived since childhood, heading into the wilderness, heading towards faith.

They were like countless little streams, converging towards the sea.

...

Ananda.

Vaidurya Bodhisattva stood on the mountain peak, looking down at the dense flow of people at the foot of the mountain. More or less every person of status and position in the Western Regions had come.

On the plain beneath the sacred mountain, rows upon rows of clearly separated tents had been pitched.

"After the Battle of Shanhai Pass ended, the fortune of our Buddhist sect became like oil poured upon blazing fire. If He can merge with Shenshu, Buddha will become the strongest Beyond-rank in Jiuzhou."

A voice from behind carried tones that made age and gender impossible to distinguish.

The young monk, wearing kasaya robes and holding his palms together, strolled over.

"Even without merging with Shenshu, Buddha is still the strongest Beyond-rank. As long as the fortune of the Western Regions is not damaged. If you ask me, Buddha suppressing Shenshu's head and thus sleeping for five hundred years instead caused Him to miss many opportunities."

Vaidurya's beautiful eyes were ethereal, her lips full and sensuous. Her skin was fair and delicate, completely unlike the rough-skinned people of the Western Regions.

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva sighed:

"That was a half-step Martial God."

Why had Buddha created the dual cultivation system of Zen and martial arts?

To become Beyond-rank first, then become a half-step Martial God. That would make Him truly invincible within heaven and earth.

But then a Confucian Sage had appeared among the human race of the Central Plains and sealed all the Beyond-ranks in Jiuzhou, utterly throwing Buddha's plans into disarray and forcing Him to seek another path.

That was how Shenshu, an uncontrollable "avatar", had been born. After that, in order to bring the plan back on track, Buddha had dismembered and sealed Shenshu, originally intending to first digest Shenshu's head...

"A half-step Martial God is deathless and indestructible. Even a Beyond-rank can only seal one, and can hardly kill one." Samantabhadra Bodhisattva sighed again.

After a pause, he stood shoulder to shoulder with the peerlessly beautiful Vaidurya and looked down at the vast multitude of believers on the plain. The corners of his mouth lifted.

"No matter what, we're still one step ahead of the Gu God and the Warlock God.

"Oh, by the way, I heard Xu Qi'an has gone to sea?"

Vaidurya Bodhisattva gave a slight nod. "Since he failed to kill Agaruka, going out to sea is the only chance left to him. A pity that ancient god-demon had long since beaten him to it and devoured the descendants of the overseas gods and demons.

"This journey is destined to be futile."

On hearing this, Samantabhadra looked east and said with a smile:

"After the Buddhist Dharma Assembly, we'll swallow the Great Feng in one gulp."

As his words fell, the clear and lofty ringing of bells arose in Ananda, peal after peal echoing between heaven and earth.

On the plain at the foot of Ananda, the believers emerged from their tents one after another and looked towards the sacred mountain. Then, as if by prior agreement, they all sat down cross-legged where they stood.

Men and women, old and young alike, all sat cross-legged on the ground, palms pressed together, their faces filled with piety.

"Clang!"

The ethereal and resonant bell tones echoed in the hearts of the believers and rang by the ears of every hearer, as if they could cleanse their souls and bring them the truest stirring of life.

And the most devout faith in Buddha.

Samantabhadra and Vaidurya said no more and each sat down cross-legged.

In the direction of the main hall, Agaruka sat cross-legged in the square. His voice, accompanied by the bell tones, rang in the ears of every believer:

"Since the opening of heaven and earth, seven hundred and twenty thousand three hundred and sixty-eight years have passed, and no one has become Buddha. In the three thousand four hundred and ninety-one years since Buddha, still no one has become Buddha. Buddha is the only one, the one true Buddha of the three thousand worlds..."

The believers listened in silence, praying devoutly.

Gradually, in their ears, Agaruka's voice turned into the faint sound of chanting.

At first, they simply listened in silence. Later, before they even realised it, they had begun chanting along with the recitation.

And so, grand sanskrit song resounded between heaven and earth.

The Buddhist chant rang on incessant, carrying an inexplicable power that tugged at the hearts of every believer in the Western Regions, tugged at the hearts of this group that symbolised the upper strata of Western Regions society.

No one noticed that the grand and towering Ananda seemed to have come alive, as if life had been bestowed upon it like some living creature.

...

"The Dharma doesn't pass to six ears. Please wait a moment!"

Xu Qi'an turned his head and swept a glance over the tall and burly dragon-man with frost at his mane, and Queen Zhenzhu beside him, delicate and beautiful.

"You two, leave this place first."

He sent the words by divine sense, pouring his thoughts directly into the consciousness seas of the two descendants of gods and demons.

Island Lord Nulang and the mermaid queen exchanged a glance. They asked no questions, obediently leapt into the sea, and with two splashes disappeared into the waves.

Xu Qi'an then looked towards the Nine-tailed Fox.

Her fine brows stood on end as she glared at him fiercely.

So Xu Qi'an said:

"Your Majesty can stay. Jianzheng, she's one of us."

The Jianzheng suddenly understood.

"Oh, so she's already become your confidante?"

*Do you really think I'm some lustful philanderer? I'm talking about camaraderie, camaraderie forged through life and death...* Xu Qi'an felt that the Jianzheng must have some misunderstanding about him.

The Nine-tailed Fox turned her head away and spat.

Once Island Lord Nulang and the mermaid queen had swum far away, Xu Qi'an raised a qi barrier to cut off the spread of sound.

Then he and the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox sat side by side at the bow, looking at the Jianzheng.

The Jianzheng didn't look at them. He beckoned, drawing the Taiping Sabre into his hand, and while examining it, said:

"It'll still take some time to digest that gate."

Xu Qi'an took the chance to voice the doubt in his heart.

"Why was what I saw a sabre?"

The Jianzheng chuckled.

"Because you're a martial artist."

*What's wrong with martial artists? Did martial artists eat your family's rice?!* Xu Qi'an said irritably:

"Stop being cryptic. Say it clearly."

After snapping back at the Jianzheng, inspiration surged through his mind.

"Is this the reason martial artists are different from all the other systems?"

The Jianzheng smiled and nodded.

"One of the reasons. In a moment, I'll explain it all to you plainly."

Xu Qi'an continued:

"That sabre, the one you said is the gate, in my perception it's certainly marvellous, but not enough to drive gods and demons mad, is it? Also, after merging with it, what changes will happen to the Taiping Sabre?"

The Jianzheng stroked the Taiping Sabre and said:

"Only with this sabre do you have the qualifications to become the gatekeeper. It's the gatekeeper's weapon.

"You're right. It has indeed lost its most essential ability for now, because the second great calamity is different from the first."

*So it really is like that, I guessed right...* Xu Qi'an was just about to ask more when the Nine-tailed Fox beside him smacked him with her tail and said angrily:

"Too much nonsense."

She didn't want to hear Xu Qi'an's nonsense. She only wanted to hear the Jianzheng speak of the secrets of the great calamity.

Xu Qi'an immediately fell silent.

The Jianzheng sat cross-legged, the Taiping Sabre laid across his knees, and slowly said:

"Before speaking of the truth of the great calamity, I want to ask you a question.

"What do you think heaven is, and what is earth? What is heaven like, and what is earth like?"

*Starting philosophy the moment he opens his mouth. I only know what filial piety is and what love is...* Xu Qi'an turned his head and looked at the Nine-tailed Fox.

"The Jianzheng's testing you!"
