# 884. Secret Discussion

Xu Qi’an smiled and said:

"Your Majesty, Your servant was fortunate not to fail in my mission!

"After many twists and turns, untold hardship, and surviving by the skin of my teeth, your servant has advanced to half-step Martial God.

"Leizhou has been preserved for the time being, and the Buddha has retreated to the Western Regions."

The nine-tailed fox at the side rolled her eyes.

*Half-step Martial God, he'd really advanced to half-step Martial God...* Huaiqing got the answer she wanted, and the heart lodged in her throat at once settled back down, but her joy and excitement didn't lessen. Instead, they surged up all the more fiercely.

A flush rose to her cheeks, delight shimmered in her eyes, and no matter what, she couldn't suppress the smile tugging at her lips.

Sure enough, he'd never let her down, whether he had been the Bronze Gong of the past or the now world-renowned Silver Gong Xu.

Huaiqing had always held the highest expectations of him, yet time and again he still exceeded them and brought her fresh surprises.

*Ningyan's advanced to half-step Martial God, and with Shenshu, that veteran half-step Martial God, they finally have enough confidence to contend with either the Church of the Warlock God or the Buddhist sect. This game can still be played. Ah, that hot-headed lad from back then is now already a half-step Martial God. It feels like another lifetime...* While Wei Yuan felt as though a heavy burden had been lifted, his emotions were complicated, tinged with wistfulness, gratification, satisfaction, and pride.

Taking into account his status, and the fact that the imperial study was full of experts, Wei Yuan maintained the calm and composure befitting his position, and said unhurriedly:

"Well done."

*Half-step Martial God. If I remember right, he should be the first half-step Martial God among the Central Plains human race, as unique as the Confucian Sage. He must have a line in the history books: Silver Gong Xu studied from childhood at Cloud Deer Academy and took the dean Zhao Shou as his teacher...* At this thought, Zhao Shou grew excited. Just as he was about to step forward and offer congratulations, intending already to embellish the future histories, he caught sight of Wei Yuan's calm and unruffled expression. So he too had no choice but to preserve the calm and composure suitable to his station, and slowly said:

"Very good!"

*The Great Feng is saved. Another escape from death. Xu Qi’an's successfully become a half-step Martial God. My judgement wasn't wrong. Eh, these two old fellows are awfully calm...* Wang Zhenwen felt as though he'd returned to the year he passed the imperial examinations with top honours, when he had wanted nothing more than to sing aloud and drink himself senseless all night.

But seeing that both Zhao Shou and Wei Yuan wore placid expressions, he too maintained the calm proper to his status and nodded slowly:

"Congratulations on your advancement!"

*As expected of the titans who've weathered the officialdom for years, not a trace of joy or anger shows on their faces...* Xu Qi’an secretly marvelled, then said:

"It's a pity I've no clue how to advance to Martial God."

Meals had to be eaten one mouthful at a time. Wei Yuan nearly blurted out advice on how to conduct himself, but then remembered that his former subordinate was now a true great figure, no longer in need of constant instruction, so he held it back.

Instead he asked:

"How's the situation in Leizhou? How many died?"

As the beyond-mortals pondered in silence, Arhat Du'e said:

"Only one large town was destroyed, a little over two thousand people."

Daoist Jinlian and Hengyuan opened their mouths, but were half a beat too slow.

*From that small detail, it's plain that Arhat Du'e cares most about the common people. He's truly been brainwashed--no, baptised by Mahayana Buddhism...* Xu Qi’an judged inwardly.

Huaiqing's expression turned rather grave as she nodded and looked at Xu Qi’an.

"While you were away overseas, the Buddhist sect held a Dharma Assembly. According to Arhat Du'e, it was precisely by means of that assembly that the Buddha underwent a dreadful mutation.

"We don't know the exact cause, but you presumably already know the result. He turned into a monster that devours everything."

She took the initiative to explain the course of this disaster and fill Xu Qi’an in.

Daoist Jinlian continued:

"When Arhat Du'e left the Western Regions, the Buddha didn't harm him. But once Mahayana Buddhism was established and the Buddhist sect's fortune began to drain away, the Buddha immediately couldn't wait to devour him.

"Clearly, the Buddha's mutation is connected to fortune. This is very likely the so-called great calamity."

Wei Yuan sighed.

"From the Buddha's behaviour, we can infer what the Gu God and the Warlock God will be like after breaking free of their seals.

"It's only that we still don't know what meaning there is in what these Beyond-rank beings are doing, nor what their purpose is."

The beyond-mortals furrowed their brows and remained silent. They vaguely felt they were already close to the truth, yet couldn't quite pierce through it and explain it clearly.

It was like a final layer of paper over the window that simply couldn't be poked through.

*Isn't it just to replace the Heavenly Dao...* The nine-tailed fox was just about to speak when she heard Xu Qi’an beat her to it with a long sigh.

"I already know the truth of the great calamity."

Inside the imperial study, everyone turned to him in astonishment.

"You know?"

Al-Asura scrutinised the half-step Martial God, finding it hard to believe how someone who'd been at sea for months could know the secret of the great calamity.

Daoist Jinlian and Wei Yuan both felt their hearts stir.

Seeing Xu Qi’an nod, Yang Gong, Sun Xuanji, and the others were visibly moved.

*This starts from the creation of heaven and earth...* Under everyone's eager and expectant gaze, Xu Qi’an said:

"I know everything, including the first great calamity, when the gods and demons perished."

*At last, the truth behind the fall of the gods and demons will be revealed...* Everyone's spirits lifted as they listened intently.

Xu Qi’an slowly said:

"This still has to begin with the birth of the gods and demons when heaven and earth first opened. How much do you know about them?"

Al-Asura answered first:

"The gods and demons were born from heaven and earth, powerful from birth. They didn't need cultivation to wield power enough to move mountains and fill seas. Every god and demon possessed a core spiritual essence bestowed by heaven and earth."

No one added anything. What al-Asura had said was probably everything they knew about the gods and demons.

Xu Qi’an sighed.

"Born of heaven and earth, dying to heaven and earth. This is an inevitable cause and effect."

*An inevitable cause and effect...* Everyone frowned, vaguely sensing some immense secret hidden in those words.

Xu Qi’an didn't keep them in suspense and continued:

"On this voyage overseas, I passed by an island. It was vast beyond measure. According to the descendants of gods and demons living on it, that island was formed from the corpse of an ancient god-demon after death.

"The gods and demons were born of heaven and earth. They themselves were part of heaven and earth, so naturally this transformation happened after death."

Du'e's eyes lit up and he blurted out:

"The Buddha!

"The Buddha can also become Ananda. Now He's even become the entirety of the Western Regions. There must be a connection here."

After speaking, the old monk stared at Xu Qi’an with a face full of urgency, wanting confirmation.

An ancient god-demon became an island after death, and the Buddha also possessed a similar trait. In other words, in a certain sense, the Buddha and the ancient gods and demons were the same?

Thoughts surged through everyone's minds and inspiration burst forth.

Xu Qi’an gave a soft chuckle, clasped his hands behind his back, and said:

"The first great calamity and the second great calamity had the same purpose."

"What purpose?" Huaiqing immediately pressed.

The others also wanted this answer.

Xu Qi’an didn't answer at once. He considered his wording for a few seconds, then slowly said:

"To replace the Heavenly Dao and become the will of the world of Jiuzhou."

It was like thunder exploding on level ground, blasting the beyond-mortals in the imperial study senseless.

Daoist Jinlian drew a deep breath. This deeply calculating Daoist Leader of the Earth Sect found it hard to remain calm and asked blankly:

"What--what did you say?"

Xu Qi’an swept his gaze across the room and found that everyone's expressions looked much the same as Daoist Jinlian's. Even Wei Yuan and Zhao Shou wore rather dazed looks.

"When heaven and earth first opened, Jiuzhou was in a state of darkness. Many years later, gods and demons were born, and life began. At that stage, order was chaotic. Day and night weren't distinct, there were no four seasons, yin and yang and the five elements were all jumbled together. There was no spiritual energy in heaven and earth for the human race and Yao race to cultivate.

"Many years passed again. As heaven and earth evolved, the five elements should have separated and the four poles fixed in place. But this world couldn't continue evolving. Do you know why?"

No one answered him. They were still digesting this earth-shattering revelation.

So Xu Qi’an looked at the ruler of the Myriad Yao Kingdom. The nine-tailed heavenly fox reluctantly acted as his straight man[1] for once and helped the smelly man save face.

"You can guess it well enough. Because heaven and earth were incomplete, and the gods and demons seized heaven and earth's power."

"Smart!"

Xu Qi’an praised her, then continued:

"So, in ancient times, a gate of light appeared, a gate leading to the 'Heavenly Dao'. The gods and demons were embodiments of the laws of heaven and earth, which meant they could pass through that gate. As long as they successfully pushed it open, they could advance into the Heavenly Dao."

Luo Yuheng suddenly understood.

"So that was why the gods and demons slaughtered one another? But in the end all the gods and demons perished, or else, is the current Heavenly Dao one of those ancient gods and demons?"

She had voiced everyone's doubt.

Under the gaze of the crowd, Xu Qi’an shook his head.

"The gods and demons slaughtered one another, and their spiritual essence returned to heaven and earth. In the end, Jiuzhou seized enough spiritual essence to shut the gate to heaven."

So that's how it was. No wonder the Buddha underwent such a mutation.

Everyone present was intelligent. Linking it to the Buddha becoming the Western Regions and what they'd witnessed with their own eyes, they no longer had any doubts about Xu Qi’an's words.

"That living beings can become heaven and earth and replace the Heavenly Dao, it's truly hard to believe," Yang Gong murmured. "If Ningyan hadn't told us, I'd never have imagined this was the truth."

As soon as he finished speaking, a streak of clear light shot out of his sleeve and smacked hard against his skull.

"I am _his_ teacher..."

Yang Gong muttered a low rebuke to the ruler, then hurriedly put it away, looking slightly embarrassed.

It was just like one's own child acting up disgracefully in public and making the adults lose face.

Fortunately, everyone was immersed in the immense shock at the moment and paid him no attention.

Wei Yuan said gravely:

"Then the coming of the second great calamity is because the gate to heaven has opened again?"

Xu Qi’an shook his head.

"This calamity is different from the ancient era. This time there's no gate of light. The Beyond-rank beings found another path, and that is to plunder fortune."

Then he told everyone in detail how devouring fortune could gain one 'recognition' and naturally allow one to replace the Heavenly Dao, including the secret that only a gatekeeper from the martial artist system could stand guard.

"So that's the reason the Beyond-rank beings plunder fortune." Wei Yuan pinched the bridge of his nose and sighed.

Daoist Jinlian and the others stayed silent, lost in their own thoughts as they digested the staggering revelation.

At that moment, Huaiqing frowned and said:

"Is this the result of the current stage of evolution? Or has the Heavenly Dao of Jiuzhou always been replaceable?"

That point was extremely important, so everyone seemed to wake from their shock and turned to Xu Qi’an.

"I can't give an answer. Perhaps this world is simply like this, or perhaps, as Your Majesty said, it's only the condition of the current stage," Xu Qi’an said after a moment's thought.

Huaiqing nodded as she thought aloud.

"So, at the current stage, a gatekeeper is needed, and you're the gatekeeper chosen by the Jianzheng."

"The Daoist Venerable!" Orange Cat Daoist suddenly said. "I finally understand why the the Daoist Venerable created the Heaven, Earth, and Human Sects. It was all to replace the Heavenly Dao and become the will of Jiuzhou."

After speaking, he looked at Xu Qi’an as though seeking confirmation from him.

Xu Qi’an nodded.

"The method of devouring fortune to replace the Heavenly Dao was indeed researched and pioneered by the the Daoist Venerable."

*It was pioneered by the the Daoist Venerable? He truly was an unparalleled figure throughout the ages...* Everyone felt both awe and shock.

Wei Yuan asked:

"Did you learn these secrets from the Jianzheng?"

Xu Qi’an answered candidly:

"I met the Jianzheng once overseas. He's still sealed by Desolation. While I'm at it, let me also tell you all another piece of bad news. Desolation has now fallen into slumber. When It wakes again, It'll most likely have returned to its peak."

*A--another Beyond-rank...* Huaiqing and the others felt bitterness rise on their tongues, and the joy of repelling the Buddha and preserving Leizhou vanished without a trace.

If the Buddha, the Warlock God, the Gu God, and Desolation joined forces, the Great Feng would have no chance of turning things around, not even the slightest hope.

Master Hengyuan, who had remained silent the whole time, wore a bitter expression and couldn't help saying:

"Perhaps... we can try to split the enemy and win over one or even two of the Beyond-rank beings."

No one spoke.

Master Hengyuan looked around, then finally turned to the Silver Gong he was closest to.

"Sir Xu, what do you think?"

Xu Qi’an shook his head.

"Desolation and the Gu God are god-demons. One has slept in the Southern Marches for endless years, the other has drifted overseas. They aren't like the Buddha and the Warlock God, who founded religions to gather fortune.

"Once they emerge into the world, the first thing they'll do will certainly be to gather fortune. The Southern Marches are sparsely populated and its fortune is weak. If you were the Gu God, what would you do?"

Master Hengyuan understood.

"Attack the Central Plains and annex the Great Feng's territory."

The Western Regions had already been replaced by the Buddha, and the northeast would surely not escape the Warlock God's grasp either, so moving north and swallowing the Central Plains was the best choice.

It was the same for Desolation.

"And what about the Warlock God and the Buddha?" Hengyuan asked unwillingly.

Al-Asura gave a mocking laugh.

"Of course they'll seize the chance to carve up the Central Plains. Would they instead help the Great Feng defend it? Would the Great Feng hand over its lands willingly in gratitude?

"You monk really are stupid."

Arhat Du'e's expression was grave.

"Before a Beyond-rank being, any scheme is laughable and pathetic."

Xu Qi’an let out a breath and said helplessly:

"That's why I said earlier that, regrettably, I didn't find a way to advance to Martial God."

At that moment, Wei Yuan spoke.

"It's not that there's absolutely no way. Since you've already advanced to half-step Martial God, make a trip to Mount Jing City and see whether you can wipe out the Church of the Warlock God. As for the Southern Marches, relocate all the people of the shaman clans into the Central Plains. That will both concentrate our strength and, indirectly, weaken the Gu God.

"Once those two matters are settled, Xu Ningyan, go overseas once more. Perhaps the Jianzheng will be waiting for you there.

"Your Majesty, arrangements for the Mahayana Buddhists must be implemented as soon as possible. That will better consolidate fortune."

In just a few sentences, he laid out everything that needed to be done next.

Suddenly, Chu Yuanzhen asked:

"Where's Miaozhen? Why didn't she come back with you?"

*Oh right, and Miaozhen...* Everyone instantly remembered Lady Flying Swallow.

Xu Qi’an froze for a moment, his heart sinking.

"The situation was urgent then, so I teleported straight back. Because of that, I didn't see her on the way. Surely she can't still be overseas looking for me?"

The members of the Heaven and Earth Society all cupped their hands towards him, indicating that he could take the blame for this one.

Daoist Jinlian, ever understanding, said:

"I'll inform her for you."

He lowered his head, took out the Earth Book fragment, and sent Li Miaozhen a private message:

【NINE: Miaozhen, come back. The Buddha's already retreated.】

【TWO: What?】

【NINE: Xu Ningyan came back ages ago. He joined forces with Shenshu and drove the Buddha back. Things are peaceful for the time being.】

There was silence from the other side for a long while.

【TWO: Why didn't anyone tell me?】

Daoist Jinlian could practically see Li Miaozhen's brows standing on end and her teeth clenched in rage.

【NINE: Xu Ningyan said he forgot about you.】

【TWO: Oh!】

Then there was no further sound.

Daoist Jinlian put away the Earth Book and said with a smiling face:

"Miaozhen is indeed still overseas."

Xu Qi’an gave a dry cough.

"She's not angry, is she?"

Daoist Jinlian shook his head.

"Very calm. Not angry."

The members of the Heaven and Earth Society once again cupped their hands towards Xu Qi’an, don't believe the LYB.

Xu Qi’an returned the salute with a grave expression.

After discussing in secret for a little longer, everyone dispersed.

"Silver Gong Xu, stay a moment. We have something to ask you."

Huaiqing deliberately kept Xu Qi’an behind.

"I'll stay and listen too." The ruler of the Myriad Yao Kingdom smiled sweetly.

Huaiqing shot her an unhappy glance, but the vixen was thoroughly tactless and thick-skinned, treating it as nothing.

Huaiqing hadn't kept him back for any major matter. She merely asked in detail about what had happened during his voyage and learnt more about the overseas world.

"Resources overseas are abundant, inexhaustible and limitless. Unfortunately, the Great Feng's navy is too weak for long-distance voyages, and there are too many descendants of gods and demons there. It's far too dangerous..." Huaiqing said regretfully.

Xu Qi’an gave a few casual words of agreement. He only wanted to go home, arrange flowers and fondle jade, and reunite with his delicate little wives after so long apart.

The nine-tailed fox's eyes rolled as she smiled and said:

"Speaking of treasures, Silver Gong Xu did ask for a treasure for Your Majesty on the mermaid island."

Huaiqing was instantly interested and looked at Xu Qi’an full of anticipation.

The mermaid pearl... Xu Qi’an glared at the nine-tailed fox. She was stirring trouble again.

The nine-tailed fox nudged him with her foot and urged:

"Where's the pearl? Hurry up and take it out. It's a unique pearl in all the world, worth a fortune."

Xu Qi’an thought seriously for quite a while, then decided to go with the flow and cooperate with the fox in this mischief.

Because he also wanted to know what Huaiqing truly felt about him.

Of all the women he knew, this female Emperor was the most inscrutable. She also possessed an intense desire for power and lofty ambitions no less than any man's.

She belonged to the type of rational, career-driven, strong woman.

Completely different from Lin'an, that love-brained silly princess.

Was Huaiqing's closeness to him due to dependence on a strong man, for the sake of using his value?

Or did she genuinely like him from the bottom of her heart, feel affection for him?

And if she liked him, how deep was it? Was it only a little fondness, or love to the marrow?

Let the mermaid pearl test it.

Xu Qi’an immediately took out the mermaid pearl, held it in his palm, and said with a smile:

"This is it."

The mermaid pearl was milky white, round and translucent, giving off a faint glow. At a glance it was clearly priceless. Any woman who loved jewellery and ornaments would be delighted upon seeing it.

Huaiqing was also a woman, and took a liking to it at first sight. "Let us have a look."

She raised a delicate hand, and the mermaid pearl in Xu Qi’an's palm flew towards Huaiqing.
