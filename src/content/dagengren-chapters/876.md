# 876. Save Us

Xu Qi’an drew the Sovereign Sword and poured a steady stream of qi into the brass blade. The surging qi made the Sovereign Sword glow like a red-hot branding iron, and the surrounding seawater quickly began to boil.

He swung his arm and slashed out streak after streak of golden sword light at random, sending them into the trench.

The first streak of sword light crashed into the edge of the trench, kicking up countless clouds of silt like dust and shaking loose chunk after chunk of boulder.

The second, the third...

A dozen or so streaks of sword light vanished into the deep black trench. A few seconds later, the entire seabed began to tremble, and the silt that had lain undisturbed here for endless ages rose up in clouds.

The soft mud layer burst apart, and the clear seawater instantly turned into a murky slurry.

A deep, heavy roar came from the trench, made all the more terrifying by the distortion of the seawater.

The ancient monster sleeping in the trench had been enraged.

The next moment, five tentacles shot out from the deep trench, carrying millions of tonnes of dark current with them as they smashed viciously towards Xu Qi’an.

At that moment, an equally thick snow-white fox tail stabbed in from behind Xu Qi’an and struck head-on against the tentacles. The waters across the entire sea region shook at that instant.

If this place had been near the shore, it would have been an absolute disaster for the coastal towns.

The tsunami raised by the battle would have overwhelmed everything with devastating force.

The snow-white fox tail wrapped around six tentacles. The two sides looked like tangled lines drawn taut to their limit.

The silver-haired yao enchantress’s pale face instantly flushed red, and veins bulged on her forehead as she transmitted her voice:

"I can hold on for no more than one stick's incense time."

Xu Qi’an said no more. He suddenly dived down like a launched torpedo, trailing boiling bubbles behind him as he plunged into the trench.

He dived in the lightless darkness for a long while, occasionally tossing out a shell and detonating it to light up the surroundings.

There were no fish to be seen here, and even underwater plants like seaweed were rare. Xu Qi’an moved between the six tentacle-pillars, and before long, his spirit sense detected the true body of the god-demon that had fallen here.

He flung out dozens of shells at once and detonated them all together.

Bang bang bang...

Amid the muffled explosions, the fire element swelled into clusters of flame, bringing the first light in endless ages.

It illuminated what remained of that ancient god-demon.

It was a monster so enormous as to defy imagination, resembling an octopus in appearance. Its body almost filled the entire trench. Its form was incomplete and covered in bite marks.

It had only one ash-grey eye left, set into a head covered in scales. When the firelight shone over it, in this deathly still deep sea, the distance between Xu Qi’an and it was less than a thousand feet.

The ash-grey eye stared lifelessly at Xu Qi’an, as though looking at a speck of dust in the air.

That was the difference in size between them.

*Fortunately, I don't have thalassophobia...* Borrowing the slowly dying firelight, Xu Qi’an realised that the monster ought to have had more than a dozen tentacles, but most of them had long since been torn away.

*There's no fluctuation of primordial spirit. It's died long ago, so how has It endured through these endless years...*

After a preliminary examination, Xu Qi’an found himself in a difficult position.

If he wanted to set down the formation disc and refine Its essence, he would first have to subdue the enemy. And against an enemy of this level, killing was the only option.

But it was already dead, and had been dead for endless ages.

*What now?*

Xu Qi’an silently gazed at the "octopus monster"’s true body, and suddenly he understood.

It had died back in ancient times. What remained was unyielding will and fearless battle intent. It was obsession that had let It endure across endless ages until today.

*When this ancient god-demon died at that enemy’s hands, It must have been unwilling to accept it, unwilling to submit. The way to eliminate obsession is very simple.*

What he had to do was not kill It, but defeat It...

Above the trench, the nine-tailed fox, still struggling in a fierce contest of strength with the tentacles, received Xu Qi’an’s voice transmission:

"Your Majesty, go up first. You don't need to interfere in this battle any more."

Irbu had never experienced such rapid flight in his life. Mountains, rivers, and the land below flashed past in a blur before his eyes. By the time the Great Warlock’s power was exhausted, he found that he had already passed through the territory of the Great Feng and arrived in the Western Regions.

*Sending me to deliver the Imperial Seal, isn't this just sending me to my death?* Irbu flew cautiously through the skies of the Western Regions. As he recalled the road he had taken, a question arose in his mind:

*Why am I always the one running errands?*

Ever since the Zhenbei King’s Blood Pill, he had constantly played the role of errand runner and hired muscle.

The other Master of Wisdom, Uda Batu, still had never even met Xu Qi’an, yet he himself had already dealt with Xu Qi’an several times.

Irbu was extremely cautious. He did not go deep into the Western Regions. After finding an ordinary corpse, he manipulated it to fly on the wind, letting the corpse go to Ananda in his place.

*If I go deep into the Western Regions myself, I'll definitely be devoured by the Buddha.*

*I can use this puppet to scout things out as well, and see what the Western Regions look like now.*

At his cultivation as a Master of Wisdom, if he manipulated only a single corpse, he could probably use about half the strength of his true body.

Irbu flew swiftly for a while, and his strongest impression was desolation.

No sign of human life, only barren stillness.

Every village and town he passed was empty.

*They're really all gone. Across the hundreds of thousands of li of the Western Regions, all living beings have vanished. This struggle over the heavens changing really is cruel... Those fools in the Great Feng probably don't even know what's happened.*

*They have no Beyond-rank sitting guard, so they've no way of learning the secrets of the great calamity. In future they won't even know how they died...*

"If the Buddha replaces the Heavenly Dao, our warlock system, no, every system under heaven will perish and become dust in history. I really can't understand why the Great Warlock wants to deliver the fortune of the Yan Kingdom to the Buddha."

As Irbu’s puppet flew towards Ananda, he continued thinking.

*Mahayana Buddhism has split away part of the Buddha’s fortune, preventing Him from fully becoming the Western Regions. But with the Buddha’s power and the Buddhist order’s foundations, He definitely won't stop there. He must have some other method. It may just take an immense amount of time, and that would be good for the Warlock God.*

*If the Great Warlock hands the fortune of Yan Kingdom to the Buddha, and the Buddha uses it to become the Western Regions, then the next step will be to devour the Central Plains...*

At that thought, inspiration flashed through Irbu’s mind, and he continued following the thread of logic:

*The beyond-mortals of the Great Feng will certainly resist to the death. Faced with the Buddha making a move, that half-step Martial God in the Southern Marches probably won't be able to stand aside, and with the other powerful beyond-mortal involved as well, when two tigers fight, one is bound to be wounded. Then our Church of the Warlock God can reap the fisherman’s profit.*

*No, even a half-step Martial God can't possibly stop a Beyond-rank by himself. The Great Warlock is playing with fire here. That doesn't fit his character. What makes him think the Great Feng can stop the Buddha? Xu Qi’an is overseas, and the Jianzheng is sealed...*

Irbu froze, then suddenly understood the Great Warlock’s true intention.

That old fellow the Jianzheng might have stumbled into disaster and been jointly sealed by Xu Pingfeng, Agaruka Bodhisattva and the others, but he was a Master of Heaven's Mandate, the kind best at laying plans.

The Jianzheng calculated everything. Could he really have failed to foresee the great calamity?

He must have left behind corresponding measures, hidden trump cards unknown to others.

In that case, the Buddha was their pathfinding pawn.

*Now this is the real fisherman’s profit from the fight between the sandpiper and clam. And if the Great Feng still can't defeat the Buddha, then at worst we can ally with the Gu God in future against the Buddha...*

At that moment, Irbu saw the majestic sacred mountain appear at the end of the horizon. He had arrived at Ananda.

He immediately stopped thinking and controlled the puppet, turning it into a streak of black light that shot towards Ananda.

Before it could draw near, a white figure flashed ahead. Vaidurya Bodhisattva, with her exquisite, sharply defined features, white robes, bare feet, and azure hair cascading like a waterfall, blocked his path.

This peerless beauty with Western Regions features said coolly:

"Irbu of the Church of the Warlock God, what are you doing at Ananda?"

The walking corpse puppet froze for a moment and blurted out:

"How did you know it was me?"

Vaidurya Bodhisattva was beautiful as a peach blossom yet cold as frost. Her voice remained utterly flat:

"Aren't you the one in charge of running errands?"

*Even if I'm usually the one sent out to handle matters, that doesn't mean I'm some errand runner. I am a Master of Wisdom...* Irbu cursed furiously inwardly, but outwardly remained aloof and cold as he said indifferently:

"The Great Warlock entrusted me to deliver fortune to the Buddha."

To save face and highlight his own importance, he avoided words like "dispatched" and "ordered".

Vaidurya Bodhisattva raised her brows. After more than ten seconds, she said:

"Sarun Agu wants our Buddhist order to charge into battle and wear down all the beyond-mortal strength of the Central Plains?"

*This woman is clever...* Irbu said coldly:

"You can refuse."

Vaidurya Bodhisattva closed her beautiful eyes and listened for a moment, then opened them and said:

"Where is it?"

"The Buddhist sect truly is confident." Irbu gave a soft snort and said:

"The Imperial Seal is with my true body. If you want it, come with me."

Vaidurya Bodhisattva shook her head.

"No need. Bring the Imperial Seal and come west."

With that, she vanished and returned to Ananda.

Irbu pondered for a moment, then cut off his control over the puppet.

At the border of the Western Regions, Irbu, wrapped in a warlock’s robe, opened his eyes.

"You want me to deliver it there?"

He thought for a while, then stretched his right hand out from his robe and made a grasping motion towards the distance.

A camel was dragged over to him. It bled from all seven orifices and died, then was transformed into a walking corpse puppet.

The camel walked over, picked up the Imperial Seal from Irbu’s hand in its mouth, kicked off with its four hooves, and flew into the sky.

The camel flew and flew, and finally reached a deserted area. Suddenly, it saw a pair of eyes open in the sandy ground below.

Immediately after, a huge mouth split open in the sand. A wave of earth shot into the sky, propelling the mouth upwards to bite at the camel and swallow it whole.

After swallowing the camel and the Imperial Seal of Yan, the earthen wave did not fall back. Instead, as if stimulated, it continued soaring upwards, and in the blink of an eye it turned into a giant wave a thousand feet high, rolling eastwards.

The Buddha had obtained a new voucher. He continued assimilating the rules, replacing the rules, devouring everything along the way.

Meanwhile, Irbu sprang into the air, shivering all over as he rode black light upwards and fled.

Through their shared vision, he had clearly and directly felt the terror of a Beyond-rank, that soul-shaking aura, and the instinctive sense of one’s own lowliness it inspired. The latter in particular was something Irbu had never experienced before.

Even when facing the Warlock God, though he trembled like a man standing over an abyss, Irbu never felt himself lowly.

*Too terrifying, far too terrifying...*

Southern Marches.

Li Lingsu drank mountain tea, a local speciality of the Hundred Thousand Mountains, and glanced at the several great beauties gathered nearby discussing important matters.

Besides Xu Qi’an’s concubine Yeji, there were three fox clan beauties whose looks, bearing, and figures were in no way inferior.

Qingji wore a pale azure long dress and a thin gauze veil. Her manner was reserved and cool. She made Li Lingsu think of a dignified young lady from a great household, graceful and composed, well read and well mannered, with an excellent upbringing.

She was polite to everyone, yet never particularly warm or cold to anyone.

The beautiful married woman in a complicated black dress was called Youji. She possessed both the mature charm of a forty-year-old woman and the grace and beauty of one in her twenties.

In every frown and smile, every glance and turn of the head, she had long since shed a young girl’s greenness, like a noblewoman long accustomed to life in a great household.

She had a kind and gentle maternal nature, but also the same moving allure as the other fox clan women.

The third was a girl named Lingji. Her lively cheerfulness made Li Lingsu think of Chu Caiwei, the new Jianzheng of the Sitianjian.

The difference was that the Jianzheng’s liveliness carried a silly, adorable innocence, carefree and guileless.

The fox clan girl Lingji, however, was more clever and mischievous, sly and lovely.

At a glance, she was clearly the sort of little yao girl who loved playing tricks on people.

*My romantic fate has come again...* Li Lingsu thought to himself. Then he rubbed his kidneys and added two more words inwardly: *probably!*

"The queen and Xu-lang have been out at sea for several months already and still haven't returned. Meanwhile, the situation in Jiuzhou grows increasingly grim."

Yeji’s delicate brows were knit tightly, her face full of worry.

"If that Silver Gong Xu can't break through to half-step Martial God, then this trip out to sea will have been for nothing. Shenshu alone can't hold back a Beyond-rank."

Lingji propped her chin on both hands, widened her bright eyes, and said with a grin:

"Big Sister Yeji is missing her lover, isn't she? You've been left alone for so long, are you finding the loneliness unbearable? When are you going to lend your lover to me for a bit of fun?"

Yeji glanced at the girl's still only slightly developed, immature body and let out a scornful sneer.

The relationships between the nine sisters were not all loving and harmonious. Aside from Youji, whose overflowing maternal nature had earned unanimous respect from the sisters, and Baiji, who was soft, adorable, and still unable to take human form, earning unanimous affection, the rest of the sisters all had more or less their own intrigues.

Youji bent a finger and flicked the girl’s smooth forehead with a sharp tap, then gently reproached her:

"We're discussing serious matters. Don't fool about."

Lingji covered her forehead, pouted, and grumbled:

"Anyway, sooner or later we're all going to marry Xu Ningyan. Big Sister Qingji said before that Mistress probably won't be able to escape Silver Gong Xu’s clutches. And if Mistress follows Silver Gong Xu, then won't we have to go along as part of the dowry?"

Qingji’s expression changed slightly.

"Stop talking nonsense. I never said anything like that."

*What? They too are the scoundrel Xu Ningyan’s reserved concubines? How can this be? That dog thief Xu Ningyan, I've never seen such a lecherous bastard in my life. This is too much, far too much...* Li Lingsu’s face slowly stiffened.

Just then, he felt a familiar lurch in his heart.

He took out the Earth Book fragment to check the transmitted message.

【TWO: Senior Brother, this is very bad. Get Shenshu to Leizhou and save us...】
