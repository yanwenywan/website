# 879. Half-step Martial God (I)

【FIVE: Why be so despairing? If Xu Ningyan can advance to half-step Martial God and join forces with Shenshu, that would just about count as one Beyond-rank, wouldn't it? Then there'd still be room for everyone to sit down and negotiate.】

Also in the Southern Marches, Lina, who was enjoying roast meat contributed by the yao troops, chose that moment to chip in.

...

Li Lingsu was speechless for a moment, but on second thought, it did seem to make sense.

The examples of Shenshu and the Buddha had already shown them that although a half-step Martial God was no match for a Beyond-rank, if there were two half-step Martial Gods together, surely they wouldn't still be completely crushed by a Beyond-rank?

If that was the case, then the Great Feng really did have the capital to sit at the negotiating table and drink tea.

【TWO: That damned Xu Ningyan's been out at sea for months. Who knows what's happened to him.】

Li Miaozhen's message fairly crackled with irritation.

*Junior Sister, mind your status. You're Lanlian of the Earth Sect, the former Holy Maiden of the Heaven Sect. You're not one of those vulgar women in Xu Ningyan's harem...* Li Lingsu silently tried to preserve his junior sister's dignity in his heart.

【ONE: Tell Arhat Du'e to return to the capital at once. We aren't at ease leaving him in Leizhou. The three Bodhisattvas still haven't made their move.】

Now that matters had taken such a turn, recalling the founder of Mahayana Buddhism to the capital was the safest course.

At the very least, the capital still had a first rank Earthly Immortal holding the fort, as well as several beyond-mortal experts.

【EIGHT: He can't come back. Several beyond-mortal experts from the Church of the Warlock God are eyeing us like tigers watching prey.】

Al-Asura replied.

Although Sarun Agu and the others were still some distance away, al-Asura had already sensed them. Of course, the Great Warlock had had no intention of hiding in the first place.

*Sarun Agu is in Leizhou too...* Huaiqing's scalp tingled slightly.

The Church of the Warlock God had handed fortune over to the Buddha, and now they'd come to "watch the battle". Their intentions were vile beyond words.

They clearly meant to stab from the shadows at a crucial moment. If Arhat Du'e tried to return to the capital now, he'd most likely be walking straight into a trap.

And if Daoist Jinlian and the other beyond-mortal experts were also recalled, then what about Shenshu?

With Daoist Jinlian, al-Asura and the other beyond-mortal experts there, they could at least support Shenshu and deal with some of the trouble for him.

【NINE: The leaders of the shaman clans are here too.】

Daoist Jinlian added another line.

*Although the leaders of the shaman clans are generally only rank three and can't serve as the main force, the seven gu arts were bizarre and unfathomable, and they could just about tie down the Church of the Warlock God...* Huaiqing let out a slight sigh of relief and sent a message:

【ONE: Report the battle situation at all times, if circumstances allow. I'll have the National Teacher and Dean Zhao rush to Leizhou at once.】

She put down the Earth Book fragment. and looked at Wei Yuan and the other two in the hall, quickly giving a brief account of the situation.

Zhao Shou pondered and said:

"I'll have Yang Gong take the Confucian Sage's carving knife to Leizhou as support. As for me, I'll remain in the capital."

He was guarding against anyone taking the opportunity to wipe out the capital.

The three Bodhisattvas of the Buddhist faction still hadn't shown themselves.

Wang Zhenwen's face was stern.

"Have Yang Gong take the teleportation jade talisman and go to Leizhou first. National Teacher... Daoist Leader Luo will remain in the capital for now. Once the Buddhist Bodhisattvas appear, National Teacher can rush to support immediately."

Wei Yuan didn't interrupt. There was nothing wrong with Wang Zhenwen's arrangements.

What they had to guard against now was the Buddhist Bodhisattvas attacking the capital. The Church of the Warlock God, by contrast, didn't need as much concern, because Buddhism no longer had the concept of a "home base", while the Warlock God of the Church of the Warlock God had still not broken His seal.

At this stage, Buddhism could afford to abandon all else, but the Church of the Warlock God didn't dare go down together with them.

Zhao Shou flicked his great sleeve, his voice ringing with force.

"Yang Gong is right beside me."

A clear light rose from nearby, sketching out the figure of Yang Gong, the Ziyang Jushi.

He was wearing a crimson official robe. A moment ago, he'd been handling affairs at the offices.

"..."

Even though they had long since grown used to Confucian spells, this style of "speak it and it happens" still felt absurd to the three in the hall, leaving them speechless.

"Dean?"

Yang Gong looked around. Seeing everyone's grave expressions, he immediately frowned.

"What has happened?"

Zhao Shou briefly explained the situation to him, and Yang Gong listened with knitted brows and a heavy heart.

Huaiqing said sincerely:

"My thanks for your trouble, sir."

Back when she studied at Cloud Deer Academy, she had indeed been a student under Ziyang Jushi.

Yang Gong nodded and was just about to accept when a streak of clear light suddenly shot out from his sleeve and smacked hard towards Huaiqing's head.

Huaiqing froze for a moment. Relying on a martial artist's instinct, she reached out and caught the streak of light. Looking closely, she saw it was a ruler.

She looked at Yang Gong in astonishment.

Was he trying to assassinate the Emperor?

Yang Gong let out a sigh.

"Your Majesty, don't call me sir. If Your Majesty calls me sir, then don't use words like 'please instruct me' or 'thank you for your trouble'."

He beckoned, and the ruler flew back into his sleeve.

Then he explained:

"I nurtured this thing with the Three Character Classic. As the saying goes, 'To raise without teaching is the father's fault. To teach without strictness is the teacher's laziness.'"

*So it hits any student it sees?* Huaiqing pressed her lips together, then said seriously:

"Very responsible indeed!"

...Yang Gong gave a bitter smile. "Your servant shall take it that Your Majesty truly means that as praise."

He knew time was pressing, so he said no more. With a sweep of his sleeve, he copied Zhao Shou's earlier action and declared loudly:

"Confucian Sage's carving knife, come to me at once."

To save time, he tried summoning the Confucian cap.

But there was no response.

Everyone turned in unison to look at Ziyang Jushi. Yang Gong's old face reddened, and he immediately said:

"We are within Cloud Deer Academy."

Clear light rose from beneath his feet, and he vanished from the spot.

...

Boom!

The ground beneath Shenshu exploded. Clods of earth and chunks of flesh alike were blasted away, clearing out a vacuum zone several zhang wide.

And he himself shot towards the Buddha like a cannonball fired at high speed.

To the Buddha's rear right, a phantom Dharma Aspect flashed and vanished, and He disappeared at once, causing Shenshu to pounce on empty air.

Immediately afterwards, the Buddha appeared behind Shenshu. To His left rear, the Vajra Dharma Aspect, symbolising conquest and strength, emerged.

All twelve arms rose at once.

Clang!

Amid a sound like iron being hammered, Shenshu staggered backwards, while the flesh beneath the Buddha's feet rippled like water, dispersing the force of the half-step Martial God's punch.

Behind the Buddha, who hadn't retreated a single step, another figure emerged again, the Great Compassion and Mercy Dharma Aspect.

Sanskrit chants resounded through heaven and earth, dissolving all rage and hostility.

Crack crack...

The dazzling golden wheel turned in reverse, and the three golden Buddhist characters for "Asura" lit up.

Shenshu's aura began to decline at a visible rate. Against his pitch-black skin, his head first turned illusory, then his right arm turned illusory as well, and only then was the power of the wheel exhausted.

The reversed Great Samsara Dharma Aspect was weakening Shenshu to a past state. If turned forward, it would instead advance him into the future.

A half-step Martial God's lifespan was endless, so turning it forward had no meaning.

And since Shenshu had only recently returned to his peak, reversing it was an effective way to weaken him.

In the distance, al-Asura's aura also showed signs of a slight decline. As a member of the Asura race as well, he couldn't avoid it, though being so far away and only at the outer edge of the Great Samsara Dharma Aspect's range meant the weakening wasn't too severe.

Yet another Dharma Aspect emerged behind the Buddha, eyes lowered and fingers pinching a flower, the wheel of wisdom behind its head turning in reverse.

Shenshu's eyes suddenly lost their light and grew vacant, as though he'd forgotten he was standing in a place of danger.

During this process, behind the Buddha, within the vast ocean of dark red flesh, mouths split open once more, one after another, and slowly spat out miniature golden suns.

Buddha light filled heaven and earth.

These miniature golden suns gathered towards the phantom behind the Buddha's head. The more they gathered, the brighter the Buddha light became, illuminating this whole world until even the night turned into day.

How terrifying the Buddha was with all nine Dharma Aspects gathered.

Li Miaozhen and Daoist Jinlian darted out. In perfect unison, they raised a hand and turned it clockwise towards Shenshu in the distance.

At the cost of weakening their own luck, they infused Shenshu with deep fortune.

At the same time, golden light shot from their eyes as they tried to use the power of their Yang Spirits to awaken Shenshu.

But the power of the Yang Spirit was dissolved and purified within the Buddha light, and had no effect.

"Spells won't work. We need another way!"

Chu Yuanzhen said in a deep voice:

"Miaozhen, lend me a flying sword."

His own sword had already been destroyed by the Buddha's corruption.

As soon as he spoke, more than ten flying swords of excellent quality flew before him for him to choose from.

They weren't Li Miaozhen's artefacts, but Sun Xuanji's.

*One is enough...* Chu Yuanzhen casually grabbed one and ran his left hand along its blade.

At once, the sword began to radiate intense emotion, greed, rage, delusion, hatred, love, loathing, desire, as though it were the very aggregation of human nature in the world.

Before setting out, Chu Yuanzhen had specially obtained karmic fire from Luo Yuheng and sealed it within his body. If used well, that power could temporarily raise the force of his sword to beyond-mortal level.

Chu Yuanzhen hurled the flying sword. The target wasn't the Buddha, but Shenshu.

He wanted to use intense karmic fire to wake Shenshu.

And karmic fire belonged neither to spells nor to the elemental forces of heaven and earth.

The flying sword turned into a streak of light, like a bright thread, and shot towards Shenshu's back.

But at that moment, heaven and earth lost their colour. Within a thousand feet around Shenshu, everything turned black and white.

The colourless lapis lazuli domain.

The flying sword froze within the domain, then clattered to the ground.

By now, the great sun behind the Buddha's head had grown to more than ten metres across, and Shenshu's skin had begun to melt.

Chu Yuanzhen's expression changed slightly.

Clang!

A sonorous bell suddenly rang out. The sound was deafening, causing spirits to tremble and blood to surge.

Li Miaozhen was horrified to discover that even she, with her Daoist Yang Spirit, showed signs of being shaken out of her body.

The one ringing the bell was Sun Xuanji. In his hand he held a brass hammer covered in formation patterns, and before him floated a bronze bell as tall as two men.

Clang clang clang...

Sun Xuanji struck the bronze bell with all his strength. With every strike, ripples of clear light accompanied the bell's toll, and the formation patterns carved on the bell immediately lit up, as though they might emerge from the surface.

Blood streamed from his eyes, ears, nostrils and the corners of his mouth, but the brass hammer in his hand never paused for an instant.

On hearing the bell, Shenshu's pupils stirred faintly, as if he were about to wake.

The Great Samsara Dharma Aspect burst with intense brilliance, the wheel behind the Medicine Buddha Dharma Aspect spun ever faster in reverse, and the lips of the Great Compassion and Mercy Dharma Aspect moved, making the chanting between heaven and earth even louder, gradually drowning out the bell.

The Great Sun Samsara Dharma Aspect behind the Buddha's head grew ever fiercer. More and more suns gathered, rapidly grinding away Shenshu's vitality.

"The effect of the bell increases tenfold."

A voice like chanting suddenly sounded beside everyone's ears.

In the distance, clear light rose and faded. Yang Gong, wearing the Confucian cap of the Lesser Sage, had finally arrived on the battlefield.

Clang!

The bell pealed across heaven and earth like a bolt from a clear sky.

The spirits of Chu Yuanzhen and Hengyuan were directly shaken out of their bodies. Arhat Du'e and al-Asura sat cross-legged with palms pressed together, resisting with meditative arts.

Relying on the strength of their Yang Spirits, Daoist Jinlian and Li Miaozhen endured the bell, but their minds swam in waves of dizziness and nausea.

The bronze hammer slipped from Sun Xuanji's hand, and his body fell from the air. His spirit too had been shaken free.

Seeing this, Yang Gong raised both hands and supported from afar the bodies of Chu Yuanzhen, Hengyuan and Sun Xuanji.

On the other side, Shenshu's ears twitched. The tolling bell echoed again and again in his mind, and in an instant he broke free from the various controlling spells. His awareness returned, and he realised the situation he was in.

Before him was the great sun that had just completed its formation.

The great sun slowly rose and crashed towards Shenshu at a speed that looked slow but was in fact extremely fast.

The other Dharma Aspects did not remain idle either, continuing to exert their might in an attempt to once more strip away Shenshu's consciousness.

"Keep striking it!"

Yang Gong shouted, then immediately spat out a mouthful of blood.

Spell backlash. Not a major problem.

Daoist Jinlian reached out, seized the bronze hammer, and slammed it hard against the bronze bell.

Clang clang clang...

The ringing bell echoed through every corner, helping Shenshu stabilise his consciousness and resist the influence of the Dharma Aspects.

This half-step Martial God let out a deep roar. His body suddenly swelled, transforming into a three hundred feet tall black Dharma Aspect. All twelve thickly muscled arms spread out and thrust upward together, holding up the Great Sun Samsara Dharma Aspect.

...

Overseas.

The nine-tailed fox stood on the surface of the sea. Around her floated the corpses of every kind of fish and shrimp, densely packed until they nearly covered the sea's surface.

The fish and shrimp corpses bobbed in the murky waves, and very few of them remained whole.

And this still wasn't all of it. By now the sea had gradually begun to calm. At its most violent, waves a hundred metres high had risen, carrying away wave after wave of marine life corpses.

*It's over...* She let out a sigh of relief. After waiting on the sea for nearly a quarter of an hour and not seeing any sign of that stinking man fleeing in defeat, the vixen knew the matter had succeeded.

She immediately twisted her slim waist and plunged into the mass of fish and shrimp corpses.

Her eight tails moved like tentacles, propelling her swiftly downward. The light above her head gradually dimmed until it vanished completely. The nine-tailed fox flicked out several streaks of white light with her fingers, and they drifted down like ghostly flames.

Illuminating the murky seawater.

After diving for a long while, the foxfire lit upon a gigantic monster. Its size was impossible to estimate. The part touched by the foxfire was no more than the tip of the iceberg compared with the whole creature.

The nine-tailed fox swept it with her divine sense and found Xu Qi'an on the monster's single eye.

She gathered the foxfire together, illuminating the figure of Silver Gong Xu.

He was stark naked, without a stitch on him. His granite-like muscles were evenly proportioned and beautifully toned. All four limbs were intact, and there wasn't a trace of injury on him.

That was understandable enough. For a first rank martial artist, unless he died body and soul, any wound could heal in an instant.

But his aura was much weaker now, so weak that the nine-tailed fox felt she could beat this crude martial artist senseless.

"Oi, how can you be such a shameless rogue? Put out the lights, quickly."

Silver Gong Xu was a man of propriety, and shifted slightly so she wouldn't see his family jewels.

The nine-tailed fox said irritably:

"Listen to you sounding pleased with yourself.

"Hurry and absorb Its essence. See if you can advance to half-step Martial God."

Deep down, she was incomparably excited. She was about to witness the birth of a half-step Martial God.
