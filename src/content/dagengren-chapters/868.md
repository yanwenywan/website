# 868. Devouring All Things

"What am I?"

The Jianzheng sighed, and there was a trace of bitterness in his voice.

"I am merely a forgotten Master of Heaven's Mandate, who was usurped by his disciple, and looked down on by a coarse martial brute he painstakingly supported."

"Don't make such a boring joke with me!" Desolation roared, its face twisting savagely.

*The Jianzheng's temperament is actually rather foul too. I guess there is a reason why the disciples he taught all had such strange tempers...* the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox thought to herself.

To be honest, if she were in Desolation's place, hearing the Jianzheng's reply, she'd want to beat him up too.

On the other side, Xu Qi'an had sharp ears and keen eyes. The Jianzheng and Desolation hadn't deliberately lowered their voices, so he heard everything clearly.

For some reason, he wasn't all that shocked. He even felt a strange sense of relief, as though the other shoe had finally dropped.

Certain suspicions in his heart had all been confirmed the moment the Jianzheng said "help him become the gatekeeper".

He shifted his attention to the pillar of light. "The thing that made gods and demons fall is actually a sabre? A sabre formed purely from condensed light..."

Xu Qi'an half believed it and half didn't.

He believed it because this sabre gave him the feeling that it could cut through anything. It ought to be an extremely rare treasure.

But it didn't seem worthy of the importance and status of the so-called "cause of the great calamity".

*The Jianzheng said this was the cause of the first great calamity, not the second. Is it because of this?*

As he thought, he withdrew his gaze and looked down below.

Piles of white bones lay heaped across the plain. Some had already rotted, becoming incomplete, weathering into dust, their original forms in life long impossible to make out.

The closer to the pillar of light, the more bones there were. By the central area, the white bones had formed a high platform, like a royal throne.

*Were these all gods and demons?*

Suddenly, Xu Qi'an slammed into an invisible wall. The wall was formed from a curtain of light, and ripples spread across it like water after being struck by the hard face of a first rank martial artist.

He couldn't get close to the pillar of light.

Desolation seemed to have been waiting for this very moment. Its golden pupils suddenly sharpened.

Pfft... crack...

The Nine-tailed Fox, whose full attention had been on Xu Qi'an, heard the sound of flesh splitting and bones breaking.

She whipped her head round and saw Desolation's gaping bloody maw, like a dark crimson abyss.

Desolation had severed its own body, splitting itself in two at the waist. Everything below the lower back remained within the domain of slowed time, while everything above the lower back regained freedom.

As a god-demon that had survived since ancient times, It was never short of ways to deal with difficulties.

Earlier, because It held the Jianzheng as a trump card, It had been secure and had no need to choose such a bloody method of self-mutilation to escape.

Now, since the Jianzheng had said all that and meant to support Xu Qi'an in becoming the gatekeeper, whether true or false, It had to respond. It couldn't allow things to continue developing.

That's right, even now, Desolation hadn't completely believed the Jianzheng's words.

Compared with Desolation's enormous size, the Nine-tailed Fox was like a speck of dust, truly not even enough to fill the gaps between Its teeth.

The silver-haired Yao beauty instinctively wanted to avoid it. Even though she'd successfully advanced to first rank, being swallowed whole by a god-demon that devoured all things was no joke.

But she immediately realised that what Desolation truly wanted to swallow wasn't her, but Xu Qi'an.

This attack was a feint. The reason It did this was to force her to retreat in fear, because now that she'd reached first rank, even if she couldn't defeat Desolation, this "half-step Beyond-rank", she still had the capital to entangle It.

And time was precisely what Desolation lacked most.

After understanding that, the silver-haired Yao beauty's lovely eyes widened. She gave up all thought of dodging, and the nine tails behind her suddenly shot up, like giant pillars thrusting into the clouds.

The fox tails expanded infinitely, baring fangs and claws. Some met the upper jaw of that abyssal maw, while others sank down to brace against the lower jaw.

Just like that, the nine tails, thick as heavenly pillars, propped open Desolation's bloody mouth like tentacles, making it hard for It to close it.

At the same time, the Nine-tailed Fox dropped to the ground. Her snow-white lotus arms transformed into forelimbs, and layer upon layer of thick, long white fur burst forth from beneath her fair skin.

Her cheeks lengthened and likewise sprouted long snow-white fur, while her eyes turned into emerald beast-eyes.

A clear, resonant howl echoed between heaven and earth.

A white fox as huge as a mountain appeared. She was noble and elegant, enchantingly seductive, like the most beautiful spirit beast in the world.

"Roar!"

Desolation let out an earth-shaking bellow. Spittle sprayed like rain, and its massive head suddenly dipped, smashing hard into the white fox's face, knocking her head sideways and making her body stagger.

Pfft...

That curved horn sealing the Jianzheng viciously stabbed into the white fox's chest, and scarlet blood rained down.

The white fox's facial muscles tightened, baring sharp fangs as she bit savagely into Desolation's nape, tearing off a chunk of flesh.

At the same time, her nine tails wrapped around Desolation's body and suddenly tightened, exerting force like a python constricting its prey.

The two giant monsters fought in the most primitive way, biting and tearing at each other. Every movement caused effects like an earthquake, every collision stirred up terrifying storms.

Their battle seemed to push time back to the ancient age, that barbaric era full of violence and disorder.

...

"She won't hold on much longer."

Xu Qi'an pressed his left palm against the light curtain and suddenly exerted force. The light curtain shook violently, yet it still withstood the terrifying brute strength of a first rank martial artist.

*To break this barrier, it'd take at least a half-step Martial God...* As the thought turned in his mind, Xu Qi'an looked at the Taiping Sabre in his hand.

Without hesitation, he raised the Taiping Sabre in his right hand and stabbed fiercely at the light curtain.

The curtain split apart soundlessly, but didn't collapse entirely. It was like cloth sliced open by a blade.

*As expected...*

He wasn't surprised. In fact, he thought it should be this way.

The Taiping Sabre had been forged by the Jianzheng, and the materials had also been provided by the Jianzheng.

When the Jianzheng had told him to bring the Taiping Sabre here, Xu Qi'an had already had a premonition.

With a turn of his wrist, the Taiping Sabre slashed upwards, cutting open a gap, and Xu Qi'an stepped into the light curtain.

After entering it, Xu Qi'an's perception of the "sabre" changed again. It still radiated brilliant divine might, as though it could cut through everything.

But behind that peerless sharpness, there was now an added sense of weight.

That heaviness came from protection.

For no reason at all, Xu Qi'an suddenly felt an urge to protect something.

Even though he himself hadn't figured out what it was he wanted to protect.

Buzz buzz...

The Taiping Sabre trembled violently, and the sabre spirit's thought entered Xu Qi'an's heart:

"It is me, and I am it!"

*It is me, and I am it...*

Xu Qi'an repeated the words several times in his heart. His gaze flickered slightly. After a moment of silence, he let go of the Taiping Sabre.

This broken sabre, which had been bloody useless ever since he'd advanced to first rank, gave a "whoosh" and flew towards the pillar of light, towards that "sabre" formed from gathered light.

The two gradually overlapped.

"Awoooo!"

In the distance, Desolation's throat erupted with the roar of a trapped beast. It seemed to have realised the seriousness of the problem.

So in that furious roar, there was now, besides anger, a bit more anxiety.

Its two hooves stamped down, pinning the Nine-tailed Fox firmly to the ground, and then came the frenzied tearing and biting. Every bite ripped away a great piece of flesh.

The scene was indescribably bloody.

It tore open flesh and bit through bones. The Nine-tailed Fox let out shrill cries of pain, her four limbs kicking madly, her nine fox tails flailing like a dying beast making its final struggle.

Whenever the force she exerted on Desolation touched Its body, it would be devoured and dissolved by an invisible power, greatly reducing its might. This was another manifestation of Desolation's innate divine power.

And on top of that, her original strength was already inferior to that of this ancient god-demon. At this moment, she could finally no longer hold on.

The fact that she'd lasted this long was thanks to the essence blood contained in her fox tails, which had been powering her.

Crack!

The Nine-tailed Fox's neck was bitten through by force, and her head was held in Desolation's mouth.

At the same time, It lowered Its head and hoisted up the white fox's body. The horn lit up, and strange, profound patterns spread across Its entire body.

It was going to devour the Nine-tailed Fox's spiritual essence, even if that would force It inadvertently into slumber.

At this point, Desolation too had no way left to retreat.

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox once again let out a shrill cry, demonic sound drilling into the brain.

A momentary daze appeared in Desolation's ferocious golden pupils. Immediately afterwards, the fox head in Its mouth turned into azure smoke and dissipated, while the limbs hanging limply from the body skewered on the horn transformed into a single tail.

The silver-haired Yao beauty appeared in the distance. She had transformed back into human form. Her flawless, peerless face was pale as paper, her aura weak, and the fox tails behind her dragged feebly along the ground. Only eight remained.

She couldn't hold on any longer.

Without the slightest hesitation, the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox ran on her two long legs towards the edge.

Away from the main battlefield.

"Xu Ningyan!"

she screamed.

Along with that scream, Desolation's two hooves suddenly leapt, and It pounced through the air at Xu Qi'an, like a mountain flying at high speed.

During the charge, Desolation's horn burst out with light that was blazing white at the edges and black within.

That light suddenly expanded, swallowing Desolation's body and turning It into a pure black hole.

Light couldn't illuminate the black hole, because even light was devoured.

Air currents rolled dust and white bones into the black hole, where they vanished without a trace.

Xu Qi'an raised his left hand and made the glass bead on his wrist light up. Then he aimed his palm at Desolation, trying to sever the space around It and throw It far away.

But he failed. The glass bead seemed to have lost its power and was unable to complete the spatial severance.

In front of the black hole, all spells were ineffective, and all spiritual essence would be utterly devoured.

Desolation's intention was obvious. It was going to make one final gamble, pushing Its innate divine power to its peak at any cost, devouring everything, including Xu Qi'an, the Nine-tailed Fox, and the sabre that had fused with the "gate".

The price was falling into slumber for a period of time.

Once It fell asleep, what happened afterwards would no longer be under Its control. Unless it was the final moment, It had never intended to use this move.

Just then, the fusion of the Taiping Sabre and the "sabre" in the pillar of light was completed. It was impossible to tell whether the former had absorbed the latter, or the latter the former.

Buzz!

The Taiping Sabre sent out a ripple of qi. Wherever it passed, the barrier collapsed into fierce winds that swept in all directions.

Now that the core had found its home, the imprisonment no longer had any reason to exist.

After the barrier collapsed, the pillar of light too slowly dimmed.

With a clang, the Taiping Sabre fell to the ground and stabbed into the bone "throne".

Xu Qi'an felt as though a burden had been lifted. He immediately pulled out the Taiping Sabre and, without bothering to inspect its condition, made a spatial jump to the Nine-tailed Fox's side. Pressing a hand to her delicate fragrant shoulder, he brought her with him as he jumped to the outermost area.

Behind them was the domain where time flowed slowly.

"Go, quickly..."

the Nine-tailed Fox urged in a trembling voice.

Xu Qi'an gave a low "mm" and didn't look back, because he could feel the black hole chasing after them. The devouring force that consumed everything seemed to be right behind him.

The glass bead on his wrist flashed.

Xu Qi'an and the Nine-tailed Fox appeared dozens of metres away. The two of them once more experienced the restriction of "slowness". Even blinking took more than five seconds, and raising a hand took more than ten. Everything had slowed by a factor of ten.

Just as Xu Qi'an raised his hand and prepared to perform a second spatial jump, the two of them clearly felt the space suddenly tremble.

Immediately afterwards, the flow of time in this space began rapidly returning to normal.

The Nine-tailed Fox turned her head and screamed:

"It's caught up..."

The black hole was less than three hundred feet away from them. If it moved forward just a few more yards, it would drag them into the core devouring field. At that point, there would be nothing but death for the two of them.

Xu Qi'an's expression changed slightly, but he didn't turn around. Slowly, he carried out the second spatial jump.

He successfully completed the jump, but the Nine-tailed Fox's sharp voice rang out again, full of fear:

"Run, quickly, it's over..."

The next moment, the force that devoured all things enveloped them.
