# 874. Mahayana Buddhism

"...Since the Buddhist contest, Silver Gong Xu founded Mahayana Buddhism, and its principles have spread widely through the Western Regions, enlightening the people and achieving remarkable achievements. Those in the Western Regions who believed in Mahayana Buddhism number in the millions, and We are greatly heartened.

"Mahayana Buddhism originated in the Central Plains. How could the Central Plains cast it aside? We wished to welcome Mahayana Buddhism back, to instruct the common people and save them from suffering. Arhat Du'e was the monk enlightened by Silver Gong Xu, and his Buddhist teachings were profound. That he was now willing to submit to the court was the good fortune of the people of the Central Plains.

"We hereby confer upon Arhat Du'e the title of National Teacher, and establish Mahayana Buddhism as the state religion...

"Thus it is decreed!"

Inside Jinluan Hall, silence fell at once.

The middle-aged eunuch looked towards the outside of the hall and called loudly:

"Arhat Du'e, step forward at once to receive the decree."

Outside the hall, Arhat Du'e, dressed in a red and yellow kasaya, slowly stepped into the hall. He walked across the crimson carpet, passing between the gathered ministers.

The assembled officials looked at one another, exchanging silent glances. Some were puzzled, some bewildered, some frowned, but not a single one stepped forward to object.

The first thing they realised was that His Majesty wanted to win over Arhat Du'e.

To establish Mahayana Buddhism and appoint him National Teacher, to treat him with such favour, was equivalent to helping Arhat Du'e break away from the Buddhism of the Western Regions and found his own branch, becoming the "Buddha" of the Central Plains.

Then the officials began to consider the possible consequences of establishing Mahayana Buddhism as the state religion, the changes it might bring to the balance of the court, and so on.

Yet still no one stepped forward to object.

Firstly, Buddhist monks had no right to interfere in government affairs, which removed the most important conflict of interest.

Secondly, the defection of a second-rank Arhat was enough to weaken Buddhism's fighting strength. For the current Great Feng, it brought a hundred benefits and not a single harm.

Better to win him over first. As for how to suppress him later, that was a matter for the future.

As scholars who had spent their lives mixing in officialdom, this was precisely the game they excelled at.

Arhat Du'e came to stand beneath the imperial throne, pressed his palms together, and said:

"Many thanks, Your Majesty!"

He calmly accepted the edict.

The instant he took it, the phala behind his head suddenly appeared, releasing boundless Buddhist light. Sanskrit chants echoed through the void, resounding throughout the great hall and in the ears of all the officials.

Arhat Du'e's whole body looked as though it had been cast from gold, radiant and gleaming.

And in Huaiqing's eyes, streams of majestic fortune coiled around his body and attached themselves to his phala--yet did not enter his body!

...

Western Regions!

Wave after wave of "animation" surged forward like the sea, advancing layer by layer. Wherever it passed, the earth was given life, the mountains and rivers were given life, the city walls were given life. All living beings vanished, merging its natural laws.

The Buddha's body had already turned into mountains, rivers, and land. His consciousness extended and expanded along with His flesh, merging into the laws of heaven and earth and becoming part of them, yet retaining His memories.

In a certain sense, He truly had refined the entire Western Regions. He had drawn all of the Western Regions' fortune into Buddhism, and using that as a foundation, was devouring this region's laws of heaven and earth.

If nothing unexpected happened, He would continue spreading and expanding until the entire Western Regions became part of Himself.

But at that very moment, streams of majestic fortune left Him, forcibly torn from His body and drifting east towards the Central Plains.

The Buddha's rate of expansion instantly slowed, then gradually came to a halt. He could no longer assimilate heaven and earth and replace their laws.

His momentum stopped, as if He had lost His strength.

Of course, given His status, even forcibly devouring the laws of heaven and earth was still possible, and He could continue. But without the shelter of fortune, or rather, without fortune as this certificate of legitimacy, there was only one possible end if He pressed on, to follow in the Daoist Venerable's footsteps.

To be assimilated by the rules of heaven and earth, and lose Himself.

After a brief stillness, the land of the Western Regions began to quake violently, like an earthquake stretching for tens of thousands of miles.

Great fissures thousands of feet long split open one after another, lined with dense rows of ghastly white teeth.

The earth had grown mouths.

All of those mouths let out the same furious roar:

"Mahayana Buddhism, Mahayana Buddhism..."

Ananda, that holy mountain, split open a gigantic mouth and gave a roar that shook heaven and earth:

"Mahayana Buddhism..."

The monks on the holy mountain prostrated themselves on the ground in terror, trembling uncontrollably.

Mahayana Buddhism...

Agaruka, Samantabhadra, and Vaidurya Bodhisattva all felt their hearts tighten. Each closed their eyes, as though sensing something, or communicating with someone.

A moment later, the three opened their eyes, understood the cause, and their expressions instantly darkened. Gnashing their teeth, they said:

"Du'e has founded Mahayana Buddhism in the Central Plains!"

Mahayana Buddhism had split away part of Buddhism's fortune.

At such a critical juncture, Mahayana Buddhism had become the stumbling block preventing the Buddha from becoming the Western Regions.

"Back then, I should have crushed his unrealistic delusions, or had Samantabhadra send him into reincarnation."

Agaruka took on the wrathful glare of a Vajra, and behind him appeared the Vajra Dharma Aspect that presided over slaughter.

None of them had thought much of Arhat Du'e, yet they had never expected this insignificant second-rank Arhat to pull the rug out from under them.

Samantabhadra sighed softly, suppressing all surging anger in his heart. In a voice that was indistinguishable in gender, he said:

"As things stand, the only option is to stop for now and use the method of the Way of Fire and Incesnse to refine the remaining territory of the Western Regions into a Mountain and River Seal, and hold it in our grasp."

By doing this, the Buddha would neither have to risk being assimilated by the laws of heaven and earth, nor would He lose control of the remaining lands. In future, once they seized more fortune, He could then devour the Mountain and River Seal.

This move had originally been intended for use against the Central Plains.

...

Mount Jing City.

Sarun Agu stood on the barren main peak of Mount Jing, gazing west.

Suddenly he frowned, pinched his fingers, and performed a brief divination. Then he gave a low chuckle:

"What a clever move. Using Mahayana Buddhism to siphon away the Buddha's fortune and hinder Him from assimilating the Western Regions. It doesn't solve the root problem, but at least it buys time."

His figure flashed, and he appeared atop the altar. Looking at the statue wearing a crown of thorns, he tilted his head to listen for a moment, then bowed and said:

"I think the same."

Sarun Agu drew the shepherd's whip from his waist and lightly struck the ground at his feet.

"Crack!"

With the crisp sound of the whip, black light surged, and Irbu's figure emerged on the altar.

"Great Warlock..."

Irbu thought to himself, *me again?*

Sarun Agu said flatly:

"Bring me the imperial seals of Yan, Kang, and Jing."

Irbu bowed, then turned into a streak of black light and shot towards Mount Jing City in the distance.

A short while later, he returned riding black light once more, holding three palm-sized jade seals in both hands.

Sarun Agu stared at the three jade seals. In a low, aged voice, he slowly said:

"Of the three kingdoms, Jing's iron cavalry had fought in the north for half a year, and over half had been lost. Its national strength had declined somewhat. Kang borders the sea. When Wei Yuan led his army to seize Mount Jing City, he marched through Yan territory, so Kang was never affected and its strength remains largely intact.

"As for Yan, it had first suffered under Wei Yuan's iron cavalry, then the battle for Yuyang Pass. Its national strength had been reduced by seventy to eighty per cent.

"This one, then."

The Great Warlock pointed at Yan's jade seal and, with a pained look on his face, ordered:

"Take it to the Buddhists."

*This...* Irbu was stunned. In disbelief, he said:

"Great Warlock, why send it to the Buddhists?"

The jade seals stored the fortune of the three kingdoms.

Sarun Agu replied irritably:

"Du'e has betrayed them. The Great Feng has made him National Teacher and established Mahayana Buddhism as the state religion, splitting away part of the Buddha's fortune. If He wants to incarnate as the Western Regions, it'll take a good deal more effort now."

Irbu was overjoyed:

"Isn't that a good thing?"

He already knew the secret of the great calamity. Some time ago, the Great Warlock had summoned the Master of Rain Nalan Tianlu, as well as the Masters of Wisdom Irbu and Uda Batu, and told them what the Beyond-ranks sought.

For beyond-mortal beings of the same system as them, once the Warlock God incarnated as the Heavenly Dao, even his dogs and chickens would ascend to heaven. Not only would they become deathless and undying, they would also rule Jiuzhou in the Warlock God's stead and become gods of the mortal world.

Among cultivators of the major systems, the higher the rank, the fainter the emotions.

In Irbu's view, mortals were like weeds. Even if they were wiped out entirely, before long they would sprout again, crop after crop.

Compared to that, the Warlock God replacing the Heavenly Dao and Warlocks becoming eternal and indestructible was the true great enterprise.

Sarun Agu shook his head:

"It's both a good thing and a bad thing. Work it out yourself.

"I'll give you a lift."

The shepherd's whip coiled around Irbu. With a hard swing, a streak of black light shot away like a meteor and vanished into the western horizon.

...

The capital.

Arhat Du'e stood upon a nine-petalled lotus dais and pressed his palms together towards the people behind him.

"Many thanks to all of you for your help."

Master Hengyuan, whose whole body was coated in "gold lacquer" and looked like a golden statue, returned the salute with palms pressed together.

"This concerns all living beings under heaven. It is our duty, Master, you needn't thank us."

After a long period of training under al'Asura, Master Hengyuan had now begun to accomodate the Arhat phala and could briefly borrow the power of the Executioner. In other words, though on the surface he was a fourth-rank Warrior Monk, in secret he was actually a second-rank Arhat.

Though only briefly.

Arhat Du'e looked at Hengyuan with some complexity. This monk who had been left to grow on his own was in fact a natural-born believer in Mahayana Buddhism. If not for his shallow cultivation, or if he had another few decades, perhaps the founder of Mahayana Buddhism would not have been Du'e.

But Hengyuan of Qinglong Temple.

Chu Yuanzhen said sternly:

"This concerns the great calamity. We ought to go and take a look."

Daoist Orange Cat, al'Asura, Li Miaozhen and the other members of the Heaven and Earth Society were all present, along with Sun Xuanji in flowing white robes. The latter had been sent by the new Jianzheng.

Also there was Yeji, with her seductive fox-like face.

The Western Regions were dangerous, and the situation unclear, so naturally they could not let Arhat Du'e take the risk alone. Thus there was now a bodyguard group from the Heaven and Earth Society.

Du'e said in a low voice:

"Once we near the Western Regions, there is no need for you all to enter its territory, in case something goes wrong."

Everyone nodded.

Li Lingsu cupped his hands and said:

"Take care, everyone. If anything seems wrong, run at once.

"Ah, I still think we ought to wait for Xu Ningyan to come back. That crude martial brute isn't the sort to die easily. If you go to the Western Regions, I just feel something will happen."

Li Miaozhen's brows shot up:

"Shut your mouth!"

Lina was much more obedient. Knowing she couldn't help, she merely waved and said nothing.

The group rose on the wind, turning into streams of light as they flew towards the Western Regions.

Watching them leave, Li Lingsu looked at Yeji and said:

"Miss Yeji, shall I go with you to the Southern Marches?"

He felt he ought to do something. This was absolutely not because he wanted to give Jill a break.

Yeji thought for a moment, then looked at Lina.

"Let's go together."

*Can we at least go after supper...* Lina nodded helplessly.

"Alright."

Yeji then took three teleportation jade talismans from her robe and handed them to Li Lingsu and Lina.

They were going to the Southern Marches to see Shenshu and ask him to come out and steady the situation. Although this trip was mainly for reconnaissance, not to wage battle with Buddhism, the situation could change in an instant, and they needed an extra layer of security on their side.

The half-step Martial God, now restored to his peak state, was undoubtedly the best choice.

From the capital to the Southern Marches, there were twelve teleportation formations along the route.

Xu Qi'an had laid down this "courier road" long ago, and now it was finally being put to use.

Under Sun Xuanji's guidance through the teleportation formations, the mountains and plains flashed beneath the feet of the assembled beyond-mortals. Before dusk, they arrived at the border of the Western Regions.

Clear light rose up, and everyone stopped. Sun Xuanji had not brought them recklessly any closer.

Arhat Du'e pressed his palms together and saluted everyone, then swept towards the Western Regions.

He did not fly too far, keeping himself within the sight of the gathered beyond-mortal experts.

After focusing and sensing for a while, Du'e turned around and said:

"There's nothing unusual."

The gaunt old monk frowned slightly. This was not quite what he had expected.

al'Asura and Hengyuan were the first to ride the wind over to Du'e's side. After each probing with divine sense, they confirmed that there was nothing abnormal in this area.

The members of the Heaven and Earth Society, puzzled, continued onwards. Half a quarter of an hour later, they reached a small town. From houses of a style quite different from those of the Central Plains, wisps of cooking smoke rose into the air.

The sounds of chickens and dogs could be heard, and the place was full of the feel of everyday life.

Arhat Du'e pondered and said:

"Perhaps it hasn't spread this far. Let's go deeper and see..."

Following the same pattern as before, with Arhat Du'e taking the lead, they continued deeper into the Western Regions.

After travelling for an hour, Arhat Du'e suddenly stopped.

It was now around half past seven. In the Great Feng, the setting sun would already have sunk below the horizon and night fallen, but in the Western Regions it had only just begun to show signs of dusk.

Ahead lay a boundless plain, and at the end of the plain stood a mountain range.

The river flowed in silence. There was no sign of human life.

Arhat Du'e did not dare go any further. Every cell in his body was screaming for him to flee, every nerve sending signals of danger.

He was of the Buddhist path and did not cultivate a martial artist's danger sense.

This was fortune issuing a warning!

"Danger?"

Al'Asura, nine feet tall and ugly in a strangely handsome way, flew over and stood shoulder to shoulder with Du'e.

He had sensed no crisis. A martial artist's danger sense had not been triggered.

Then al'Asura saw, in the distant mountain range, a pair of eyes open.
