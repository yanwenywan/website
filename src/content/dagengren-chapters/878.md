# 878. Shenshu vs. the Buddha

That humanoid figure made of dark red flesh bent its knees slightly and, amid a thunderous tremor of the ground, shot towards al-Asura at the front.

The fiery halo behind al-Asura's head exploded with a bang, its flames lighting up the darkness. The muscles in his waist and back bulged as he drove his right arm and fist forward, striking straight at the Buddha's incarnation.

He was so confident because he had sensed that this incarnation was not particularly strong, merely a part of the Buddha's vast, ocean-like body.

He might not be able to beat the Buddha, but taking on one incarnation shouldn't have been a problem.

The instant that thought flashed through his mind, al-Asura saw a Dharma Aspect appear behind the "Buddha incarnation", one that battled heaven and earth and symbolised strength and slaughter.

The Vajra Dharma Aspect!

Li Miaozhen's brows twitched. She raised her right hand, palm facing the Buddha incarnation, and lightly wiped through the air.

As though she had wiped something away.

Twelve pairs of arms layered over one another, and twelve pairs of fists came crashing down like a torrential storm.

Twenty-four blows merged into a single sound:

"Clang!"

Al-Asura's fist, imbued with the power of the Executioner Phala, exploded on impact, bone fragments and flesh flying everywhere.

But relying on the hatred in his heart, with the Buddha's fortune weakened, he dealt more damage than expected, staggering the Buddha incarnation backwards.

The dazzling halo of the Executioner Phala spread over the Buddha incarnation's chest, eroding its vitality.

Perhaps because its fortune had been weakened, the power of the Executioner Phala once again achieved striking results. The flesh on the Buddha incarnation's chest dissolved away, corroded into a savage wound.

At that moment, on the left side of the Buddha incarnation, a golden Dharma Aspect condensed, holding a white jade purification vase in its palm, its face serene and compassionate.

Specks of golden light overflowed from the vase. Bathed in them, the Buddha incarnation's dissolved flesh instantly recovered, neutralising the power of the Executioner Phala.

Thud thud thud...

As the team's close-combat mainstay, al-Asura charged at the Buddha incarnation without hesitation. In the process, the Vajra divine art receded, and pitch-black matter crawled over the surface of his body.

He had activated his Asura bloodline.

His combat strength rose another level.

Yet he charged into empty air. The Buddha incarnation vanished from before his eyes, disappearing without warning.

Only the Walker Dharma Aspect could come and go so soundlessly. Even teleportation spells couldn't achieve such utter silence.

The next moment, the Buddha incarnation appeared behind Arhat Du'e.

The chest of the humanoid figure made of flesh suddenly split open into a hideous mouth full of fangs, stretching from chest to abdomen.

Then it collapsed into a curtain and covered Arhat Du'e.

The Buddha's objective was exceptionally clear, to devour Du'e and reclaim the fortune the Buddhist sect had lost.

*Shit...*

Li Miaozhen and Daoist Jinlian both reached out at once, one weakening the Buddha's fortune, the other summoning the "earth aspect" of the Four Dharma Bodies and driving it to intercept the Buddha incarnation.

To the left of the Buddha incarnation, a third Dharma Aspect now appeared, hands pressed together, head lowered in meditation, with a benevolent and compassionate face, the Great Compassion Dharma Aspect.

The moment it appeared, Buddhist light flooded the sky and Sanskrit chants rang out.

Compassion inevitably welled up in the hearts of Li Miaozhen and Daoist Jinlian. They could no longer make a move, and the wind aspect immediately collapsed into a hazy cloud of dust.

At that very moment, a purple-gold hammer shot in from the side and struck Arhat Du'e with a clang, knocking him flying.

The curtain formed by the Buddha incarnation enveloped the purple-gold hammer and devoured it.

Arhat Du'e narrowly escaped the jaws of death.

*Whew...* Everyone let out a breath of relief, their hearts still pounding. If Du'e had been devoured by the Buddha, then all their painstaking planning and the immense cost spent supporting Mahayana Buddhism would have become meaningless.

And the Buddha, having gained that enormous fortune, would have become even more terrifying.

*Too dangerous. We can't stop the Buddha. We can't even defeat one of His incarnations. We have to withdraw. But, but all those innocent lives, we can't just let Him go on devouring them...*

Li Miaozhen slowly let out a breath, only to suddenly realise that it was unnaturally long.

She had been exhaling for ages and still hadn't finished.

She immediately sensed that something was wrong. At the same time, the scenery before her lost all colour and turned into pure black and white.

*This is... the Colourless Lapis Lazuli Dharma Aspect... the Buddha really can use every Dharma Aspect...*

Li Miaozhen's thoughts became uncontrollably sluggish. Bit by bit, panic and fear appeared on her face.

Similar expressions appeared on the faces of Daoist Orange Cat, Sun Xuanji, and the others.

Despair began fermenting and spreading through everyone's hearts.

The Buddha incarnation slowly turned and looked towards Arhat Du'e trapped within the colourless domain. Its chest split open again into a gash stretching to the abdomen, lined with fangs, saliva pouring out like rain.

In the next instant, its figure manifested behind Arhat Du'e once more, transforming again into a curtain that descended over him.

At the critical moment, a great hand descended from the sky and slammed the curtain down. With a boom, the ground shook violently.

Along with that earth-shaking impact, the Colourless Lapis Lazuli domain collapsed with a crash.

Colour returned to the world, and Li Miaozhen and the others regained their mobility.

They all turned to look. The reinforcements descending from the sky was a young monk with firm, well-defined features, dressed in an azure kasaya, with six neatly arranged burn marks on his shaven head.

The Buddha's incarnation struggled violently in his hand, but could not budge him in the slightest, like a child pinned to the ground by an adult.

"Master Shenshu?"

Li Miaozhen asked tentatively.

Al-Asura's expression was somewhat complicated.

Shenshu nodded and gave an "Mm". Then he lifted his foot and stamped down. The incarnation formed of flesh matter exploded into powder with a bang.

...

"As expected of one half-step from Martial God."

In the night sky far away, Sarun Agu looked down on the scene and sighed.

Not far from the beyond-mortal experts of the Church of the Warlock God stood the leaders of the shaman clans. Chunyan's attention was not on the half-step Martial God who had emerged out of nowhere, but on the overwhelming tide of dark red flesh that was devouring everything in its path.

"Grandma, what is the Buddha doing?"

The two slender snakes hanging from Chunyan's earlobes hissed in agreement with their mistress.

Heaven Gu Grandma frowned for a moment, then shook her head slightly.

She did not know the truth of the great calamity.

Sarun Agu said with a smile:

"He wants to seize Leizhou and refine a Mountain and River Seal."

The other leaders adopted attentive expressions.

"Seizing territory and condensing a Mountain and River Seal means bringing that region under one's control. For every province the Great Feng loses, a portion of its fortune drains away, until the nation falls.

"And when that happens, the Buddha can devour the Central Plains fortune dispersed throughout Jiuzhou, and devour the Mountain and River Seals."

Then replace the land of the Central Plains, just as He had replaced the Western Regions.

In essence, this was no different from sending troops to attack the Great Feng and destroy the Central Plains dynasty. The method was simply different.

The Buddha had no need of an army. He Himself was an army of myriads.

Chunyan keenly asked, "And what's the purpose of doing that?"

Sarun Agu did not answer. Instead he looked down below and continued watching the battle.

Baji, chief of the Poison Gu tribe, said in a deep voice:

"Do you still remember the prophecy of the Heaven Gu tribe's seer? On the day the Gu God revives, Jiuzhou will become a world of Gu.

"Doesn't that mean if the Gu God breaks free of the seal, It will do the same as the Buddha?"

Hearing this, the expressions of the various chiefs grew grave.

...

"Shenshu!"

The oceanic mass of flesh split open one mouth after another, all speaking with the same voice.

Immediately after, each mouth spat out a fist-sized sphere of light, like miniature suns.

These shrunken suns burst with Buddhist light that purified all things, sending the elements of heaven and earth into slumber and rapidly weakening every power that did not belong to Buddhism.

When Daoist Orange Cat and the others were illuminated by the Buddhist light, wisps of blue smoke rose from their bodies and their cultivation rapidly weakened.

Only Hengyuan, Du'e, and al-Asura remained completely unharmed.

"Retreat!"

Jinlian shouted.

This was the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect.

Without the slightest hesitation, everyone retreated at full speed.

On the other side, Shenshu, in his pale azure kasaya, stood unmoving in the Buddhist light. He stared directly at the dazzling radiance, stretched out his right hand, and clenched it abruptly.

Bang bang bang...

One miniature sun after another exploded, collapsing into pure halos of energy.

The Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect had been broken before it could even fully take shape, but the Buddha did not care. The dark red flesh matter continued advancing. He had not yet reached His limit and could still cover an even larger area.

Until He was "full", after which He would refine the occupied territory into a Mountain and River Seal and strip it away from the lands of the Great Feng.

Shenshu glanced at the sea-tide-like flesh matter spreading towards him, considered for a moment, then took the initiative to step into it.

The flesh matter parted of its own accord, as though welcoming his arrival. Shenshu walked forwards step by step. Behind him, the flesh matter spread back together, cutting off his retreat.

The flesh matter surged like thick syrup, trying to engulf Shenshu.

But when it came within ten feet of him, it was repelled by his vast and powerful aura. Within ten feet, nothing could enter.

Just as a martial artist cut himself off from heaven and earth, no longer resonating with the outside world, becoming a self-contained cycle.

The dark red flesh matter resembled a storm-tossed sea, raising an enormous wave. That wave condensed into a figure hundreds of feet tall, seated while holding a flower, silently gazing at Shenshu.

...

Huaiqing kept trying to contact Xu Qi'an, but never got a response.

Instead, she received a reply from Li Miaozhen:

【TWO: We're fighting on the front lines, and you're dragging us down from the rear. Well done, you.】

Huaiqing found herself speechless. Although she knew Li Miaozhen was venting some displaced anger, at their level of cultivation the slight heart-lurch caused by transmitted messages would not create any real interference.

But then again, Li Miaozhen really was fighting on the front line. Even the Emperor dared not offend generals shedding blood and risking their lives in battle.

【ONE: We were not thoughtful enough. How's the situation?】

Huaiqing could bend when needed.

【FOUR: Master Shenshu has arrived. The situation's stable for now. He's currently fighting the Buddha.】

They then discussed the Buddha's true aim in attacking Leizhou, concluding that this would most likely also be the method the Warlock God would use to erode the Central Plains after breaking free.

【NINE: A single Buddha is already this dangerous. If the Warlock God breaks free as well, the Central Plains will be attacked from both front and rear. What are we supposed to do?】

Li Lingsu rubbed his brow and replied from the Southern Marches:

【SEVEN: Daoist, stop talking. You're only making everyone more anxious.】

This time, the former fledgling phoenix of the Heaven Sect unusually did not criticise her senior brother, because he was right.
