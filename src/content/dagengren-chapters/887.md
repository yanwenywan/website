# 887. Subduing Three Kingdoms

No sooner had she asked the question than she had her answer. A name surfaced in her mind--Xu Qi’an!

Across Jiuzhou, among those who bore a grudge against the Church of the Warlock God, and who had grown to the point that even the Warlock God could no longer suppress them, there was only that newly advanced first rank martial artist.

Dongfang Wanrong had seen with her own eyes Xu Qi’an come knocking at their door.

"But the last time I saw him come to collect a debt, the Great Warlock drove him back," Dongfang Wanrong voiced her doubt.

Even the Great Warlock had managed to drive him back then, let alone now, when the Warlock God had further broken free of His seal and could wield far greater power than when He had only just begun to escape it.

With the Warlock God and the Great Warlock holding Mount Jing City, even if Xu Qi’an was a first rank martial artist, he shouldn't have made the Great Warlock so wary.

"Besides, a while ago I heard Elder Uda Batu say that martial artist had already gone out to sea," someone else said.

That ruled out the possibility of the enemy being Xu Qi’an.

It made sense too. A first rank martial artist was indeed lofty and untouchable to them, but to the Warlock God and the Great Warlock, he might not have been all that formidable.

If the enemy was Xu Qi’an, things shouldn't have escalated to this extent.

"Could it be... the Buddha?"

One Warlock put forward a bold guess.

The moment he said it, he saw the hooded heads around him all twist towards him, pairs of eyes staring fixedly at him.

The expressions of his fellow disciples roughly said, "Don't talk rubbish", "That actually makes sense", "What a jinx", "Have you gone mad?" and so on.

"But if it isn't the Buddha, then who could make the Warlock God and the Great Warlock so wary?" Dongfang Wanrong said softly.

The news that the beyond-mortal experts of the Great Feng had fought the Buddhist sect at Ananda several months ago had long since made its way back to the Church of the Warlock God.

It was said that the Buddha had broken free of His seal even earlier than the Warlock God.

The cultivators of the Warlock system were unwilling to admit it, but it seemed that the Buddha was somewhat stronger than the Warlock God.

For a moment, no one spoke. The expressions of the surrounding Warlocks all turned rather ugly.

After a while, one Warlock muttered in a low voice:

"The Great Warlock summoned all of us to gather in Mount Jing City to help the Warlock God resist the Buddha?"

If that was true, the casualties would certainly be devastating.

As all manner of thoughts rose among the Warlocks, some alarmed, some afraid, Sarun Agu, the Great Warlock seated cross-legged atop the altar beside the statue of the Warlock God, suddenly rose to his feet.

By his side, the Rain Master Nalan Tianlu, and the two Masters of Wisdom Irbu and Uda Batu, also stood up and took their place shoulder to shoulder with the Great Warlock. The four beyond-mortal figures of the Church of the Warlock God all looked southwards, that is, behind the gathered Warlocks.

"Lively, isn't it."

A clear voice rang out and echoed through the night.

The expressions of the sisters Dongfang Wanrong and Dongfang Wanqing changed. The voice was incomparably familiar, and they had heard it more than once.

The Warlocks all abruptly turned their heads and saw, beneath the silver full moon, a young man in an indigo robe striding through the air.

Xu Qi’an!

*It really is him...* Dongfang Wanrong's expression was slightly blank. She had never imagined that the person who made the Great Warlock so wary, who had prompted such a great mobilisation, really was Xu Qi’an.

She looked again at her younger sister and found her expression much the same, shock mixed with bewilderment.

*Xu Qi’an?!* Several thousand Warlocks turned their heads in unison and looked up at the sky behind them, seeing that lofty young man.

Who in Jiuzhou today didn't recognise this legendary martial artist?

But it was actually him, the enemy who made the Warlock God and the Great Warlock so wary that they had gathered all Warlocks in Mount Jing City at any cost. It was actually Xu Qi’an.

Was he worthy?

Could a mere first rank martial artist really force the Church of the Warlock God to this point?

The Warlocks couldn't accept this fact. As they looked left and right, searching for any other possible enemy lurking nearby, they also pricked up their ears and listened silently to what the Great Warlock and the legendary martial artist would say.

"Sarun Agu, ever since I killed Zhende back then, you've targeted me at every turn. Yesterday, when I fought the Buddha at the border of Leizhou, your Church of the Warlock God was still fanning the flames behind the scenes. Did you ever think there'd be a reckoning like today!"

Xu Qi’an's clear and calm voice rang in every Warlock's ears.

Several thousand Warlocks heard it perfectly clearly. The first thing they confirmed was that Xu Qi’an really had come for revenge, because the Great Warlock had repeatedly offended him in the past.

But the words that followed were beyond them.

*What is he talking about, fighting the Buddha at the border of Leizhou? Xu Qi’an fought the Buddha at the border of Leizhou? Isn't he a first rank martial artist? Since when could a first rank fight a Beyond-rank...* Questions surged wildly through the Warlocks' minds.

To ordinary cultivators, first rank experts were beings far beyond reach, but Beyond-rank beings were gods in people's eyes.

Anyone with some experience and knowledge knew there was an unbridgeable gulf between the two.

Boom!

Dark clouds crowded the night sky and covered the full moon.

The Great Warlock stood at the edge of the altar, arms spread wide, connecting with the power of heaven and earth in this region.

Bolts of lightning as thick as water vats descended one after another, striking at the martial artist in the sky. The whole world seemed to reject him, resist him, and seek to slay and subdue him.

Under this heavenly might, the Warlocks trembled, but they also gained a little more confidence and assurance.

This was their Great Warlock.

The world was instantly filled with a blazing white light, and the lightning pillars twisted and danced wildly.

Faced with this overwhelming heavenly punishment, Xu Qi’an raised a hand and gave a light grasp. In an instant, darkness returned to heaven and earth, and the dark clouds dispersed.

And in Xu Qi’an's palm there was now an orb of lightning, its surface crackling with arcs while its core blazed white-hot.

"Sarun Agu, the you of now still falls a bit short!"

He clenched his palm and extinguished the lightning orb. Then his waist and back tightened, his right arm drew back, and intricate, profound patterns that made one's eyes swim lit up across his skin.

The space around his fist rapidly twisted, as if it couldn't bear the pressure and was about to shatter.

Xu Qi’an threw a punch through the air, and the force of the blow produced a shrill sonic boom.

A martial artist's attack was plain and unadorned.

But the Warlocks below saw with their own eyes that the space before the Great Warlock shattered like a mirror, while deep rumbling sounds echoed through the void.

It was common knowledge that a first rank Great Warlock could draw upon the power of heaven and earth to fight enemies and was innately placed in an invincible position.

Experts of the same level would find it hard to injure a Great Warlock unless they refined the very heaven and earth of that region.

Sarun Agu had used this move against the Jianzheng, and against Wei Yuan at the height of his powers. He had never failed.

"Pff..."

But this time, the first rank ability of the Warlock system seemed to have failed. Sarun Agu spat out a mist of blood, his body bent forward, and his legs slid backwards across the ground.

Dark red blood clung thickly to his dense beard.

The Great Warlock's face rapidly withered, his eyeballs shot through with blood, like an old man whose oil had run dry and lamp had guttered out.

Sarun Agu sat cross-legged, and bursts of blood light rose around him as he swiftly expelled the invading qi from his body and repaired his injuries.

He didn't try to counterattack with a curse of death, because that was destined not to harm a half-step Martial God.

A clamour broke out at once.

The Warlocks below had witnessed the scene with their own eyes, yet none of them dared believe what they had seen.

One punch, just one punch, and a first rank Warlock had been heavily injured.

Was that something a first rank martial artist could do?

Then they thought of what Xu Qi’an had just said, I fought the Buddha at the border of Leizhou.

They suddenly understood. They understood why the Great Warlock had been so wary. The martial artist before them had reached a realm of cultivation far beyond their imagination.

And it had only been a few short months...

Faced with a legendary figure like this, if they had chosen to make him an enemy, then back then they ought to have erased him at all costs. Otherwise there would sooner or later be backlash. No, the backlash had already come...

What realm was he in now...

All sorts of thoughts surged through the Warlocks' hearts.

The Dongfang sisters exchanged horrified glances. Each saw fear and shock in the other's eyes. At the same time, Dongfang Wanrong saw that the Warlock God beside her was trembling slightly from fear.

After injuring the Great Warlock with one punch, Xu Qi’an didn't attack again at once. Instead he raised his voice and said:

"Warlock God!

"Do you believe I'll smash all your disciples and descendants to death with a single punch?!"

As his words fell, the statue wearing the crown of thorns gave a buzzing tremor. Thick black mist, viscous as oil, gushed forth and suddenly spread high in the sky, forming a curtain that blotted out the full moon.

Behind that curtain, a pair of cold eyes opened, gazing upon the whole world.

Xu Qi’an didn't try to kill the several thousand Warlocks below, because he knew it was doomed to fail. The moment he entered the bounds of Mount Jing City, this patch of heaven and earth had already fused into one with the Warlock God.

Trying to kill under the Warlock God's gaze was extraordinarily difficult.

That punch just now had worked on Sarun Agu, presumably because the Warlock God had been assessing his combat strength.

"The Warlock God above us!"

Several thousand Warlocks bowed down in worship.

A powerful sense of security surged up in their hearts once more, and they no longer feared the pressure of a half-step Martial God.

"My turn to test you now!"

The crude martial artist showed not the slightest reverence towards a Beyond-rank existence. The intricate and profound patterns once more crawled across his entire body, his skin turned blood-red, and blood mist sprayed from his pores. In an instant, he seemed to have become the very embodiment of strength.

The space within thirty feet around him twisted violently, as if unable to bear his power.

From the oily, sky-covering curtain there emerged nine figures. Their faces were blurred, but each one brimmed with terrifying might, and their vast auras pressed down over heaven and earth.

Nine first rank martial artists.

These were first rank martial artists the Warlock God had slain or targeted throughout endless ages of the past.

At this moment He had summoned them through the ability of fifth rank, "Blessing Ritual".

In theory, the Warlock God could also have summoned the first Jianzheng and the Confucian Sage. He had deep ties with both of them too, except that the existence of the first Jianzheng had already been erased from the root by the current Jianzheng.

As for summoning the Confucian Sage, the Confucian Sage might well have turned around and dealt a crushing blow to the "summoner".

Xu Qi’an stretched out his right arm, palm facing the heroic spirits of the nine first rank martial artists, and clenched hard.

Bang bang bang...

The nine first rank martial artists exploded one after another, reverting into pure black mist and returning to the curtain that covered the heavens and blotted out the sun.

The martial artist heroic spirits summoned by the Warlocks only possessed their original owners' strength, defence, and abilities below the beyond-mortal realm.

They lacked both the toughness of an undying body and the intent of the Hedao realm.

And if it was purely a contest of strength, then after devouring the spiritual essence of god and demon, Xu Qi’an could take on ten first rank martial artists.

One had to know that even among half-step Martial Gods, Xu Qi’an was outstanding. At the very least, even Shenshu's strength fell short of his.

The next moment, a great clang rang out from Xu Qi’an's chest, like metal and stone colliding.

His chest caved in.

Using the "fall" of the nine heroic spirits, the Warlock God had struck him with a crurse of death.

To deform by brute force the body of a half-step Martial God, that power would have been enough to grievously wound any first rank.

As expected of a Beyond-rank. With a casual spell, He could temporarily strip any first rank other than a martial artist of combat ability... Xu Qi’an gained a preliminary judgement of the Warlock God's power.

It was not much different from the Buddha when he had rescued Shenshu, but it fell short of the Buddha now, who had already transformed into the entire Western Regions.

Snap!

He snapped his fingers.

The next moment, the sticky curtain blanketing the sky shook violently and began to boil, as though it had suffered a heavy blow.

Broken Jade!

He had returned the injuries inflicted on him by the Warlock God in full.

The Warlock God did not continue casting curse-killing spells, because they would just be reflected again by Broken Jade, and then He would cast another curse of death, and so the cycle would continue endlessly through generation after generation. There was no meaning in it.

The oily curtain slowly descended, covering the several thousand Warlocks around the altar.

The Great Warlock stood up and said slowly:

"Xu Qi’an, you can't stop the great calamity. The day the Warlock God breaks free of His seal will be the day the great calamity descends.

"You can change paths and cultivate the Warlock system instead. That way you can protect those around you. Only by joining hands with the Warlock God can you resist the other four Beyond-rank beings."

Xu Qi’an said flatly:

"Sod off!

"The three nations of Yan, Kang, and Jing are under my control now. That's the price your Church of the Warlock God must pay."

The curtain slowly contracted and returned into the statue wearing the thorny crown.

Several thousand Warlocks, including Sarun Agu, Nalan Tianlu, and the two Masters of Wisdom, all merged into the Warlock God's body.

This was the Warlock God's protection of them, sparing them from the reckoning of a half-step Martial God.

But within the borders of the three nations, including Mount Jing City right before his eyes, there were not only Warlocks. There were far more ordinary people and ordinary martial artists.

Those people the Warlock God could not protect.

The Church of the Warlock God had effectively surrendered the vast north-east with both hands. That was the price Xu Qi’an had spoken of, one they had no choice but to pay.

Of course, to the Warlock God, fortune had already been condensed and stored in the imperial jade seal. For the short term, territory no longer mattered much.

Once He broke through, He could absorb that fortune and devour the lands of the three nations.

"Without the Church of the Warlock God, the three nations of Yan, Kang, and Jing can be absorbed into the territory of the Great Feng. With these millions of people, the Great Feng's fortune will certainly rise with the tide. For now, that's a good thing. I should inform Huaiqing and have her take over the three nations in the shortest time possible."

Population represented fortune.

The fortune of the three nations of Yan, Kang, and Jing was already gone, so their only possible end was to be absorbed into the Great Feng. From now on, the three nations would cease to exist.

The workings of fate were indeed inscrutable.

Just then, Xu Qi’an saw that one figure below had not left.

She had delicate features and a graceful figure, and she was an old acquaintance too.

The Holy Son's former lover, Dongfang Wanqing.

Because she was a martial artist, the Warlock God had not taken her away, and at this moment she stood there blankly, at a complete loss.

"I'll bring her back to the capital and give her to Li Lingsu. Consider it a little present. Holy Son, do take care of your kidneys."

Xu Qi’an took out the Earth Book fragment and sent a message:

【THREE: Everyone, I'm in Mount Jing City.】
