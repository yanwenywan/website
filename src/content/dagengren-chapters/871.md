# 871. The Secret of the Great Calamity (Complete)

*Crude martial artist...* A faintly reproachful look appeared on the Nine-tailed Fox's face as she shot him a sidelong glance full of irresistible charm.

Xu Qi’an had once again suffered for his "lack of education". He didn't understand the least bit of these abstruse theories and philosophies, and there certainly hadn't been any such lessons in the nine years of compulsory education in his previous life.

So in that respect, he truly was a crude martial artist, through and through.

"Supreme detachment from emotion is Heaven, great virtue bearing all things is Earth."

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox gave a brief and pithy answer.

*Quite clever, using the Heaven Sect's mental method so neatly to sum up "Heaven",* Xu Qi’an thought to himself with a slight nod.

"But that is only the Heaven and Earth you now see. In truth, the laws of Heaven and Earth had always been changing. Just as the mortal world saw seas turn into mulberry fields, every thousand years brought a wholly new scene."

The Jianzheng spoke with feeling,

"At a moment like this, what one needs is a jug of wine."

"You can't drink it anyway," Xu Qi’an snapped back irritably.

At that, the Jianzheng looked disappointed.

Xu Qi’an then turned to the alluring silver-haired beauty with fox ears beside him and said,

"But we can."

He immediately took out a jar of wine from the Earth Book fragment, and he and the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox shared it between them, one mouthful each.

The disappointment on the Jianzheng's face instantly turned into fury.

He let out a sigh, looked away from the shameless couple, cast his gaze into the distance, and slowly said:

"When Heaven and Earth were first opened, all was dim, uncivilised, barren and desolate. Apart from the lofty, boundless sky and the bleak, deathly earth, there was nothing at all in Jiuzhou. Perhaps it was simply too desolate. After countless years had passed, the first god-demon was born.

"Then more and more gods and demons were brought forth, and thus life appeared. Every god and demon possessed terrifying might, each holding authority over the power of a certain domain between Heaven and Earth.

"The source of that power was what you often call spiritual essence.

"It seemed that this world had also found its own future, and living beings began to appear. As a result, more and more races emerged.

"But if you are familiar with ancient history, then you will know that in the distant past, the human race and the Yao race possessed no supernatural abilities at all. They couldn't cultivate. They could only steal a trace of power from the gods and demons by peering into the spiritual essence of gods and demons.

"Or, by offering women and breeding with gods and demons, they could produce descendants who possessed part of that spiritual essence."

"Couldn't cultivate?" Xu Qi’an sharply caught the oddity in the Jianzheng's words.

Long ago, he had already learned that in ancient times, the human and Yao races had mastered supernatural power by studying and imitating the patterns formed from the spiritual essence of gods and demons.

But that was because the human and Yao races were latecomers. They hadn't yet discovered cultivation systems of their own, so they could only study and imitate gods and demons.

Only later had they gradually worked out cultivation systems, such as the earliest "Martial arts" and "Dao", and later "Buddhist", "Warlock", "Confucian", and "Arcanist".

But what the Jianzheng had said was--they couldn't cultivate!

Not "they didn't know how to cultivate".

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox frowned. Clearly, she too had noticed the issue.

The Jianzheng swept his gaze over the two of them and asked:

"You have both dealt with Desolation. How strong was this ancient god-demon?"

*What a pointless question...* Xu Qi’an said:

"Very strong. Strong enough to make one afraid."

The Jianzheng asked again:

"In the ancient war, Its spiritual essence was damaged and It fell from Its peak. For countless years, It had exhausted Itself trying to repair Its spiritual essence, yet only now had It seen hope.

"Did It never think of cultivating the human race's systems, to strengthen Its own foundations?"

Without waiting for them to answer, the Jianzheng gave the answer himself:

"Because gods and demons could not cultivate. Whatever realm they were born into was the realm they remain in."

Xu Qi’an froze for a moment, then at once looked towards the Nine-tailed Fox and found an expression of sudden understanding on her face.

He could not help recalling that the fox spirit had once told him that the cultivation methods of the descendants of gods and demons differed from those of the human and Yao races.

Having grasped that point, Xu Qi’an combined it with what the Jianzheng had just said and guessed:

"In that age, only beings like gods and demons, born of Heaven and Earth, could command supernatural power. No matter what methods the human race used, they couldn't cultivate, unless they imitated gods and demons?"

The Jianzheng nodded.

"At that time, the powers of Heaven and Earth, such as yin and yang and the five elements, were all in a state of chaos. At times the sun would hang in the sky for decades, even centuries. At times the moon alone was the only source of light in the heavens.

"In some places there was no power of fire spirit at all, and fire could never be lit. In other places, flames burned through every inch of space, eternal and unquenchable. That was the ancient age."

*No wonder other living beings couldn't cultivate. Chaotic and disordered spiritual power simply can't be used, and only gods and demons, who were born with control of laws, could wield supernatural power. But their power was innate, fixed from birth...* Xu Qi’an suddenly understood.

Then he heard the Nine-tailed Fox murmur under her breath:

"The deathly stillness after Heaven and Earth were opened, then the ancient age of chaotic and disordered spiritual power, and then the ordered yin and yang, the differentiated five elements of the present..."

The Jianzheng smiled.

"The world we live in did not come into being all at once. It had evolved step by step.

"From the moment a living being is born, it inevitably moves towards ageing; from the moment a dynasty is established, it likewise inevitably moves towards destruction.

"This world is no different. The barrenness at the opening of Heaven and Earth, the chaos of the age of gods and demons, and the present cycle of sun and moon, with the four seasons in order, are all results of evolution.

"'Evolution' is a law of the Great Dao. All things without exception must follow this law.

"From the opening of Heaven and Earth to the age of gods and demons was the first evolution of this world. But when it tried to evolve towards the second stage..."

At this point, Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox exchanged a glance, realising the truly important part was coming.

The Jianzheng said softly:

"It failed."

A string of question marks flashed through their minds. They couldn't understand.

"It failed?"

The Jianzheng gave a slight nod.

"Because Heaven and Earth were flawed, incomplete, and thus unable to pass from chaos into order. In other words, this world ought to have come to an abrupt halt in the chaotic and disordered ancient age, and remained that way until now."

The Nine-tailed Fox frowned in thought.

"Why would that be?"

The Jianzheng looked at the two of them and asked:

"If Heaven and Earth were flawed, where did the flaw lie?"

After a brief pause, Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox's pupils contracted, their breathing quickened, and they spoke in unison:

"The gods and demons?!"

The Jianzheng inclined his head.

"That's right. Gods and demons were born of Heaven and Earth and held authority over part of the laws of Heaven and Earth. Therefore, the Heavenly Dao of this world was incomplete. Part of its authority was in the hands of gods and demons. If it wished to continue evolving, it had to reclaim the spiritual essence and mend what was lacking.

"This is part of the truth of the first great calamity."

*So that was why the gods and demons had met their end. No wonder the Hell Silkworm had said that on that day, the gods and demons had suddenly gone mad and begun slaughtering one another. No wonder it had been so difficult for Desolation to repair Its spiritual essence, because the part It had lost had already returned to this world...* Countless thoughts surged through Xu Qi’an's mind.

*The end of the gods and demons had come because Heaven and Earth reclaimed the spiritual essence to repair itself...* The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox only felt as if a great knot in her thoughts had suddenly come undone, and a question that had long troubled her was finally answered.

But immediately, even more questions surged forth.

She had just opened her mouth to ask when she heard Xu Qi’an say:

"Part of the truth?

"And you mean that the gods and demons were affected by the laws of Heaven and Earth, lost their sanity, slaughtered one another, and after death their spiritual essence returned to Heaven and Earth?"

After Xu Qi’an finished asking, the Nine-tailed Fox softly added:

"And what has all this got to do with that gate of light?"

The Jianzheng considered his words for a moment, then said:

"Your account is not quite accurate. The laws of Heaven and Earth would not act with such initiative. They would not consciously influence gods and demons in order to reclaim spiritual essence. There was another reason the gods and demons slaughtered one another. That is also why I said that reclaiming spiritual essence is only part of the truth."

Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox both held their breath and listened intently.

The Jianzheng continued:

"I said before that evolution is a law of the Great Dao, and nothing can escape it. Heaven and Earth were flawed, and so unable to carry evolution further. Thus, at the end of the ancient age, a 'gate' appeared.

"It was this gate that drove all gods and demons mad."

The Nine-tailed Fox said instinctively:

"The gate on Gods and Demons Island."

Xu Qi’an, meanwhile, followed the thought and looked towards the Taiping Sabre laid across the Jianzheng's knees.

*What did this gate symbolise? Why had it driven the gods and demons mad?* He had a feeling that what the Jianzheng was about to say next was the truly crucial point.

The Jianzheng sighed and said:

"This gate was the condensation of the laws of Heaven and Earth. It was the embodiment of the concept of reclaiming spiritual essence. Once spiritual essence passed through this gate, it could return to Heaven and Earth and complete what was lacking. But this also meant that, through this gate, one could touch the most fundamental core level of this world."

"The most fundamental level?!" Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox both felt their hearts jolt. For no reason at all, they felt as if their blood were speeding up and their heartbeats quickening.

The Jianzheng's tone suddenly grew heavy as he slowly said:

"You may understand it as becoming the master of this world, becoming... the Heavenly Dao!"

Boom!

It was as though thunder had exploded in their minds. The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox turned pale with shock, seemingly unable to accept such a revelation, and said in a trembling voice:

"How can that be..."

The Jianzheng said gravely:

"The gods and demons did have such qualification, because they themselves were manifestations of the laws of Heaven and Earth. To put it more accurately, the laws of Heaven and Earth had given birth to consciousness and become living beings.

"When the 'gate' descended into the world, and Heaven and Earth reclaimed spiritual essence, the restraints limiting the gods and demons were opened. They discovered that they could strengthen themselves by seizing one another's spiritual essence, and that they could pass through the gate and replace the unconscious laws of Heaven and Earth, becoming the Heavenly Dao.

"So they went mad. Thus began the cruellest slaughter since the opening of Heaven and Earth."

At that instant, countless flashes of insight darted through Xu Qi’an's mind, and innumerable thoughts surged up. Many details and clues all linked together at that very moment:

No wonder the gods and demons could turn into islands after death. They had originally been part of heaven and earth itself. The opportunity for the laws of heaven and earth to perfect themselves could likewise be their opportunity to "perfect" themselves and replace the laws of heaven and earth.

*I understand now. I understand the true meaning of the prophecy left behind by the seer of the Heaven Gu tribe, when the Gu God awakens, the world will turn into a world of Gu!*

*The real meaning of that sentence is that the Gu God would devour the laws of heaven and earth and become the Heavenly Dao!*

*Then this world would become a world of Gu.*

*No wonder the Buddha had become Ananda, and Ananda had become the Buddha. Does that mean He had obtained the same characteristics as the gods and demons of old? Has He gained the qualifications to contend for the Heavenly Dao?*

*So I'd already known the true face of the great calamity all along. I'd known the truth. The more one thinks about it, the more terrifying it gets...* Xu Qi’an took a deep breath and said:

"Then why did all the gods and demons fail?"

the Jianzheng said:

"Because the 'door' had preliminarily perfected itself. It no longer needed spiritual essence, and the gods and demons failed to push it open before it completed itself. Heaven and earth then evolved into the next stage, and so the gods and demons lost the qualification to push it open.

"Even today, even if Desolation truly pushed it open, It still couldn't become the Heavenly Dao.

"As for what use it has, once the Taiping Sabre has digested it, you'll naturally understand."

Xu Qi’an looked towards the Taiping Sabre again, while the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox asked:

"Jianzheng, is the second great calamity also a struggle for the Heavenly Dao?"

Xu Qi’an withdrew his gaze and looked at the Jianzheng.

The latter stroked his long beard and remained silent for a long while, no one knew what he was thinking about. Then he said:

"After the age of gods and demons ended, heaven and earth evolved into the third stage. Order gradually appeared in the world, spiritual energy was born, and the human and Yao races rose accordingly.

"Although the first great calamity had already ended, its influence remained. The appearance of the gate of light told the surviving beings one thing, the Heavenly Dao could be replaced.

"But unlike in the age of gods and demons, for later generations, the only way to become the Heavenly Dao was to advance to Beyond-rank. Yet after reaching Beyond-rank, how was one to replace the Heavenly Dao? No one knew, until many years later, when the Daoist Venerable appeared.

"This was a figure whose aptitude ranked among the top three since the creation of heaven and earth."

Xu Qi’an suddenly had a thought, *what would've happened if the Daoist Venerable at his peak had met the Confucian Sage...*

Unfortunately, the two Beyond-ranks hadn't lived in the same era.

Although the Buddha had been the Human Sect aspect of the Daoist Venerable, he clearly didn't count as the true the Daoist Venerable.

"After the Daoist Venerable advanced to Beyond-rank, he began exploring ways to replace the Heavenly Dao. Since he was crossing the river by feeling for stones, he naturally had to be prepared to fail. But this matter was perilous, and even he didn't dare say he could definitely retreat unscathed.

"Thus, the technique of One Qi Transforming into the Three Purities was born."

When Xu Qi’an heard this, the many clues from the past suddenly connected, bringing with them a shuddering sensation.

He opened his mouth, but in the end still didn't interrupt, and patiently listened as the Jianzheng continued.

"Drawing on the old affairs of the gods and demons, the Daoist Venerable came up with a method. Since gods and demons had been formed from the rules of heaven and earth, and thus could replace the Heavenly Dao and become the Heavenly Dao, then why couldn't the human race imitate the gods and demons and turn themselves into a part of the laws of heaven and earth?"

The moment the Jianzheng finished speaking, the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox murmured:

"The Heaven Sect mental cultivation method, Supreme Detachment from Emotion!"

the Jianzheng nodded.

"That's right. The Heaven Sect's Supreme Detachment from Emotion and the unity of heaven and man were originally mental cultivation methods created by the Daoist Venerable in order to imitate the gods and demons and replace the Heavenly Dao."

The Nine-tailed Fox sighed.

"But He failed."

the Jianzheng shook his head and said:

"He both succeeded and failed.

"He succeeded in that he truly merged into the Heavenly Dao and became part of the laws of heaven and earth, just like the gods and demons of those years.

"He failed in that heaven and earth had already evolved into the third stage, and the methods of the gods and demons were no longer applicable. Like the gods and demons of old, he turned into a part of the laws of heaven and earth.

"The path of Supreme Detachment from Emotion wouldn't work, so the Daoist Venerable turned his attention to the Way of Fire and Incense. You can understand that, yes?"

Xu Qi’an let out a breath, his tone low:

"The Way of Fire and Incense refines the power of mountains, rivers, and ley lines. Within one's own territory, one is virtually invincible, undying and imperishable. the Daoist Venerable believed that if he refined the heaven and earth of Jiuzhou, he could replace the Heavenly Dao in this way."

Take the Jianzheng for example. If the Great Feng didn't perish, then he wouldn't perish either. Even the ancient god-demon Desolation couldn't do anything to him.

Then, if one refined all of Jiuzhou, in a certain sense, wouldn't that become the Heavenly Dao?

It really was a method worth trying.

the Jianzheng said:

"He used the Earth Sect aspect as material and forged the Earth Book as a magical artefact. His primordial spirit became the artefact spirit. To ensure that the Earth Book could withstand the heavenly tribulation at the moment of its birth, he laid the groundwork in advance and created the Earth Sect's mental cultivation method, cultivating merit and increasing fortune, using that to offset the backlash of the Heavenly Dao. But unfortunately, although the Earth Book was successfully forged, his intelligence was erased and he became a pure artefact spirit."

"Why was this path wrong?" the Nine-tailed Fox asked.

the Jianzheng smiled and said:

"Back then, the Daoist Venerable was probably just like you, unable to understand why he had failed. In fact, it's not hard to grasp. In the Way of Fire and Incense, what are the two core words?"

Xu Qi’an said slowly:

"Fire and Incense."

the Jianzheng said in a deep voice:

"Fire and Incense are fortune!

"The true core is fortune. He seized the Mountain and River Seals and forged the Earth Book, trying to use the Earth Book to control Jiuzhou, but he forgot how the Mountain and River Seals had come into being.

"Later, the Daoist Venerable gradually understood. If one wished to replace the Heavenly Dao and become the Heavenly Dao, one had to contain fortune.

"You should know what happened next."

Xu Qi’an gave an "Mm".

"He usurped the throne and ascended as emperor, gathering fortune upon himself. In the end, he hadn't expected that one blessed with fortune couldn't attain immortality."

At the same time, Xu Qi’an couldn't help sighing inwardly. No wonder the Heaven, Earth, and Human Sects had such huge problems. Those three paths had been wrong from the very beginning, they were the Daoist Venerable's three failed attempts.

the Jianzheng nodded.

"Fortunately, this time, the path was finally the right one. There was some deviation, but not enough to doom everything beyond redemption. That Human Sect aspect pulled back from the brink in time, cast off the old body, and escaped the law that one blessed with fortune couldn't attain immortality."

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox glanced at Xu Qi’an.

"If one blessed with fortune can't attain immortality, then why do the Warlock God, the Buddha, and Desolation still want to seize fortune?"

Xu Qi’an shook his head.

"They're only seizing fortune, they do not have fortune upon them. The path of containing fortune had already been attempted by the Human Sect aspect of the Heavenly Venerable, and it doesn't work. But there's one thing I still don't understand.

"Only Arcanists can manipulate fortune. Even a Beyond-rank shouldn't be able to control an ability that doesn't belong to their own system."

Xu Qi’an first frowned, then his eyes lit up.

"I understand now. By founding a sect and gathering Fire and Incense, then merging oneself with the religion, one can control fortune without having fortune added to oneself. In essence, it's still the path of the Way of Fire and Incense."

This was entirely different from the Confucian system.

The Confucian system cultivated by merging with fortune.

That was why the Confucian Sage hadn't escaped the law of heaven and earth, one blessed with fortune couldn't attain immortality.

Of course, given the temperament of the Confucian Sage, he might not have wanted immortality anyway.

For in Confucian ideals, the three forms of immortality were virtue, merit, and teachings.

Not lifespan.

the Jianzheng said, "With the evolution of heaven and earth to this day, fortune is of the utmost importance. Fortune belongs to the human race, and so the human race is the master of the heaven and earth of Jiuzhou. If a Beyond-rank wants to become the Heavenly Dao, it must seize fortune. You may treat fortune as a certificate, the certificate for becoming the Heavenly Dao.

"This too is a law of heaven and earth."

Xu Qi’an's expression instantly became complicated.

"So, the reason you trained me to be the gatekeeper was because I'd obtained fortune and couldn't attain immortality, so I've lost the chance to contend for the Heavenly Dao?"

The Nine-tailed Fox felt that this was the truth.

Unexpectedly, the Jianzheng shook his head.

"I chose you as the gatekeeper because you walk the martial artist system."

At this point, the Jianzheng revealed a deeply meaningful yet incomparably mocking expression.

"You should've read the notes I left in the Sitianjian."

Xu Qi’an had a flash of inspiration.

"That book on how to advance to half-step Martial God?"

the Jianzheng gazed at him and smiled.

"Do you remember the very first sentence?"

*Of all the systems under heaven, all transcend the Three Realms while remaining within the Five Elements. Only martial artists remain within the Three Realms but outside the Five Elements...* Xu Qi’an repeated the sentence several times in his heart. His body suddenly trembled, and like one talking in his sleep, he said:

"So that's how it is. So that's how it is."

He recalled Song Qing's explanation of those words.

The Nine-tailed Fox looked at the Jianzheng, then at Xu Qi’an, and chose to ask the latter:

"What do you mean?"

Xu Qi’an ignored her, and the Jianzheng smiled.

"All the major systems can borrow the power of heaven and earth and wield the Five Elements. Only martial artists do not interact with the outside world, but instead become a world unto themselves. The martial artist system is the only system that won't replace the Heavenly Dao."

The silver-haired demoness couldn't help recalling the words the Jianzheng had said to Desolation on the island of gods and demons:

The gatekeeper could only come from the martial artist system.

*So that was it...* she said in sudden realisation.

At that moment, Xu Qi’an pinched the bridge of his nose and let out a dispirited sigh.

*I thought a Martial God could kill Beyond-ranks, and that the martial artist system was the strongest among all the systems. Who'd have thought the Martial God was just a gatekeeper.*

*The whole world is insulting martial artists, even the laws of heaven and earth are insulting martial artists!*

After lampooning inwardly, he suddenly realised he had been overlooking a crucial question all along:

"Since the existence of martial artists is to guard the gate, then there must be a reason why Beyond-ranks can't become the Heavenly Dao, right?"

Ananda.

Countless immeasurable believers sat cross-legged upon the plain, palms pressed together, chanting scriptures and praying with utmost devotion. Buddhist chants reverberated between heaven and earth.

Beneath the nine heavens, a sacred and majestic Buddhist light descended, shining upon the mountain peak of Ananda.

A golden sun slowly emerged atop the summit.

That blazing sun radiated grand and resplendent light, illuminating every corner of this world.

Immediately after, a seated figure with lowered brows appeared, and behind its head was a magnificent halo symbolising wisdom, the Great Wisdom Dharma Aspect.

After those two Dharma Aspects formed, there was no further movement.

But as the Buddhist chants echoed and the prayers continued, believers began to emit specks of light like shattered gold. They rose in countless points and gathered towards the peak of Ananda.

Those fragmented Buddhist lights condensed to the right side of the Great Sun Tagathata Dharma Aspect, forming a Dharma Aspect with a compassionate countenance, one of utmost benevolence and boundless love.

Then more Dharma Aspects condensed into being.

The Great Samsara Dharma Aspect, the Immovable Lord Dharma Aspect, the Vajra Dharma Aspect, the Colourless Vaidurya Dharma Aspect, the Walker Dharma Aspect, the Medicine Buddha Dharma Aspect.

No one noticed that on the reverse side of Ananda, a pair of enormous eyes without eyelashes slowly opened.
