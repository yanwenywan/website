# 872. Pull the Rug Out (I)

"This was why the Confucian Sage wanted to seal the Beyond-ranks, and it was also the greatest source of conflict between us and the Beyond-rank."

The Jianzheng showed a rare serious expression, a very, very serious expression. Xu Qi’an had never seen the Jianzheng this solemn before.

Just as he had never seen Yang Qianhuan not posturing.

"Tell me, do you think it's better for the Heavenly Dao to be emotionless, or sentient?"

Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox exchanged a glance and each fell silent for a few seconds. Then the former said:

"Heaven is supremely selfish in itself, yet supremely impartial in its use.[^1]

"If the Heavenly Dao is without emotion, then for all living beings, that is the greatest fairness."

The silver-haired Yao enchantress nodded.

"I think so too."

The Jianzheng said:

"But whether they are gods ord demons, or Beyond-rank beings, they are still living creatures with thoughts."

He did not continue, but Xu Qi’an and the Nine-tailed Fox understood.

If a Beyond-rank replaced the Heavenly Dao, then the Heavenly Dao would gain consciousness, as well as selfish desires.

That was easy enough to understand. If replacing the Heavenly Dao meant becoming one with the laws of nature, like the Heaven Sect incarnation of the Daoist Venerable, and losing one's consciousness, then Beyond-ranks would not have gone to such painstaking lengths, exhausting themselves to become the Heavenly Dao.

"Then all living things in this world, for generation after generation, would be enslaved by Beyond-ranks, no, by the Heavenly Dao!"

The Nine-tailed Heavenly Fox wore a nasty expression.

Only emotionless laws made good laws. If the Heavenly Dao gained self-awareness, thoughts, and selfish desires, that would be something truly terrifying.

*Although there had never been absolute freedom in this world, at least for now one could still cry out, "Are kings, nobles, generals and ministers born that way?" But if the Heavenly Dao becomes conscious, then all living beings would become puppets and playthings...* Xu Qi’an felt heavy-hearted and said:

"It wouldn't be as simple as enslavement.

"The progress of every race's civilisation would also come to an end. The evolution of heaven and earth would stop as well, or be driven into some extreme direction."

From a broader perspective, human civilisation would no longer be able to grow wild and free. Future generations would live forever under the shadow of the Heavenly Dao.

The Jianzheng smiled.

"The Confucian Sage also thought that was no good. What the future should become ought to be decided by future generations. The Heavenly Dao being without emotion is the greatest compassion. It has no need for selfish desires or consciousness. So the Confucian Sage sealed all the Beyond-ranks and won more than twelve hundred years of breathing space for the living beings of Jiuzhou.

"He also bought time for the appearance of the gatekeeper."

After saying that, he looked at Xu Qi’an and said:

"You came out to sea this time to seek an opportunity to advance to half-step Martial God, didn't you?"

Xu Qi’an gave an "Mm".

He had failed to kill Agaruka Bodhisattva and plunder his divine essence, so his only way out was to head overseas in search of descendants of gods and demons.

He couldn't exactly devour Master Kou and al-Asura.

The Jianzheng nodded. "Actually, in my original calculations, the best outcome was to recover Shenshu's head and swallow Agaruka Bodhisattva along the way. Shame that when you were given the chance, you were useless."

*In his schemes, I should already have advanced to half-step Martial God by now? No, that doesn't make sense. If that had happened, I wouldn't have gone out to sea at all, and then today's interception wouldn't exist. Without that interception, I wouldn't have gained the qualification to become the gatekeeper...*

*Was this old bastard fooling me, or had he originally had another plan? Was it my appearance that changed his earlier arrangements...*

For a moment, Xu Qi’an could not tell whether the Jianzheng was mocking him maliciously, or whether he truly had some other design in mind.

*I hate LYBs...* Putting on a thoroughly resigned expression, he said:

"So what do I do now?"

The best way to deal with a LYB was to freeload off him.

The Jianzheng turned his gaze to the north-east and said:

"Sail in that direction for three days, and you'll reach an ancient battlefield. There you'll find what you want. Hmm, that useless disciple Song Qing of mine did tinker up a formation disc for collecting essence blood, didn't he?"

Though he asked it as a question, both his tone and expression were full of certainty, as if he had great confidence in his useless disciple.

Xu Qi’an followed his gaze, and his heart stirred as he recalled the information given by Zhenzhu, the mermaid queen.

"The queen of Mermaid Island told me there was an ancient battlefield to the north-east, and that a terrifying monster lived there."

Back then, the mermaid queen had pointed south-east. Judging by their current location, that ancient battlefield was indeed to the north-east, exactly the direction the Jianzheng had indicated.

It was not hard to infer that the two places were most likely one and the same.

The Jianzheng said:

"That monster is a Beyond-rank god-demon from the ancient age. After its death, the lingering spiritual essence and will fused together and became a soulless, spiritless monster. Compared with Agaruka Bodhisattva, it suits you better.

"Because that god-demon's spiritual essence symbolised 'strength'. In terms of sheer force, even ten Desolations together wouldn't be Its match."

Xu Qi’an's eyes lit up.

For martial artists, the temptation of strength was even greater than that of peerless beauty.

Put before a martial artist the chance to double his strength and a stunning beauty, and he would choose the former without the slightest hesitation.

At this moment, the Nine-tailed Fox, who had not spoken all this time, suddenly frowned and asked:

"How can a Beyond-rank rely on fortune to replace the Heavenly Dao?"

"If one gathers all the fortune of Jiuzhou into oneself, then He is Jiuzhou, and He is the Heavenly Dao." The Jianzheng's answer was brief and to the point.

The Nine-tailed Fox considered this and said:

"So whether it is the Warlock God or the Buddha, both want to spread their faith in the Central Plains and gather belief. If they can make all living beings in Jiuzhou believe in Buddhism or the Church of the Warlock God, then They can replace the Heavenly Dao?"

The Jianzheng sighed.

"In theory, yes. But in reality, it won't work. Reality is always crueller than you imagine.

"The Great Feng dominates the Central Plains and has gathered immense fortune, but that doesn't mean all the fortune of the humans of the Central Plains belongs to the Great Feng. From small Jianghu sects to massive powers like the Wulin Alliance, as long as they reach a certain scale, they all represent a portion of human fortune.

"Besides, among both Jianghu and the court, outstanding figures emerge in great numbers. They themselves symbolise a certain measure of fortune.

"The Great Feng has merely gathered the largest share of human fortune.

"By the same logic, even if all living beings in Jiuzhou worship the Buddha or the Warlock God, They still cannot monopolise all the fortune of Jiuzhou. So how could They replace the Heavenly Dao?"

The Nine-tailed Fox vaguely grasped something, yet did not quite dare to confirm it, so she probed:

"Then how would it be done?"

The Jianzheng looked at Xu Qi’an and said:

"In that great tomb, you once obtained an imperial seal. It contained stored fortune."

Xu Qi’an nodded.

The Jianzheng said:

"But that dynasty had long since vanished into history."

"What does that prove?" the Nine-tailed Fox did not understand.

Xu Qi’an considered his words and said:

"Fortune is gathered from the people, or rather, from living beings, but it does not disappear with the extinction of living beings. So long as it is preserved by special means, it can be treated as a kind of power..."

At this point, his face suddenly changed.

The Nine-tailed Fox's eyes widened.

...

The Western Regions.

Nine great Dharma Aspects gathered around the peak of Ananda, like deities descending from the nine heavens into the mortal world, receiving the worship of believers.

But on the shadowed side, hidden from all sight, a pair of enormous eyes opened, eyes without eyelashes, without emotion.

Within those huge sockets, the eyeballs rolled once, and both pupils slanted to the right at the same time, as if looking at the believers behind them.

At this moment, on the mountainside of Ananda, the outer layer of rock cracked apart, revealing dark red flesh and two rows of ghastly white teeth.

Each tooth was as large as a grown man.

The corners of the mouth slowly curled upwards, as if it were grinning.

Very soon, it closed again, covered over by the outer rock, and once more became part of the mountain.

The Buddhist chant continued, and no one noticed that the holy mountain had come alive.

On the plain at the foot of the mountain, a young girl in noble dress was "woken" by an urgent need to urinate and broke free from her state of devout prayer.

She felt as if she had just slept through everything, muddle-headed and unsure where she was.

"Mum, I want to..."

Her voice stopped abruptly. The parents, elder clansmen and servants who had been sitting cross-legged beside her had all vanished. Around her was nothing at all, an emptiness so silent it was terrifying.

The little girl stood up in panic, looked all around, and shouted the names of her parents and elder brother.

Her voice echoed through the dim world, but no one answered her. In the distance, those believers still sat cross-legged with palms together, immersed in their own worlds, smiles of bliss upon their faces.

For some reason, the hairs all over her body stood on end.

Terrified, she wept, hugged her knees, and crouched on the ground, staring at the earth beneath her feet, not daring to move.

She noticed two bulges pushing up under the thin layer of soil. She froze for a moment, then cautiously stretched out a hand, trying to brush away the dirt.

Before her fingers could touch those two bulges, the soil suddenly split apart. It was a pair of bloodshot eyes without eyelashes.

The eyes stared at her silently for a moment, then the eyeballs rolled once.

The little girl's face visibly twisted, the skin of her face twitching. She slowly opened her mouth wide, about to let out a shrill scream, but all at once, the ground beneath her feet split open and swallowed her whole.

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva opened his eyes and looked down from the sky. The believers on the plain were rapidly diminishing.

These believers in Buddhism, who represented the fortune of Buddhism, had finally become one with the Buddha they worshipped.

"Only by merging one's body with the Great Dao can one attain eternal life."

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's face was full of compassion as he pressed his palms together. "May all living beings attain the Phala, and may every corner of Jiuzhou become Buddha's country."

Beside him, Vaidurya Bodhisattva lowered her head and gazed at the ground below. Her gaze pierced through soil and stone, seeing into the mountain's interior, where she saw one believer after another sitting cross-legged. They were merging into the Buddha's body, as if ascending to bliss.

Vaidurya withdrew her gaze and looked towards the plain below. The Buddha's will was extending without cease. The plain was becoming Him, the rivers were becoming Him...

...

"Devour all the fortune of Jiuzhou. At that point, it won't matter even if all living beings in Jiuzhou are wiped out. Because fortune won't dissipate, just like the fortune stored in that imperial seal."

Xu Qi’an stared fixedly at the Jianzheng, trying to get a different answer from him, hoping he would overturn his guess.

To his disappointment, the Jianzheng slowly nodded.

"This is the great calamity!

"If all it meant was fighting over believers and preaching through Jiuzhou, what sort of great calamity would that be?

"Of course, the more believers there are, and the wider the territory occupied, the greater the boost to the Beyond-rank. At the beginning of the dynasty, if the Church of the Warlock God had succeeded, there would have been no place for the Buddha or the Gu God at all.

"And three hundred years ago, if the Confucians had not exterminated Buddhism, then by now the Buddha would probably already have transformed into heaven and earth and replaced the Heavenly Dao."

It was as if a shadow had fallen over Xu Qi’an's heart.

Replacing the Heavenly Dao was already intolerable, yet who could have thought the truth would be even crueller and worse than he had imagined.

The Nine-tailed Fox said in a low voice:

"At most by the end of the year, the Gu God and the Warlock God will break free of their seals..."

The sense of anxiety and crisis instantly exploded.

Xu Qi’an opened his mouth, just about to say something, when he suddenly saw the Jianzheng's figure slowly dissipating.

The Jianzheng looked at him and revealed a faint smile.

"Do you know why the Confucian Sage's carving knife drew close to you, why it chose you?"

He did not manage to finish. His figure vanished like an illusionary bubble and disappeared completely.

...

Thirty li outside Ananda, Pingkang City.

As a city-state near the holy mountain Ananda, this place had blossoming prosperity and a dense population.

The city's economy depended on wine houses and inns. For believers travelling to Ananda on pilgrimage, this was the inevitable stopping place.

The heavy flow of people also brought flourishing trade.

On the city wall, a soldier on watch gazed into the distance. The barren land stretched all the way to the edge of sight.

Suddenly, he saw the distant ground heave once like a wave, as if it had come alive.

He suspected there was something wrong with his eyes, rubbed them hard, and looked again. This time, there was no movement at all.

Then suddenly, the ground moved again. This time it was very close to the city wall, so he saw it with perfect clarity.

"Hey, hey, there's something underground."

Subconsciously gripping his spear, he turned and spoke to the comrade beside him.

Hearing this, his comrade looked into the distance for a while, saw nothing, and complained:

"Not even a ghost in sight. Stop making a fuss over nothing."

The soldier refused to believe it and fixed his eyes on the front, examining it with full concentration. After a long while, he gave up helplessly and turned to the comrade beside him.

"Strange, I clearly..."

His voice cut off sharply. The space beside him was empty, his comrade gone.

Not only that, all the soldiers on watch atop Pingkang City's walls had disappeared.

The whole world was deathly silent.

The soldier looked left and right in bewilderment, unable to understand what was happening before him. When he ran to the other side of the wall to inspect the situation within the city, he truly felt fear.

The entire city was empty.

At that moment, an eye split open in the crenellation a few feet in front of him, an eye without eyelashes, without any trace of emotion.

Then all the crenellations split open into eyes, row after row. Their eyeballs all turned at once and looked at the soldier.

...

The capital.

Huaiqing, dressed in smooth silk inner robes, jolted awake.

The gauzy silk clung tightly to her full and exquisitely curved body. Her clothes had already been soaked through with sweat.

She threw off the light quilt. The robe hanging on the screen flew up by itself and draped over her shoulders.

Huaiqing, barefoot, her snow-white feet stepping on the polished floor, strode quickly towards the outer chamber of the bedchamber and called:

"Attendants!"

The palace maid waiting outside came over in small quick steps with her head bowed, bent at the waist, and said:

"Your Majesty, this servant is here."

"Immediately send people to summon Scholar Zhao and Duke Wei. In a quarter of an hour, we want to see them." Huaiqing spoke with extreme speed, then glanced at herself and added, "Help us dress first."

...

With Ananda as the centre point, fleshly matter spread rapidly. The earth had come alive, the rivers had come alive, and the distant city-states had also come alive.

Vaidurya's exquisitely beautiful face was like a sculpture, lacking any emotional change. Her voice was gentle but flat.

"If we can summon all the believers of the Western Regions here, then within three days, the Buddha will be able to refine the Western Regions."

"If the Buddha is the Western Regions, what need is there to speak of refining it?"

Agaruka Bodhisattva's voice came from behind her.

The Buddha was assimilating with the Western Regions. Buddhism had operated in the Western Regions for several thousand years. Faith was everywhere, and fortune had long been fused into Buddhism.

Therefore, nothing hindered the Buddha's progress in becoming the Western Regions. It happened naturally.

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva smiled.

"Once the Buddha's great work is complete, He can go east and devour the fortune of the Central Plains. At that time, the Gu God and the Warlock God will still be sealed."

The two Bodhisattvas smiled faintly at those words.

Samantabhadra looked at Agaruka Bodhisattva and said:

"Xu Qi’an has gone far overseas, and it is fated to be in vain. The descendants of gods and demons above third rank were long since slaughtered clean by that ancient god-demon.

"He will most likely make a desperate gamble and set his sights on you. We can lay a trap and kill him."

Agaruka Bodhisattva's face was grave, though his tone remained calm.

"He may not dare come to the Western Regions."

As soon as he said that, he heard Vaidurya Bodhisattva beside him frown and say:

"Where is Du'e?"
