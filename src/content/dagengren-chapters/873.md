# 873. Pull the Rug Out (II)

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva and Agaruka Bodhisattva frowned at the same time. The former closed his eyes, then opened them again and said:

"He's not in Ananda."

Vaidurya Bodhisattva's beautiful eyes flickered. "Before the prayer, he was still there. Afterwards, I didn't sense him leaving either."

What method could let someone leave silently under the noses of three Bodhisattvas?

Agaruka said in a deep voice:

"The Worthy One Phala!"

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva and Vaidurya Bodhisattva narrowed their eyes at the same time, recalling some rather unpleasant memories. This move was a common trick of a certain Buddhist traitor.

If Du'e had used the power of the Worthy One Phala to create a false body, then controlled that false body to mingle among the Buddhist monks, he really could have slipped past them with ease. A false body created by the Worthy One Phala was almost indistinguishable from the real thing.

And none of the three of them would deliberately try to distinguish that tiny difference.

A bad premonition surged in Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's heart.

"What does Du'e want to do?"

...

The outskirts of the capital.

Du'e sat cross-legged on a nine-petalled lotus throne, riding golden light as he sped towards the capital.

As he neared the city, a middle-aged Confucian scholar shrouded in clear qi came forward to meet him. Under his feet was a ruler.

This scholar wore a purple robe. His features were refined, his brows thick and his eyes bright. Every glance he cast carried great authority, imposing even without anger.

"Arhat Du'e, I've been waiting for you."

The purple-robed scholar revealed a smile and bowed with cupped hands.

When he smiled, the authority vanished, replaced by a practised smoothness and worldly polish. His years in officialdom hadn't gone to waste.

Before Arhat Du'e could speak, he added:

"This official is Yang Gong, ordered by Her Majesty to wait here. Her Majesty has commanded that the moment you arrive, you are to enter the palace to see her at once.

"What delayed you, Arhat?"

Arhat Du'e had arrived a full half hour later than agreed.

Arhat Du'e's expression was grave. He pressed his palms together, but said nothing.

He seemed in no mood for conversation.

At that moment, the ruler beneath Yang Gong's feet suddenly slipped free of its master's control and flew up, aiming to strike Arhat Du'e on the knee.

Yang Gong quickly stopped it and apologised:

"This thing is my bonded magical artefact. After I entered the beyond-mortal realm, it gave birth to a trace of weak intelligence, and it likes hitting people..."

*It probably took you for my student. If a teacher asks and the student doesn't answer, then he should be struck...* Yang Gong added inwardly, but didn't say it aloud.

Any magical artefact that gave birth to weak intelligence had the potential to become a peerless divine weapon.

Arhat Du'e nodded, indicating he didn't mind the ruler's offence.

Yang Gong hesitated, then said:

"Not long ago, this official suddenly became uneasy in mind and spirit, as though the end of days were at hand. What exactly happened in the Western Regions?"

Or rather, what exactly was the Buddha's purpose in holding the Dharma Assembly?

The Confucian system was tied to fortune, and was extremely sharp in certain respects.

Arhat Du'e slowly said:

"I don't know."

Seeing this, Yang Gong didn't press further and said:

"I'll take the Arhat to see Her Majesty."

With that, he stirred noble spirit and used the art of laws follow commandments. In a chant-like tone, he said:

"Arhat Du'e and I are in the imperial study of the palace."

Clear light rose from beneath his feet and enveloped Arhat Du'e. The two vanished from the spot in an instant.

Du'e's vision blurred, and then he saw the imperial study, decorated with imposing luxury, paved with black floor tiles and red pillars. He saw the female Emperor of the Great Feng behind a large desk draped in yellow silk.

She wore a crimson inner robe embroidered with golden dragon patterns, with a wide black outer robe over it, also embroidered with golden dragon patterns. Her long azure-black hair was bound up with a golden crown. Coldly beautiful and noble, she combined feminine elegance with imperial attire to create a unique charm.

Standing to the left and right below her were Wei Yuan in a dark blue long robe embroidered with cloud patterns, and Grand Scholar Zhao Shou in crimson robes.

The ruler and her ministers all looked over at once.

Arhat Du'e pressed his palms together.

"Greetings, Your Majesty."

Huaiqing returned a cool smile, then her face turned stern as she asked:

"Did Arhat Du'e witness the Dharma Assembly?"

Arhat Du'e asked in return:

"Has Your Majesty noticed anything unusual?"

Huaiqing nodded slowly. "We were napping in our bedchamber just now, and suddenly dreamt that a giant Buddha came crashing down and pinned us beneath it."

She paused. Her cool face grew grave, and her tone sank with it.

"Eyes grew on the Buddha's body, and they stared at us coldly. A mouth grew on it too, and bite by bite, it devoured us completely."

Even recalling that nightmare now still made a chill run down her spine.

"We are already a beyond-mortal martial artist. We would never have such a dream for no reason. Xu Ningyan once said that those blessed with fortune receive a warning when the nation faces destruction. Thinking of the recent situation in the Western Regions, the cause is most likely there."

Zhao Shou agreed:

"It is indeed fortune giving warning. This servant too has been anxious all day, uneasy in mind and spirit."

Arhat Du'e showed a look of understanding, as though some guess in his heart had been confirmed. He pressed his palms together and said:

"Before the Dharma Assembly began, I had already left Ananda. I don't know the exact situation there either. It was just that, on my way to the Central Plains, I suddenly sensed that heaven and earth had changed..."

He chose his words for a moment, trying his best to explain:

"The spiritual energy of heaven and earth was rapidly dissipating, replaced by surging Buddhist power, as though the world had become the legendary Pure Land."

Although for someone like him who cultivated Buddhist Dharma, such a world was incomparably wonderful, practically a paradise.

Huaiqing, Wei Yuan, Zhao Shou, and Yang Gong exchanged silent looks. All of them were bewildered, yet grave.

"I then turned back to investigate. As I neared Ananda and passed a certain city, I discovered that the city was completely empty, with no trace of human habitation..." At this point, Arhat Du'e pressed his palms together. His expression was compassionate, and he repeatedly chanted, "Amitabha."

*No wonder he was late...* Yang Gong frowned and said:

"No trace of human habitation?"

Huaiqing and the other two also knitted their brows. Although they hadn't fully understood, they realised the situation was bad.

Arhat Du'e quickly steadied his emotions and continued:

"I was about to enter the city to investigate when I suddenly saw a pair of eyes split open in the city wall. Those eyes had no emotion at all, not even coldness, but after being looked at once by them, every hair on my body stood on end, and I was terrified beyond measure.

"Strangely, it didn't harm me. It completely ignored me.

"I didn't dare return to Ananda again, and came all the way to the capital."

Let alone Du'e, who had personally experienced it, the four people present only had to hear it to feel waves of chill rising in their hearts.

A city had become an empty shell, and its walls had come alive?

Then where had the people gone?

Huaiqing thought of what she had seen in her dream, and a thought faintly arose in her heart.

Zhao Shou said in a low voice:

"What is Arhat Du'e's view?"

Du'e pondered for a moment, then slowly said:

"On the day of the battle to seize back Shenshu's head, Ananda collapsed into ruins. Yet when the Dharma Assembly was held, Ananda rose from the ground overnight and was restored to its original state.

"I saw with my own eyes that the Buddha transformed into the sacred mountain."

Wei Yuan's pupils contracted slightly.

Huaiqing's breathing quickened for a moment, and she said:

"The pair of eyes you saw on the city wall, was that the Buddha?!"

She voiced her guess directly.

The members of the Heaven and Earth Society had discussed that battle through Earth Book fragments and had commented on the Buddha transforming into the sacred mountain. At the time, they hadn't understood the principle behind it, and had mostly marvelled at it.

If the Buddha could transform into a mountain, why couldn't It transform into a city?

No one refuted Huaiqing, because they were thinking the same thing.

"Duke Wei, Lord Zhao, what do you think?" Huaiqing looked at the two pillars of the court.

Though she was clever and resourceful, in such matters she was still a young woman of shallow experience.

What had happened in the Western Regions was both expected and unexpected.

Expected, because they knew something would happen. Unexpected, because the situation had gone beyond their understanding. Huaiqing hadn't even grasped the exact circumstances.

Wei Yuan shook his head.

"This matter sounds far too bizarre. This subject dares not make a conclusion."

Zhao Shou echoed Wei Yuan's words, then added:

"The situation is unclear for now. We need to send someone to investigate first. Arhat Du'e, you said just now that the Buddha... let us assume for the moment that it is the Buddha, did not harm you?"

Du'e nodded.

Zhao Shou said:

"That may be because you come from the Buddhist sect. Would you be willing to go to the Western Regions once more and investigate? Rest assured, we will provide you with enough magical artefacts, including pages inscribed with the art of laws follow commandments, teleportation jade talismans, and so on, to ensure your safety."

Du'e did not hesitate.

"Yes!"

Zhao Shou then looked towards the female Emperor and said:

"Your Majesty, summon the civil and military officials at once, appoint Du'e as National Teacher, and declare Mahayana Buddhism the state religion.

"Although we don't know what the Buddha wants to do, it is an indisputable fact that a Beyond-rank is fighting for fortune. Weakening the Buddha's fortune can only help."

Du'e said approvingly:

"Excellent!"

...

Overseas, two figures swept in from the horizon, bringing deafening sonic booms.

The figures descended from the sky. Just as they were about to plunge into the sea, they abruptly halted, shedding all inertia.

But the fierce wind they carried pressed down on the sea surface, forming a ripple several metres across.

Xu Qi’an turned his head to look at the nine-tailed fox wearing Mu Nanzhi's dress, and said in a deep voice:

"It's decided then, you're up. Go on, Your Majesty!"

The silver-haired demoness shot him a sidelong glance and said resentfully:

"You won't marry me, yet you still order me about and send me into fire and water for you."

Her silver hair was like snow, her skin fairer still. With nothing but her colouring, this seductive and voluptuous beauty gave off a sense of purity and nobility.

As Xu Qi’an spread his divine sense to probe the situation below the sea, he said:

"Talk properly."

The nine-tailed fox gave him a "Heh heh".

Before long, Xu Qi’an withdrew his divine sense and said:

"I didn't detect anything unusual. Let's go down and take a look."

He was the first to leap into the sea.

With a splash, water flew up, and he vanished into the blue waves.

The nine-tailed fox gave a light leap and followed him into the vast ocean.

...

Inside Jinluan Hall.

Upon the imperial throne, Huaiqing sat upright in dragon robes, majestic and still. Her phoenix eyes and arched brows, her elegant nose and crimson lips, together with the dragon robe, turned her original cool bearing into one of imposing and icy splendour.

The assembled ministers stood in the hall holding ivory tablets.

After loudly crying, "Long live Your Majesty, long live, long long live!" Huaiqing glanced at the eunuch with the seal standing below to the left.

The eunuch from Dexin Courtyard held an imperial decree in both hands and stepped forward.

At this moment, most of the assembled lords still didn't know the reason for this sudden court session, nor its purpose.
