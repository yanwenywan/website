# 877. A Loose Tongue Causes Trouble

The Holy Son's face darkened. Using a finger in place of a pen, he wrote a message:

【SEVEN: What happened? Hadn't you already left the Western Regions?】

His strokes flew, as swift as wild cursive.

But no one replied for a long while. After the time it took to drink half a cup of tea, a response finally came, though it wasn't from Li Miaozhen, but Chu Yuanzhen:

【FOUR: Bud--Buddha has come...】

...

Li Lingsu felt a chill run through his whole body. It was as if an icy snake had crawled over his back, shot up along his spine to the crown of his head, and left his scalp instantly numb.

【SEVEN: What's going on?】

【SEVEN: Didn't you just say the Buddha had lost fortune and could no longer expand?】

【SEVEN: Say something...】

He sent three messages in a row, but they vanished like mud oxen into the sea, with no reply at all.

Li Lingsu sprang up from his chair with a swish, clenched the Earth Book fragment in his hand, turned to look at the four Ji, and said in a voice edged with sharpness:

"The Buddha has come."

The four Ji all looked over at once, their lovely faces changing colour slightly. Yeji pressed:

"What do you mean, the Buddha has come?"

The girl Lingji looked around in terror and silently edged closer to Youji.

Li Lingsu had wanted to show them the Earth Book, but then remembered the contents had already vanished, so he explained at great speed:

"My companions sent word asking me to quickly invite Master Shenshu to Leizhou to help."

Leizhou was the westernmost province of the Great Feng, right next to the Western Regions.

"Why Leizhou?"

Yeji asked as she rose and headed outside.

*How should I know...* Li Lingsu shook his head and, together with the other three Ji, followed Yeji out of the great hall. After a few bounds through the towers and pavilions of Nanhua Temple, they arrived outside the sealing tower.

The tower door was tightly shut, with a faint sliver of lamplight showing through the crack.

Graceful as a noble matron, Youji stepped forward with lotus-like steps. She raised a hand. Her smooth silk sleeve slipped down, revealing her fair wrist as she bent it and knocked softly on the tower door.

"Master Shenshu, have you finished your cultivation?"

When Yeji had arrived, Shenshu had happened to be in seclusion. And Li Lingsu had also learnt the result of the exploration of the Western Regions from the Earth Book chat group and knew that Li Miaozhen and the others were safe, so they had not disturbed the half-step Martial God again.

He had even been thinking of a romantic encounter with the fox beauties, each lovely in her own way.

Of course, once the Holy Son learnt these fox women were Xu Qi’an's concubines, he lost all interest in paying them any attention.

"What is it!"

Shenshu's deep, resonant voice came from within the tower.

Youji briefly and succinctly told him what had happened.

The tower door opened by itself. Candlelight poured out like flowing water, and Shenshu's tall figure slowly emerged.

His appearance had changed greatly. He now stood seven feet tall, no different from an ordinary grown man. His features were handsome, his complexion fair and clean. He was a very good-looking young monk.

This was what he had originally looked like. More precisely, this was Shenshu's original appearance, not that of the Asura King.

The physical traits of the Asura race were far too obvious. If Shenshu had possessed the Asura King's appearance, the Nine-tailed Fox could never have failed to know her own father was the Asura King.

Nor could Shenshu possibly have concealed himself from the monks while cultivating in Ananda?

"I understand."

Shenshu gave a slight nod, and in the next moment, his figure vanished before them.

...

Imperial Study.

Huaiqing gripped the Earth Book fragment and abruptly rose to her feet. Her clear eyes, like still pools, stared fixedly outside the hall.

Wei Yuan, Zhao Shou, and Wang Zhenwen, the three great figures holding real power, had not left and remained in the Imperial Study.

Seeing Huaiqing react so strongly, the three of them all turned their gaze to the peerless female Emperor.

"A message from the Earth Book. The Buddha has come."

Huaiqing drew a deep breath and said:

"They're currently in Leizhou..."

Wei Yuan, Zhao Shou, and Wang Zhenwen were so startled that they all stood up from their seats, some with changed expressions, others with solemn faces.

After a moment's thought, Zhao Shou said:

"The Buddha absorbed the entire Western Regions? Where did He get the fortune from?"

Wang Zhenwen gave a cold snort.

"Either He had some hidden trump card that helped Him complete the final step, or some old friends of ours delivered it to Him."

*The Church of the Warlock God...*

The four people present were all supremely intelligent. A question like this couldn't stump them.

"What a dangerous move," Wei Yuan sighed.

He comforted them, saying:

"With Shenshu there, he should be able to hold the Buddha off."

Zhao Shou glanced at him, his face full of disagreement, but he did not refute him.

Huaiqing sat back down in the large red sandalwood chair and quickly wrote into the Earth Book fragment with her slender jade fingers:

【ONE: Xu Ningyan, Xu Ningyan...】

...

Irbu rode a streak of black light across the high skies, the vast earth flashing by beneath him.

He had just reached the borders of Yuuzhou when he suddenly saw two streaks of black light approaching from ahead. The two sides came face to face.

"Great Warlock?"

Irbu was startled, delighted, and baffled all at once.

"Why have you come to the Central Plains?"

At the front was none other than Sarun Agu, dressed in a warlock's robe, his white hair braided into many little plaits, half his face covered by a white beard.

Accompanying him was Uda Batu.

Sarun Agu did not answer, but asked:

"Did you give the item to the Buddha?"

Irbu nodded, then said with a sigh:

"I'm afraid Yan Kingdom is about to suffer endless natural disasters."

Just like the Great Feng back then, after losing half its national fortune.

Uda Batu said flatly:

"It doesn't matter any more."

*True enough. Once the Warlock God broke free of the seal, He would assimilate the northeast just like the Buddha, plunder the fortune of Jiuzhou, and Yan Kingdom would soon cease to exist...* Irbu said coolly:

"I know. I don't need you to tell me."

Fortunately, although the territories of the three kingdoms would be assimilated by the Warlock God, cultivators of the warlock system would all survive. And in future, they would receive the blessing of the Warlock God, no, of the Heavenly Dao, and live forever.

As the Great Warlock had said, the Warlock God could alter the laws of Heaven and Earth and bestow divine abilities upon the warlock system.

"Where's Rain Master Nalan?"

Irbu asked.

Sarun Agu smiled.

"Such a vast territory in the northern frontier, wouldn't giving it to Zhujiu be a terrible waste?"

Irbu's eyes widened and he instantly understood where Nalan Tianlu had gone.

The Great Warlock intended to seize the moment while the Great Feng and the Buddhist sect were clashing, strike swiftly, and take the lands of the northern yao and barbarians.

That way, the fortune Yan Kingdom had lost could be replenished.

Killing three birds with one stone. Brilliant!

Just then, Irbu saw a group of beyond-mortal experts riding the wind from the south.

Looking carefully, he realised they were the seven leaders of the shaman clans.

The other side had clearly noticed the beyond-mortal experts of the Church of the Warlock God as well. One of them, a person draped in a black cloak, abruptly spread out a domain, covering the six others in a layer of shadow.

Ready at any moment for shadow-jumping.

Heaven Gu Grandma, leaning on her walking stick, her head full of silver hair, gave Sarun Agu a slight nod.

The Great Warlock returned the gesture with a smile.

"What are people from your Church of the Warlock God doing here?"

A woman in a gauzy robe, with a graceful figure, seductive curves, and a delicate, coquettish face, frowned as she demanded an answer.

Heaven Gu Grandma had brought them rushing to Leizhou, saying only that the great calamity had arrived and they were going to see what was happening.

Irbu's first impression of her was--slut!

Every gesture and movement of hers exuded a bewitching allure, like a natural-born temptress made to seduce men.

Irbu didn't know who she was, but he recognised that sort of method. She must be the current leader of the Desire Gu tribe.

"Naturally we've come to see what hidden trump cards the Great Feng still has."

Irbu chuckled.

"Perhaps to watch how the Buddha slaughters the Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts."

Luan Yu's charming face instantly darkened.

...

The Leizhou border.

In a small town, dark red flesh-like matter swept through the streets and houses like a rising tide. Veins were clearly visible in that flesh, clinging to the houses and covering the ground.

It was already night. Most of the residents had gone to sleep. Like the houses, they were silent and motionless, making not the slightest sound.

But the town's only goulan was still brightly lit, and the wealthier men of the town were still enjoying themselves inside.

Some held women in their arms while drinking, some listened to opera, and some were already naked in bed, performing forbidden arts of the alchemical domain.

But when the tide of dark red flesh came surging in, the clients and courtesans in the goulan screamed and ran out of the rather imposing little building.

Then, as if ants caught in thick syrup, they were trapped in the dark red flesh, struggling bitterly one by one. Then their struggling gradually weakened, and the fear on their faces slowly faded.

They sat down cross-legged where they were, palms pressed together, like the most devout of believers.

Little by little, they were assimilated by the flesh-like matter...

Whoosh!

A shrill tearing sound broke through the air as section after section of withered branches descended from the sky, harvesting the heads of those residents who had become devout believers.

The flesh-like matter surged up like a fountain, blocking the sword array formed by the withered branches and then swallowing it whole.

In the far distant sky, Chu Yuanzhen's robes were tattered. He held a broken sword in his hand, blood seeping from the corner of his mouth.

Al'Asura, Daoist Jinlian, Arhat Du'e, Li Miaozhen, as well as Sun Xuanji and Master Hengyuan, all bore wounds, traces left behind by battle.

Calling it a battle gave them too much credit. More accurately, they had just managed to snatch back their lives from the Buddha's hands, and that was only because Sun Xuanji had reacted in time and al'Asura had withstood the pressure.

Earlier, they had gathered on a mountain peak at the border of Leizhou to discuss the situation in the Western Regions, using the Earth Book to describe and analyse the Buddha's condition to the female Emperor.

They had planned to remain in Leizhou and observe for a few days, but in the night they saw the dark red tide surging towards Leizhou beneath the moonlight, and wherever it passed, all things were covered by the flowing flesh-like matter.

"How could He have crossed the Western Regions and invaded the territory of the Central Plains?"

Al'Asura, his chest heaving violently, could not understand what he was seeing.

According to their previous deductions, the Buddha's expansion required fortune. But not only had He swallowed up all the lands of the Western Regions, He was now expanding disorderly towards the Central Plains as well?

If that was the case, why had He not killed them earlier while still in the Western Regions?

"It's different!"

Chu Yuanzhen's powers of observation were keen. He said in a low voice:

"What He's revealing now should be His 'true body'. But in the Western Regions, He was wrapped in sand and soil, as if mountains and rivers had come alive. Earlier, when chasing Arhat Du'e, after crossing the boundary, He also revealed this kind of form.

"In other words, Leizhou right now is to Him what those parts of the Western Regions were before, the parts He couldn't assimilate due to insufficient fortune."

"So why is it like this?" Li Miaozhen didn't want to hear the reasoning, only the answer.

Daoist Jinlian stayed on guard and explained:

"Have you noticed? Although He has 'occupied' Leizhou, He hasn't assimilated it. That shows two things. First, without enough fortune, He can't assimilate the lands of the Central Plains the way He assimilated the Western Regions.

"Second, He's turning people into believers, then swallowing the believers.

"What does that mean?"

A thought flashed through everyone's minds. Seize territory, gather fortune!

Just like Xu Pingfeng last year.

Arhat Du'e pressed his palms together.

"This is the so-called great calamity. On the day Beyond-ranks revive, They will surely devour the Central Plains."

Compared with large-scale war, this kind of devouring was a thousand times, ten thousand times more terrifying.

When two armies fought, there was still victory and defeat, still room to manoeuvre.

But who could stop the Buddha's expansion?

Even beyond-mortal experts like them could only make a few harmless attempts at obstruction from very far away. They simply did not dare draw near.

"Though He sweeps forward like fire, irresistible, He seems to have restrictions. His movements are impeded."

Sun Xuanji scribbled wild cursive across paper and showed it to the others.

What he meant was that, though the Buddha was powerful and unmatched, in transforming into a flood that devoured all things, His massive body had in turn become a burden, and He could no longer move about like an individual, going wherever He wished.

This seemed to be the corresponding price.

Suddenly, the dark red matter rose high into the air and formed into a vague human shape, faintly that of a bald monk. Its facial features were blurred, and its body was only roughly sketched into human form.

But it had a pair of real eyes, utterly emotionless.

It silently watched the Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts.

*Senior Brother Sun, you're not really Zhong Li in disguise, are you? You'd better stop offering opinions...* Li Miaozhen's heart skipped a beat. Looking at the others' expressions, she knew everyone was thinking the same thing.
