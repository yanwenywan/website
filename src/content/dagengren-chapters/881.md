# 881. Entering the Stage

On Mermaid Island, Zhenzhu, who had just ridden the waters back to her people's settlement, suddenly felt an inexplicable lurch in her heart.

She spun round sharply and saw the sea surface surging with layer upon layer of waves. They crashed again and again against the reefs, spraying white foam and letting out thunderous rumbles.

The entire stretch of sea was in turmoil, roaring.

In the far distance, the sky churned with ink-dark clouds, and now and then lightning flashed within them.

Such a scene was not unusual at sea. Zhenzhu had seen storms fiercer than this, and the merfolk had even lived through tsunamis that swallowed half the island.

But unlike an ordinary natural disaster, Zhenzhu could clearly sense a kind of fear, a fear that made one want only to crawl and kneel.

It was a fear branded into the deepest part of every living being's genes.

On the beach, the merfolk who had come to welcome their queen's return were all lying prostrate on the ground, faces buried in the sand, trembling.

The Arsu Archipelago.

Nulang, the dragon-man island lord, stood on the top floor of the main hall, gazing towards the north-east.

Behind him were the servants and subordinates in the hall, all showing obvious fear. This place was extremely far from the ancient battlefield, so the effects they suffered were far less severe than those on Mermaid Island.

Although the descendants of gods and demons on the island could feel fear in their bones, it was not enough to frighten them into grovelling on the ground.

"This aura is in the north-east. The lord of the Wanyao Kingdom and that supreme human expert both headed north-east..."

A thought flashed through the dragon-man island lord's mind:

"Has he stepped into the ranks of the strongest gods and demons?"

At this thought, while considering how he ought to build a good relationship with the Mermaid Queen in future, he also resented himself for not putting down his pride in time and currying favour with that human expert.

...

Heading south from Leizhou, crossing the Southern Marches, with the scenery below flashing past in a blur, Li Miaozhen stood on her flying sword, frantically urging on her true essence while probing her spirit into the Earth Book, trying to "private chat" Xu Qi’an.

In the chaotic space, the light clusters symbolising the nine Earth Book fragments were scattered in the four directions. The cluster representing Number Three was dim.

That meant he was out of contact.

*Bastard, you've drifted so far overseas you've gone out of contact. If I find out you're living it up on Mermaid Island in some romantic escapade...* Li Miaozhen remembered how, when Xu Qi’an had sent messages before, he had boasted about meeting merfolk overseas, every one of them as beautiful as flowers and gentle and lovely, especially how marvellous the Mermaid Queen was, and so on.

She only needed to get within a certain range of Xu Qi’an for the Earth Books to establish a connection.

But she had to get the direction right, or she would be travelling south to go north and stray farther and farther off course.

After a while, the boundless ocean finally appeared at the edge of her vision.

She immediately sped off towards the south-east. A quarter of an hour later, the divine sense she had deposited in her Earth Book fragment sensed that Number Three's Earth Book fragment had finally lit up.

It flashed on and off, extremely unstable.

Li Miaozhen's spirits rose.

...

Xu Qi’an delightedly sensed that after essence, qi, and spirit had merged into one again, the fishing net formed by his primordial spirit had completely fused into the essence of his flesh and blood, becoming tougher and more unshakable than before.

The greatest change was that every part of his body could now possess thoughts of its own.

He no longer needed his brain alone to think. His hands and feet could do it too, or even the head below.

Every part of his body possessed a portion of his primordial spirit. Just as with Shenshu back then, even if he were cut apart, the soul would go with the severed piece.

A first rank martial artist had the same trait, except that the primordial spirit within severed limbs lacked independent thought.

Besides that, the improvement in his stamina and qi mechanism could only be described as terrifying. As he was now, he could punch his former self so hard he'd blast the shit out of him, and that former self had been a first rank martial artist.

Apart from the comprehensive surge in all attributes, the thing Xu Qi’an cared about most in the half-step Martial God realm was the change on the microscopic level, the cells that made up his body had mutated.

Xu Qi’an focused inward and discovered that his cells now contained twisted tadpole-like markings.

They existed within the cell nuclei, as if they were something inherent in the genes themselves.

Each and every cell had one twisted tadpole-like marking. They looked similar, yet each was different.

If they were put together, they resembled... a formation?

Xu Qi’an immersed himself in the terrifyingly vast number of markings, trying to decipher them, but in the end he gained only one conclusion:

The undying property!

These formation markings possessed an undying property.

Unlike beyond-mortal martial artists, whose immortality stemmed from immense and vigorous life force, allowing them easily to regenerate flesh and blood.

The undying property of a half-step Martial God was that he was difficult to destroy.

There was a foundational difference between the two.

Apart from all that, what delighted Xu Qi’an most was that he had gained control over part of the spiritual essence of the ancient gods and demons. Once activated, it would grant an enormous boost in power.

In a contest of pure physical strength, even Shenshu was probably no match for him now.

*And with the amplification of the blood sacrifice art of the Strength Gu on top of that, my strength has already reached the ceiling of this world...*

Ending his inward inspection, he opened his eyes and saw the nine-tailed fox hiding cautiously in the distance, watching him.

Her large round eyes carried a trace of timidness and a trace of excitement.

She had witnessed the birth of a half-step Martial God.

There was no need to doubt it. That terrifying power just now was already no weaker than Shenshu.

"Whew..."

When Xu Qi’an reined in his aura, the nine-tailed fox let out a breath of relief, wagged her fluffy snow-white tail, and flew over.

"Compared with Shenshu, the spiritual essence on you gives me an even greater sense of oppression."

The nine-tailed fox forcibly tried to save face and find a reason for how she had been trembling just now.

"Descendants of gods and demons are more sensitive to, and more fearful of, powerful gods and demons. Mm, how does it feel?"

"No problem beating up your old man."

Xu Qi’an laughed.

The nine-tailed fox narrowed her eyes and egged him on:

"Go on then, go on!"

Xu Qi’an briefly told her about his condition, placing particular emphasis on the "markings" imprinted in his genes, and said:

"What do you think?"

The nine-tailed fox looked him over. Surprise flickered in her long seductive eyes.

"Have you turned into a god-demon?"

What she meant was that this sort of situation only appeared in gods and demons and their descendants.

*That's more or less what I thought too. Am I gradually becoming god-and-demon-ified? Bloody hell, then can I still have children or not...* At that thought, Xu Qi’an felt a bit panicked inside.

Day after day of frolicking among flowers and jade, yet not a single belly had shown any reaction.

Fuxiang was also not pulling her weight.

Lin'an had been married for two months and there was still no sign of anything.

*Surely the problem isn't with me... Mm, gods and demons can reproduce too. If all else fails, I'll find a descendant of gods and demons and give it a try...*

With an appraising gaze, he looked the nine-tailed fox up and down.

"What are you looking at?"

The fox spirit frowned.

That stinking man's gaze made her rather uncomfortable.

*One pregnancy, nine babies, and the father is a half-step Martial God...* A book title surfaced uncontrollably in Xu Qi’an's mind. He pondered and said:

"If this is spiritual essence, then what it symbolises is 'indestructibility'. Is this unique to half-step Martial Gods, or do Beyond-rank beings of other systems also undergo this kind of change?"

The nine-tailed fox shook her head.

"No.

"Beyond-rank beings are different from gods and demons, so They cannot directly replace the Heavenly Dao the way gods and demons can. They need to seize fortune and gain recognition.

"The Daoist Venerable only managed to find this path after dying three times.

"Besides, the Jianzheng said before that martial artists can never replace the Heavenly Dao, so turning into a god-demon doesn't make sense.

"Your situation isn't becoming a god-demon. I think it may be the ultimate secret of the martial artist system, something related to the Martial God."

Her analysis was reasoned and well supported, and more or less the same as Xu Qi’an's own thoughts.

He was just about to speak when he suddenly felt as though someone had given his head a heavy knock.

Eh, has a member of the Heaven and Earth Society come out to sea too? Xu Qi’an frowned slightly, his Adam's apple bobbing as he spat out the Earth Book fragment.

【TWO: Xu Ningyan Xu Ningyan Xu Ningyan...】

Li Miaozhen's voice frantically rang in his mind.

【THREE: Miaozhen, why have you come out to sea?】

At his current distance, he was extremely far from the continent of Jiuzhou, already beyond the signal range of the Earth Book fragment.

On receiving Xu Qi’an's reply, Li Miaozhen finally relaxed. In a tone that said, "You deadbeat, what took you so long, where the hell have you been?", she sent:

【TWO: Where the hell did you run off to? Have you been fooling around with some enchantress overseas until you've forgotten your own name? Get your arse over here right now, I'll split you in two with one sword.】

【THREE: If you've got something to say, say it.】

Silver Gong Xu wasn't a man who feared women.

【TWO: The Buddha has attacked Jiuzhou.】

That single sentence made Xu Qi’an's pupils contract slightly.

Li Miaozhen swiftly told him everything that had happened, then said:

【TWO: You can't imagine how strange the Buddha is. He has not only merged into the mountains, rivers, and cities, He even intends to swallow Leizhou. If I hadn't seen it with my own eyes, I wouldn't have believed it either.】

After chattering it all out, Li Miaozhen asked with a faint trace of hope:

【TWO: Have you advanced to half-step Martial God?】

【THREE: Mm!】

【TWO: It's fine, becoming a half-step Martial God isn't something that happens in a day. There's still plenty of time in future. Come back first, the Great Feng needs a first rank martial artist...】

Her voice grew softer and softer until she trailed off altogether.

After quite a while:

【Y--you actually did it?!】

【THREE: I'll explain later. I'm coming back at once.】

Xu Qi’an first took out a set of robes from the Earth Book fragment and changed into them. Then he pressed a hand to the nine-tailed fox's shoulder, causing the "eyeball" on his left wrist to light up, and directly used spatial transference to leave.

...

Between sea and sky, Li Miaozhen stood on her flying sword, her slender jade-like hand holding the Earth Book fragment as her heart slowly settled back into place.

All her anxiety, fear, and every sort of negative emotion vanished in an instant.

He had really succeeded. He had become one of the very few half-step Martial Gods since ancient times.

He had stepped into the ranks of the strongest beneath Beyond-rank.

He had become a terrifying figure like Shenshu.

Once the negative emotions disappeared, what rose in Li Miaozhen's heart instead was a wistful, sighing sense of how fleeting the years were.

Three years since I first met you, yet it feels like three thousand springs and autumns.

"You really did it."

She muttered, then gave a spirited wave with the hand holding the Earth Book fragment.

*Mm, Xu Ningyan can't be too far away. If he's returning to Jiuzhou, he'll definitely pass the Southern Marches first. I'll wait here...*

Li Miaozhen put away the Earth Book fragment and sat cross-legged on the spine of the sword.

...

Leizhou.

Al-Asura was bent slightly at the waist, hearing his own heavy breathing. His right arm hung limply, and half the left side of his face was damaged.

His pitch-black body was battered all over.

With him at the front, behind him stood Daoist Jinlian and Arhat Du'e, then Yang Gong and Sun Xuanji.

Chu Yuanzhen and Hengyuan had temporarily withdrawn from the battle due to severe injuries.

The one fighting this group of beyond-mortal experts was Agaruka Bodhisattva, Buddhism's strongest combatant.

As for Luo Yuheng, she alone was holding back two Buddhist Bodhisattvas.

This was not to say that her cultivation had already surpassed two experts of the same level, but rather that the Earthly Immortal's immunity to all magic happened to counter the two Bodhisattvas, who specialised in crowd control.

In particular, Samantabhadra Bodhisattva's "Great Reincarnation Dharma Aspect" and "Great Compassion and Mercy Dharma Aspect" were almost useless here.

Crack, crack, crack...

The sound of space collapsing rang out endlessly. The colourless glazed barrier surged and spread like a tide time and again, but the moment it touched Luo Yuheng's protective golden light, it collapsed inch by inch like a shattered mirror.

Throughout the process, Vaidurya Bodhisattva's figure kept flashing in and out of sight as she circled around Luo Yuheng.

Her objective was very clear. She intended to kill this female Daoist Leader of the Human Sect, former National Teacher of the Great Feng, by physical means.

In close combat, Vaidurya Bodhisattva was far from a match for the Human Sect Daoist Leader, who was renowned for killing power. But among all techniques under heaven, only speed was unbreakable. If she seized the opportunity, then at the same realm, it would not be difficult to cut down the physical body of an Earthly Immortal.

Not every system was like the martial artist path.

Though Luo Yuheng was not as fast as Vaidurya, who possessed the Walker Dharma Aspect, the "earth aspect" combined with the "wind aspect" among her four dharma bodies allowed her to deal with the situation with ease.

She could even spare attention to wield flying swords against Samantabhadra Bodhisattva and Agaruka.

The former sat cross-legged on the spot, using Zen arts to withstand the flying swords, while the latter merely found the Earthly Immortal irritating.

"After the struggle between Heaven and man, Luo Yuheng's cultivation rose greatly. Give her a few more years and she will step into the middle stage of first rank. The younger generation is truly formidable."

Sarun Agu spoke with feeling.

Irbu, meanwhile, was racking his brains for a way to deal with the Daoist Earth's Immortals. The trait of immunity to all magic was simply too hard to counter.

Most methods of the Warlock system were restrained by it.

But in the very next moment, he stopped thinking about it, because it was not a matter he needed to concern himself with.

The gap between third rank and first rank was like a chasm.

An Earthly Immortal could crush a third rank Master of Wisdom with one finger.

"She alone is holding back two Bodhisattvas. Truly terrifying," Uda Batu commented.

Over on the side of the Gu clan chiefs, their taut emotions eased somewhat.

Their position was exceedingly awkward.

If the Great Feng suffered a complete collapse, they would be forced to enter the battle, and in a fight of this level, a third rank could truly die at any moment.

This relatively balanced situation before them was exactly what they wanted to see.

Sarun Agu tightened his grip slightly on the shepherd's whip in his hand and said:

"Whether Samantabhadra or Vaidurya, neither is fighting with full strength. Certainly, their Dharma Aspects are countered by the Earthly Immortal, but their power is far from limited to this."

Irbu was stunned for a moment, then said:

"Great Warlock, you mean..."

Sarun Agu smiled.

"They are waiting for an opportunity."

The moment his words fell, that golden sun finally exploded.

Buddhist light several times more dazzling than before burst out, and the shockwave swept across dozens of li, directly engulfing Luo Yuheng, al-Asura, and the others.

The power of the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect spread in all directions together with its collapse.

At the sight, Sarun Agu's smile broadened.

The opportunity had come.

The pitch-black Dharma Aspect formed by Shenshu melted away within the Buddhist light, revealing his true body. The upper half of him had already become skeletal, burned into a red-hot skeleton.

Even the undying property of a half-step Martial God could not leave him completely unharmed.

Of course, apart from weakened stamina and a drop in aura, Shenshu had not suffered any fatal injury.

The Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts reacted at once. Everyone tacitly moved towards Yang Gong.

Yang Gong flicked his Confucian cap and stirred clear light to envelop the group.

"Retreat three thousand feet."

The laws follow commandments failed.

Sun Xuanji stamped his foot, and a teleportation formation rapidly spread out, trying to envelop everyone. But before it could fully expand, it collapsed at once, purified by the Buddhist light.

Where the Buddhist light of the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect shone, all spells would be purified.

Luo Yuheng's immunity to all magic also failed to take effect against power on the Beyond-rank level.

Samantabhadra Bodhisattva immediately broke from his meditative state. His Great Reincarnation Dharma Aspect turned with harsh cracking sounds, and the character "human", carved in Buddhist script, lit up. The Great Compassion and Mercy Dharma Aspect raised its eyes and "looked" towards the Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts.

Agaruka Bodhisattva charged towards al-Asura and the others with heavy thudding steps, like a starving wolf pouncing upon sheep.

Even faster than them was Vaidurya Bodhisattva. Taking advantage of the interval in which the Great Sun Tathagata Dharma Aspect suppressed the beyond-mortal experts, she silently emerged behind Arhat Du'e, a Demon-Sealing Nail pinched between her fingertips, and struck towards the back of his head.

As fellow members of the Buddhist sect, Arhat Du'e and al-Asura would not be purified by the Buddhist light. But just as brilliant seven-coloured halos rose behind their heads, the scenery around them lost all colour.

Their movements, thoughts, and even the Executioner Phala fell into a sluggish state.

Vaidurya Bodhisattva gave a light pat.

With a puff, the Demon-Sealing Nail viciously pierced into Arhat Du'e's crown, completing the initial stage of the seal.

Then Vaidurya Bodhisattva seized Du'e by the shoulder and vanished.

*Shit...* The Great Feng's beyond-mortal experts all changed expression.

The fortune on Du'e's body was of vital importance, and his identity even more so. His existence determined whether Mahayana Buddhism could continue in the Central Plains.

He was the link between the supreme Buddha Xu Qi’an and the followers of Mahayana Buddhism.

With only Xu Qi’an, who did not understand Buddhist doctrine, it would be difficult to maintain Mahayana Buddhism. And once Mahayana Buddhism weakened, the fortune would flow back to the Buddhist sect.

Even an old hand like Daoist Jinlian felt a deep sense of powerlessness rising in his heart.

Luo Yuheng's willow brows shot up, yet there was nothing she could do. One tree could not prop up a whole forest. She could not save Du'e.

The next moment, Vaidurya Bodhisattva in her flowing white robes appeared in an uninhabited area covered in dark red fleshly matter.

She had wisely chosen not to remain near the Buddha's "incarnation", because that was too close to Shenshu.

"Du'e, to merge with the Buddha is your honour!"

Vaidurya said regretfully:

"You should have stood with us, eternal and undying, becoming one of the Buddha's spokesmen."

Du'e glanced down beneath him. The dark red fleshly substance writhed like tentacles, already impatient to devour him.

"Not my path."

He put his palms together and calmly faced his fate.

Vaidurya said no more. Releasing her grip, she threw him down.

The dark red fleshly substance surged skyward, wrapping around Du'e and swallowing him whole.

"Leizhou can't be saved."

Sarun Agu shook his head, then frowned.

Did the Jianzheng truly have no trump card?

He swept a glance over the Gu clan chiefs and found all their faces ugly, except Heaven Gu Grandma, whose expression remained normal.

"What did you see?" the Great Warlock asked.

"Heaven's secrets must not be divulged."

Heaven Gu Grandma smiled.

Sarun Agu fell into thought.

*Grandma isn't anxious at all. What she saw was a future favourable to the Great Feng?* Chunyan's eyes lit up, and much of the anxiety in her heart subsided.

*The Great Feng still has another hidden move? What could it be...* The Gu clan chiefs all began to speculate.

Just then, the portion of fleshly matter that had swallowed Du'e suddenly twisted wildly, as though suffering indigestion.

Then, with a loud bang, the fleshly matter exploded, like a cannonball dropped into a swamp, sending mud flying everywhere.

This sudden change left everyone stunned. Focusing their gaze, they saw Du'e appear before the eyes of the beyond-mortal experts, completely unharmed.

Beside him stood one more person.

He wore an indigo robe, his black hair hanging loose.

His features were handsome, and he had a tall, well-proportioned figure.

Xu Qi’an, Silver Gong of the Great Feng.
