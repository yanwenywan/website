# 883. Return to the Capital

At the border between the Western Regions and Leizhou.

Xu Qi’an and Shenshu appeared abruptly. The two stood outside the border line, watching the dark red flesh-and-blood matter withdraw back into the Western Regions and merge into the earth.

At this point, the Buddha's aura vanished without a trace.

By now, the two of them had completely purged the power of the Great Sun Samsara and returned to normal, but both were stark naked.

"The Mahayana Buddhist faith has already been established, yet the Buddha can still use fortune to devour the Western Regions?"

As Xu Qi’an spoke, he took out two robes and tossed one to Shenshu.

Otherwise, if he wasn't careful, he might end up becoming sworn brothers with Shenshu, and then the Nine-Tailed Fox would have to call him Uncle Xu.

"It has to do with the Church of the Warlock God," Shenshu explained simply. He draped on the robe, then said after a moment of thought:

"I cultivate Buddhist Dharma. I can go in and try."

*How crude...* Xu Qi’an muttered inwardly, then shook his head.

"If we can use a puppet to scout the way, there's no need to risk ourselves."

After a moment's thought, he still couldn't bear to use Moyu, the river dragon hidden in the Earth Book fragment. Instead, he used spatial magic to seize a wild rabbit, crushed it to death, and implanted a corpse gu child into it.

The reason he chose Corpse Gu rather than controlling it with Heart Gu was that Heart Gu could only share certain vague senses, such as vision.

The child gu allowed a deeper level of control. The puppet was like a double.

That would let Xu Qi’an better sense the Buddha's current state.

The rabbit hopped into the Western Regions. After only a few steps, a mouth suddenly split open in the ground. Just as the rabbit was about to be swallowed, it made a nimble leap and sprang high into the air, avoiding the huge mouth beneath it.

But in the next moment, the airborne rabbit took the initiative and plunged headfirst into the mouth split open in the ground.

This... Xu Qi’an's expression turned grave.

Shenshu glanced sideways at him, waiting for his analysis.

"I didn't detect any restriction or control. It was just a simple leap," Xu Qi’an said.

But the reality was that the rabbit that had just leapt up had suddenly hurled itself into that mouth.

After a while, the two half-step Martial Gods came to the same realisation. Xu Qi’an said in a low voice:

"The Buddha has altered the rules.

"He changed the rule of leaping into falling. Yes, that's probably it."

For something to make even a half-step Martial God detect no restriction or manipulation at all, yet still walk willingly into the trap, the only explanation was a change in the rules themselves.

That was how the rules of heaven and earth worked.

So Xu Qi’an hadn't sensed anything abnormal.

"That isn't something the Buddha should be capable of," Shenshu commented.

The Confucian Sage had also been able to forcibly alter the rules, but that was due to the special nature of his system, and there would be backlash afterwards.

"Because in the Western Regions, the Buddha is no longer Beyond-rank. He has become heaven and earth itself!" Xu Qi’an let out a sigh.

The Jianzheng had been right. The true goal of the Beyond-ranks was to replace the Heavenly Dao and become the embodiment of the will of the world of Jiuzhou.

If there had still been some doubt in his heart before, now he completely believed the Jianzheng's words.

Shenshu thought for a moment, then stepped forward. Vast and terrifying power surged out, triggering upheaval in heaven and earth and throwing the elements into disorder.

But when those chaotic elements approached the Western Regions, they were all calmed by an even greater power. The Martial Artist domain Shenshu raised was blocked outside the Western Regions.

This further proved that a "severance" had appeared between the Western Regions and the world of Jiuzhou. They occupied the same space, yet no longer belonged to the same world.

"So this is the secret of the great calamity. The Buddha wants to devour Jiuzhou and evolve a brand new world?" Shenshu looked at Xu Qi’an.

"Not evolve. Replace," Xu Qi’an said gravely.

Shenshu gazed at the vast territory of the Western Regions before him. After a long silence, he said slowly:

"So that's how it is."

It was as though he had finally unravelled a question that had puzzled him for a long time.

"What does Master think?" Xu Qi’an took the chance to probe.

"A calamity for all living beings," Shenshu judged.

He waited for a while. Seeing that Shenshu had no intention of continuing, he asked:

"Master, I've already become a half-step Martial God, and I've discovered many strange patterns inside my body, like the spiritual essence of gods and demons."

Shenshu said:

"They possess the property of indestructibility. That is the capital with which a half-step Martial God dares contend with a Beyond-rank.

"I've studied them. My only conclusion is that they are incomplete."

Xu Qi’an frowned.

"Incomplete?"

He hadn't felt any incompleteness.

Shenshu thought for a moment, then analysed:

"To be more precise, it's like an array formation of which only a prototype has been inscribed. The details still await completion.

"Each 'formation pattern' is independent, but they lack connection with one another. They possess the property of indestructibility, yet they are not a single whole.

"Perhaps only by advancing to Martial God can this formation truly take shape."

*Every cell possessed the property of indestructibility, but each is independent...* Xu Qi’an's heart stirred.

"Is that why you were dismembered and sealed by the Buddha back then?"

Countless cells represented countless formation patterns, but because they were independent, they could be separated.

Shenshu nodded.

Xu Qi’an eagerly continued the discussion.

"Then do you know how to advance to Martial God?"

"I do."

Shenshu's reply took Xu Qi’an by surprise. He said:

"Complete the 'formation' on your body, and you'll most likely be a Martial God."

*Isn't that nonsense? I know that too. What I'm asking for is the specific method...* Xu Qi’an said irritably:

"How do I complete the formation?"

Shenshu looked at him and said expressionlessly:

"Just now, the Buddha called you Gatekeeper."

Xu Qi’an explained:

"During this voyage overseas, I met the Jianzheng. He told me that the Gatekeeper can only be born from the Martial Artist system."

Shenshu studied him.

"The Jianzheng's purpose in supporting you was to cultivate you into the Gatekeeper."

Xu Qi’an nodded.

Shenshu said:

"I am also a half-step Martial God, yet the Jianzheng didn't support me. He chose you instead.

"We can infer the truth from the Jianzheng's plans in the past. You need to think carefully about two questions. First, why did he choose to support you? Second, what did he leave in you?"

*Left something in reserve?* Xu Qi’an instinctively examined Shenshu.

The latter frowned.

"I understand," Xu Qi’an said.

The answer was obvious. It was fortune.

The reason he had become one of the Jianzheng's pieces was because he was Xu Pingfeng's son, and Xu Pingfeng had stolen the national fortune of the Great Feng.

Up to now, although the Jianzheng had given him a great deal of help, it had all been to assist his advancement and raise his strength, and all of it had still revolved around fortune.

Shenshu gave the final verdict:

"You only need to guard the fortune well. Guard the fortune, then go and explore how to advance to Martial God."

At that moment, a flash of azure light appeared, and Sun Xuanji arrived with a group of beyond-mortals.

Seeing that Xu Qi’an and Shenshu had not rashly begun a great battle, Yang Gong, Daoist Jinlian and the others let out sighs of relief.

Shenshu said flatly:

"The Buddha won't continue devouring Leizhou for the time being. I will remain here to guard the border. Do as you please."

Xu Qi’an had Sun Xuanji leave Shenshu several transmission jade talismans and a few Confucian pages of laws following commandments. These were to deal with the spells of the Buddha's various Dharma Aspects. Then he said:

"If the Buddha makes a comeback, contact me immediately."

It would take time for the Buddha to devour Leizhou, while for him to travel from the capital to Leizhou would take only a very short time.

So he wasn't afraid that once he returned to the capital, the Buddha would seize the chance to annex Leizhou.

Then he said to the group:

"Let's go back to the capital first. Anything else can be discussed later."

The Nine-Tailed Fox and al-Asura glanced towards the Western Regions, unwilling to accept it, but since neither Shenshu nor Xu Qi’an had any intention of venturing deeper into the Western Regions, they could only give up.

Xu Qi’an raised the big eyeball on his wrist and left with the group of beyond-mortals.

...

At this moment, Diao Chan was still on the way...

No, at this moment, Lady Flying Swallow was still waiting between sea and sky for Silver Gong Xu.

...

The sky in the east was beginning to pale.

The Capital, in the Imperial Study.

After a sleepless night, Wang Zhenwen was visibly exhausted. His eye bags were swollen and his eyes were bloodshot.

The anxiety in Huaiqing's heart was on the verge of bursting. In a gentle voice, she said:

"Lord Wang, you should go and rest first."

Wang Zhenwen shook his head.

"I'd only toss and turn and find no sleep. Better not to sleep at all.

"The fact that no news has arrived yet is the best possible news."

If Leizhou couldn't be defended, then matters would enter their worst phase. Only then would true catastrophe descend.

Huaiqing didn't urge him any further. She held the Earth Book fragment and remained silent in thought.

Wei Yuan and Zhao Shou were comparatively calm. The former had been through too many tempests. Even with a blade at his neck, his emotions would hardly fluctuate.

The latter had cultivated his composure superbly. Even if his heart was overflowing with anxiety, none of it showed on the surface.

After thinking for a while, Zhao Shou said:

"If Leizhou is lost, Your Majesty's first priority must be to stabilise the court and the people's hearts. Then Silver Gong Xu must be summoned back at once to discuss how to hunt Agaruka Bodhisattva and help him advance to half-step Martial God.

"As long as Xu Ningyan advances to half-step Martial God, every difficulty can be solved with ease."

Huaiqing looked at Wei Yuan.

Wei Yuan shook his head and sighed.

"Easier said than done. The Buddhists won't give us that chance. And if they do, then it is we who should be wary instead."

Wang Zhenwen agreed with his old political enemy's view.

"At this stage, rather than discussing how to help Xu Ningyan advance to half-step Martial God, we'd be better off probing the attitude of the Church of the Warlock God and forming an alliance with them. It will still take two or three months for the Warlock God to break the seal."

Although the Church of the Warlock God had helped the Buddha, so long as the two sides were in competition, an alliance could still be attempted.

Zhao Shou sneered.

"The Church of the Warlock God is clearly waiting on the mountain to watch the tigers fight, then profit as the fisherman."

Wang Zhenwen retorted sharply:

"So long as we make the Church of the Warlock God believe we don't have the strength to end in mutual destruction with Buddhism, they will naturally change their attitude."

"How humiliating," Zhao Shou shook his head. "And this would amount to handing our weakness to the Church of the Warlock God and letting them butcher us as they please. It would be another peace negotiation."

By "peace negotiation" he meant the cession talks initiated by the Yunzhou rebel army after the Jianzheng had been sealed.

It wasn't hard to imagine that the Church of the Warlock God would certainly put forward corresponding demands, annexing Great Feng territory without bloodshed, and they would be even more excessive than the Yunzhou rebels.

Wei Yuan commented:

"Drinking poison to quench thirst!"

Huaiqing, behind the great desk coverd with yellow silk, waved a hand.

"The situation is not yet settled. It's too early to discuss this."

She could only use such words to calm the argument, but she also knew that if Leizhou really were swallowed by the Buddha, arguments like this would break out again, and by then the full court of civil and military officials would be gathered in Jinluan hall arguing without end.

Those advocating surrender, or seeking refuge with the Church of the Warlock God, would probably become the mainstream.

Dying for the nation required conviction. One couldn't expect every official to possess such resolve.

*And by then, rumours would probably already be spreading among the common folk that "a woman upon the throne brings disaster to the state"...* Thinking of this, Huaiqing wearily pinched the bridge of her nose.

Although by relying on her own methods, as well as the help of Wei Yuan, Xu Qi’an and the others, she had stabilised her throne, there were still criticisms among lower-ranking officials, among the common people, and even among Confucian scholars.

When the country was peaceful and prosperous, these criticisms were nothing more than harmless grumbling.

But once the country fell into turmoil, the four words "woman upon the throne" would be magnified and become the target onto which blame was cast.

She had only just brought the country into good order, allowing the people, who had suffered natural disasters and war, to recuperate, and yet before one wave had subsided another had risen.

At times like this, she would remember that she was a woman, and think that she needed someone to rely on.

And as the ruler of a nation, the only man she could regard as support, the only man she wanted to rely on, was Xu Qi’an.

At present, that support was still drifting overseas, out of contact.

Yet it was precisely because he still couldn't be contacted that Huaiqing continued to hold hope for him.

Perhaps he would return having advanced to half-step Martial God. That man had never disappointed her.

Suddenly, Huaiqing sensed something and looked up.

Wei Yuan and Zhao Shou reacted even earlier than she did.

In the spacious Imperial Study, a large group of people appeared without warning.

The man at the front had handsome features and wore an indigo robe, just as before. It was none other than Xu Qi’an, from whom they had been parted for several months.

Behind him stood Luo Yuheng, al-Asura, the Nine-Tailed Fox, Daoist Jinlian and other beyond-mortal experts.

Wei Yuan, Wang Zhenwen, Zhao Shou, and Huaiqing all rose to their feet at the same time.

*He's returned? And brought back the beyond-mortal experts from Leizhou as well?*

Huaiqing seemed to think of something. Then she heard the pounding of her own heartbeat. She tried her utmost to keep her expression calm, but a faint tremor still entered her voice:

"The Buddha has retreated?"

At those words, Wang Zhenwen, Wei Yuan, and Zhao Shou all fixed their gaze on Xu Qi’an.

Xu Qi’an gave a grunt of assent.

Huaiqing pressed her lips together. With a trace of hope, a trace of caution, she probed:

"You've advanced to half-step Martial God?"

The way she hardly dared breathe, the mix of hope and caution in her manner, made her look slightly pitiful, like a little girl asking whether her father had brought back her favourite doll.

Wang Zhenwen unconsciously clenched his fists, and his sleeves trembled faintly.

Wei Yuan looked comparatively calm, but never before had he looked at someone with such concentration.

Zhao Shou couldn't help holding his breath.
