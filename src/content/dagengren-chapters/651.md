# 651. Half-Step Martial God (double chapter)

"Elder Yeji has fallen unconscious again."

At dawn, Hongying stood atop the southern cliff of the valley, his amber vertical pupils gazing down at the distant mountains.

He possessed a strong dark-vision ability. Even in moonless night, he could spot targets from the heights amidst the vast forests below.

Elder Yeji had encountered al-Asura at the Nanfa Temple. It could not be ruled out that the latter might follow the clues and come looking for them. Remaining vigilant was an essential principle.

The white ape with the mouth of the thunder god stood under the tree, its pure azure eyes glancing at him, and said,

"Your heart tells me..."

"Stop, stop, stop!"

Hongying hurriedly interrupted, putting on an affable smile, "Peering into the thoughts of others is rather rude."

He forcibly reined in his thoughts, not allowing himself to curse aloud in his heart.

The white ape continued slowly,

"You are becoming more and more like a bureaucrat from the human race, adept at pleasing everyone so as not to offend anyone. But have you forgotten you are a proud member of the Scarlet Bird clan, a king of the skies?"

Hongying followed suit readily, "You're right, this is my fault, I shall certainly correct it."

The white ape glanced at him, "Yet your heart tells me: human bureaucracy allows one to amass yao connections swiftly and build relationships, from which one can benefit. Even if you gain nothing, there is no harm either. Foolish monkeys like me can only be kings in their mountains, so uncouth!"

Hongying’s mouth twitched violently.

He disliked Protector Yuan precisely because this damned monkey could see through people's hearts.

Fortunately, Hongying was not thin-skinned either. With his wealth of experience, he changed the topic with a calm expression,

"Protector Qingmu said that Elder Yeji has only two days to live.

"I wonder who the help that the Princess mentioned is."

The white ape pondered for a moment, then replied,

"Twenty years ago, in the battle at Shanhai Pass, those who allied with our Wanyao Nation were the Church of the Warlock God, northern Yao clans, barbarians, and the shaman clans. Though the northern Yao clans are a different branch from us, they are still Yao, making them the most likely.

"The experts from the Church of the Warlock God and the shaman clans are also possible. Hmm, since the Princess said that person could save Elder Yeji, an expert from the Church of the Warlock God is most likely. The Warlock’s blood spirit art might be able to dispel the power of the Executioner phala."

The relationship between Elder Yeji and Xu Qi’an, as well as the schemes of the Nine-tailed Fox, were not matters they as Protectors were qualified to know.

They barely knew about Silver Gong Xu from the Great Feng, as the Southern Marches and the Great Feng were far apart and rarely interacted, making their information quite scarce.

Suddenly, Hongying’s voice deepened, "Someone is approaching!"

He stared hard at the night sky in the distance.

A few seconds later, he suddenly exclaimed, "Elder Baiji?"

The white ape, whose aura had been rapidly rising, seemed to choke all of a sudden, and looked at him in confusion.

Hongying explained, "Elder Baiji has brought a man back."

"A man?"

"Yes, he doesn’t seem to be a Warlock, but rather a martial artist..." Hongying peered into the distance.

"Martial artist?!" The white ape grew even more puzzled.

Hongying didn’t answer again, because that person was travelling at great speed on the wind, less than a thousand feet from the mountain where they stood. At this distance, white ape could already see clearly.

*Plop.* Xu Qi’an landed on the mountaintop, glanced at the two Yao ahead, and fell silent.

"Protector Hongying, Protector Yuan!"

Baiji lay on Xu Qi’an’s head, happily waving her forepaws and calling in a soft, childish voice.

"Elder Baiji, why are you here?" Protector Hongying asked in surprise.

"I was ordered by Mistress to return to the Southern Marches to help Sister Yeji," Baiji replied sweetly.

"This is...?"

Hongying and the white ape simultaneously looked at Xu Qi’an. Anyone with a bit of sense would know that the reinforcement the Princess spoke of could not possibly be Elder Baiji.

After all, she was only a fox cub.

Xu Qi’an stood with his hands behind his back, his expression calm, neither indifferent nor enthusiastic, conveying an air of serene detachment to display the bearing of an expert.

Baiji introduced him in a delicate voice, "This is Silver Gong Xu, Silver Gong of the Great Feng. Have you ever heard of him?"

Hongying and the white ape exchanged glances. The former suddenly realised, "So you are the influential figure who rose to fame during the year of official evaluation, renowned for your uncanny talent for solving cases?"

The white ape said,

"The Silver Gong Xu, that solved mysterious cases even whilst imprisoned, and single-handedly held off tens of thousands of rebels on Yunzhou?"

... Xu Qi’an thought to himself, *Are you two just now catching up with the news? Are you the villagers who just got the internet?*

Baiji whispered in his ear,

"The two Protectors are only responsible for matters in the Southern Marches. They never leave the Hundred Thousand Mountains and pay little attention to anything in the Great Feng."

At this point, the white ape with the thunder god's mouth frowned,

"Silver Gong Xu solving strange cases and holding off rebels in Yunzhou was just news from last year, not old history. By the way, what does ‘villagers who just got the internet’ mean?"

Xu Qi’an was taken aback, "You can read my thoughts?"

The white ape nodded, "Understanding the hearts of others is my clan's innate divine ability. Also, when I was young, I served as a Yao slave in Liangchan Temple, where I secretly learnt the Buddhist Heart-reading technique."

*The Buddhist Heart-reading technique, plus the talent of discerning people’s hearts?* Xu Qi’an studied the white ape, quietly suppressing his thoughts.

The matter of Silver Gong Xu being an lsp must absolutely be kept secret.

With his third rank spirit, he could still keep others from prying into his thoughts.

"Where’s Sister Yeji?" the little white fox asked.

Hongying looked worried, "Elder Yeji scouted Nanfa Temple the other night and was wounded by the young son of the Asura King, al-Asura. That al-Asura has attained the Executioner phala, his power is extremely domineering and cannot be removed. Elder Yeji only has a day left to live.

"Her Highness said an expert would be coming soon to help..."

After speaking, he glanced at Xu Qi’an, then said respectfully, "Could that be Silver Gong Xu?"

The white ape beside him said blandly,

"Hongying’s heart tells me: could it really be this lad? At most he’s a fourth rank, never mind saving Elder Yeji, he wouldn’t even be enough to get stuck in al-Asura’s teeth."

Hongying’s expression changed slightly. He forced a smile, polite yet awkward,

"Protector Yuan is good at everything, except for being far too blunt from spending too many years in Buddhist temples."

*A bird demon very adept at socialising; a monkey who could see into the minds of others but is too straightforward...* Xu Qi’an mentally labelled the two Protectors.

"I am an old acquaintance of Elder Yeji. Take me to her. By the way, my follower is still behind, Protector Hongying, please fetch him for me. His name is Miao Youfang."

With Baiji vouching for him, the two Protectors believed Xu Qi’an. The white ape led him into the valley, while Hongying transformed into a giant scarlet bird and soared away.

The two Protectors believed that the helper the Princess mentioned was connected to this Silver Gong from the Great Feng, perhaps someone behind him.

He was just the vanguard, sent to pave the way.

One man and one Yao floated lightly down into the valley. The white ape took him into a cave and, after walking through a not-very-deep passage, they arrived at the entrance of a stone chamber.

Xu Qi’an scanned the furnishings of the stone chamber and was momentarily dazed. The arrangement here was identical to the bedchamber in the Reflecting Plum Pavillion of the Jiaofangsi.

For an instant, he felt as if he had returned to the Jiaofangsi in the capital.

Those were his most comfortable, happiest days.

*So a part of my time was left here with Fuxiang...*

"Sister Yeji!"

Baiji leapt from Xu Qi’an’s head, darted to the bedside on all fours, and with a strong jump, her little belly, unsurprisingly, bumped into the bed edge. With a few more scrambles from her hindlegs, she finally made it onto the bed.

She seemed to sense the danger, and did not recklessly touch the beauty lying on the bed.

Xu Qi’an’s gaze followed her before shifting to the old man in green at the bedside. He was holding a cane entwined with vines, resting it on the forehead of the young woman on the bed, where emerald light flowed like water.

When the green-haired, green-browed, green-bearded elder saw outsiders had come in, he withdrew his staff and looked over gently.

The white ape introduced him,

"This is the Nightwatcher from the Great Feng, Silver Gong Xu."

Then he introduced Protector Qingmu,

"Protector Qingmu is the oldest among us Yao, has lived for thousands of years, and is said to have watched the previous sovereign grow up. Even our present princess has to call him grandfather."

*His cultivation is not high, but his seniority is terrifying, and this is only an avatar, formed from wood spirit...* Xu Qi’an judged silently and saluted,

"Greetings, Protector Qingmu."

Protector Qingmu waved his hands repeatedly, looking nervous and apprehensive,

"We would not dare, we would not dare. Sir is a beyond-mortal warrior, you may simply call this old one Qingmu."

*A beyond-mortal warrior? So he is the helper the princess had sought, and not some pawn sent to probe for others hiding in the shadows...* White Ape’s azure eyes widened instantly in disbelief as he looked at Xu Qi’an.

If the reports were right, Xu Qi’an did indeed rise to fame during the Year of the Official Evaluation, and the information described him as a brilliant detective, but it never said he was a prodigy at cultivation.

No matter how much of a prodigy he was, it still could not explain how he had ascended from a nobody to beyond-mortal in just over a year.

A suspicion arose in White Ape’s mind:

Maybe this man was not actually Silver Gong Xu, but someone impersonating him.

*With my current mastery of Qi, ordinary people would never be able to discern my true level. These Yao clans really are full of talents...* Xu Qi’an nodded slightly, neither confirming nor denying.

"This old one is just especially sensitive to life force. Your vitality is like an ocean, only someone at the beyond-mortal level could possess such overwhelming life," Protector Qingmu said, unassuming and humble.

Xu Qi’an nodded and did not waste words: "Let me take a look at her."

Protector Qingmu immediately stepped back to make room.

Xu Qi’an sat by the bedside, examining the unconscious beauty with genuine admiration.

Compared to the refined grace of the beauty in Reflecting Plum Pavillion, Fuxiang here looked like an entirely different person. Her cheeks curved down to a pointed chin, outlining a seductive, delicate face.

Her lips were small, but lush and full, naturally alluring.

A beautifully shaped nose, long straight brows, thick lashes, and a hint of red at the corner of her eyes.

There was no one in Xu Qi'an’s pond more beguiling than her.

*A Yao woman is truly a Yao woman...*

Xu Qi'an chuckled inwardly and let his gaze fall, glancing over the chest propping up the thin blanket before scooping up Fuxiang’s wrist.

*Pop~*

A golden ripple surged out defensively, striking Xu Qi'an in the chest like a wave crashing against a reef, but failing to shake him at all.

Witnessing this, Protector Yuan was now utterly convinced that this "Silver Gong Xu" was indeed third rank.

The strength of the “Executioner of Evil” phala was not something a fourth rank could withstand.

"What do you think?"

Protector Qingmu asked.

But before Xu Qi'an could answer, Protector White Ape said,

"His heart tells me: he is very satisfied with this body and wants to consummate the marriage tonight."

After this, White Ape looked utterly shocked, standing with Protector Qingmu as both of them warily eyed Xu Qi'an.

*Fucking...* Xu Qi’an quickly gathered his thoughts and coughed,

"I can remove the power of the Executioner Phala from her body, but you two must step back."

Qingmu and Protector White Apes silently watched him, faces plainly saying “don’t even think about it”.

*Whatever...* Xu Qi’an summoned the Stupa tower, the palm-sized dark gold tower hovering above the bed.

"The Stupa Tower?!"

Protector Qingmu’s voice suddenly went shrill.

White Ape didn’t recognise the artefact, but could sense the Buddhist energy within.

Their eyes were ever more guarded, already doubting if he was truly the helper mentioned by the lord.

Protector Qingmu silently gripped the vine staff in his hand.

White Ape’s cheeks sprouted white fur.

The Yao women in the cave all braced themselves as if facing a great enemy.

Baiji stood by the bedside, raised her paw and waved it energetically, calling out sweetly,

"Don’t be afraid, the Stupa tower belongs to our Yao, no, it’s our artefact!"

The expressions of the Yao in the cave relaxed a little, their confusion and curiosity held in check, and they did not ask further.

By this time, Xu Qi’an was already communicating with the tower spirit, asking it to use the power of the Medicine Buddha Dharma Aspect to help expel the Executioner Phala.

The miniature Stupa tower spun slowly, shedding gentle golden light.

Bathed in this golden glow, Yeji seemed holy and alluring all at once, creating an oddly captivating charm.

"The Medicine Buddha Dharma Aspect..."

Protector Qingmu murmured softly, unsurprised. As a long-lived tree spirit, he had a deep understanding of the Stupa tower.

Yeji’s complexion became visibly rosier, her breathing steadier, and the tormenting power of the Executioner Phala within her melted away like spring snow.

After all, she had not suffered a direct blow from al-Asura, just aftershocks at most. Given the Stupa tower’s level, driving it out was not difficult.

"It’s done."

Xu Qi’an stowed the Stupa tower away.

Protector White Ape immediately looked at Protector Qingmu, who nodded slightly in confirmation.

All doubts vanquished, the two were now convinced, this beyond-mortal had healed Elder Yeji, with Elder Baiji vouching for him, so he was indeed the helper the Princess had mentioned.

White Ape’s clear azure eyes observed Xu Qi’an for a while, but "heard" nothing from his heart, and he looked a bit disappointed.

"Mmm..."

At this moment, Yeji moaned softly, her brows knitting, lashes fluttering, then she opened her eyes.

Her first sight was a blurry figure, and as her vision cleared, she realised it was a man.

Remembering what Her Highness said yesterday, alarm and anxiety welled up inside her.

"Awake?"

The figure smiled.

In that instant, Yeji felt as if struck by lightning, her body stiffening as she gazed at the man by the bed, her eyes gleaming with misty tears.

"Xu Lang..."

She murmured.

Her tone was dreamy, as if speaking of someone she had longed for day and night, now appearing before her so easily.

She even doubted if this was only a dream.

"Is it really you?"

Having decided it was not a dream, Yeji sat up excitedly and grabbed Xu Qi’an’s hand.

Glowing with joy, she kept repeating, "Xu Lang, Xu Lang..."

"Of course it’s me, nothing’s changed, if you don’t believe me, you can measure."

Xu Qi’an replied in the teasing tone he used to, matching his old persona.

When they used to frolic in the bedroom at Reflecting Plum Pavillion, they would often trade raunchy jokes.

Yeji’s pale cheeks turned red, and she spat softly at him.

Propping up her still-weak body, she nestled against Xu Qi’an, her voice filled with the joy of reunion and a host of questions,

"Xu Lang, how are you here? How did you find this place?"

In Yeji’s eyes, Xu Qi’an was still only a fifth rank Transforming Force expert, caught up in a huge conspiracy with a bleak future.

After her "death", she had returned to Her Highness, their reunion seemingly impossible, separated by mountains and rivers.

Protector Yuan tried to speak, his mind reeling.

Was this Silver Gong Xu from the Great Feng really Elder Yeji’s man?!

Elder Yeji, who served the Princess of the Myriad Yao, had actually found a human man as her companion?

If this got out, countless male Yao would be furious.

He knew it was true, because Elder Yeji’s heart told him: she was aroused!

*I understand, in the southern marches you really don't have any internet connection...* Xu Qi’an said,

"You don’t know anything that happened in the Great Feng?"

"Every Yao in the Wanyao Kingdom is assigned their own region. After returning to Her Highness, I was sent to govern the Yao clans of the Southern Marches, to monitor all the movements of the Southern Country, and to search for the sealed remnants of Shenshu.

"I am now a third rank beyond-mortal, undying body."

“Xu Lang is the reinforcement Mistress invited? Did you also heal me?”

Just as Yeji had said.

Xu Qi’an explained.

“How fearsome is someone Beyond-rank? Even the Nine-tailed Celestial Fox, who was a half-step Martial God, lost to the Buddha.”

“The sovereign was not a half-step Martial God.”
